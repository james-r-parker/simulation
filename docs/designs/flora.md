# Flora

**Status:** In progress - Phases 1 to 4 code built, including the flora look. E69 reference recorded; E70 to E74, detector power, survival, the 4K clip and standalone performance measurements remain owed.
**Validated:** 5158921 (2026-11-01)
**Priority:** 2 - flora phases in order, ahead of the paused natural-selection balancing work (user, 2026-10-31)
**Depends on:** [natural-selection.md](natural-selection.md) (Phase 4 balancing paused; adopted split rules merged in PR #223), [brain-capacity-and-memory.md](brain-capacity-and-memory.md)

Today the world has one plant food: a 100 energy item with a 10% variant at 140, spawned by 16 regrowing patches (80% of spawns) and a background (20%), scaled by a season factor that changes only the amount, plus carcasses. Every food item looks the same, tastes the same and is there all year. This doc replaces it with plant species whose traits evolve through pollination, each fruiting in its own window of the year, some of them toxic, and gives the blob one new fact about each food it sees: its hue.

The scenes it serves ([principles.md](../principles.md)): **Season changes the map** (the food map differs by season), **Leave a dying patch** (a species that stops fruiting empties a patch), **Scavenge** (decomposers recycle the dead), **Other blobs are the climate** (plants co-evolve with the blobs that eat and carry them), **Signals cost, and they can be false** (a warning colour, a mimic), and **Something unnamed**. It adds no fitness term: every new fact reaches selection only through the world, as [principles.md](../principles.md) "Measure scenes, reward them almost never" requires. A plant that is eaten loses energy, a blob that eats a toxic plant gets sick, and a flower that is visited seeds more flowers.

Scope: the plant model in `js/sim/`, the hue sense in the brain layout, the sickness state, the pollination and plant evolution rules, the detectors, and the look of all of it in `js/app/renderer/`. Out of scope: a plant that moves, grows between ticks as an individual, or has a brain; a second nutrient; a plant defence other than toxicity; carcass changes beyond the decomposer.

## The species

A species is an archetype: a row of the `flora` config table. Every plant item and every patch carries the traits of its archetype.

| Trait           | Meaning                                                                                                                           |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `share`         | Weight in the initial patch mix (the patches are assigned by largest remainder over `foodPatchCount`)                             |
| `energy`        | Item energy as a share of `foodEnergy`; `energySpread` is the +- share around it                                                  |
| `radius`        | Item radius as a share of `foodRadius`                                                                                            |
| `yield`         | Relative item rate of a patch of this species (a patch's spawn weight is stock x `yield` x fruiting)                              |
| `regrow`        | Multiplier on the patch's regrowth rate                                                                                           |
| `life`          | Item lifetime in ticks (replaces `foodMaxAge`)                                                                                    |
| `start`, `span` | The fruiting window: year phase where it opens and its length as a share of the year (1 is all year)                              |
| `hue`           | The hue coordinate (below)                                                                                                        |
| `toxicity`      | 0 to 1; 0 is edible                                                                                                               |
| `pollenChance`  | The chance an item eaten loads pollen (0 for a species that is not pollinated)                                                    |
| `range`         | How far a patch's `hue`, `energy`, `regrow`, `life` and `start` may drift from the row, as a share (hue: x 1 on the [-1, 1] axis) |

Each row has `energySpread` 0.2, a share of `foodEnergy`.

- **Grass** fruits all year and fast, and is the background food: the `foodBackgroundShare` of spawns are grass items. Background spawns are grass only, so grass is the one species that cannot go extinct; every other species exists only through its patches and the seed bank, and no other path recreates it.
- **Berry** fruits from early summer to early autumn, and its items last about a tenth of a year.
- **Nut** fruits in autumn and its items last 60000 ticks, into the next winter and past the first of spring.
- **Flower** gives small items (nectar) from spring to midsummer and is the main pollen host.
- **Fungus** has no patches. Its items spawn on mycelium marks (the next subsection), which `reap` leaves where agents died, from late autumn through winter (year phase 0.62 to 0.97). `spawnFloraItem` takes the mark branch with probability `fertileZoneSpawnChance` x the fungus fruiting weight when a mark is alive; a draw that does not take it is made again as a patch or background draw, so the total item rate is unchanged and a mark feeds blobs in winter only through fungus.
- **Toxic cap** is a patch species with a warning hue and a toxicity of 0.8; its patches exist from T14.

The `flora` table holds exactly these six rows, validated by `checkFlora`, which lives in the new `js/sim/config-flora.js` and is called from `../../js/sim/config-validate.js` for a `flora` rule type, the way `checkSpecialisations` is called for `specialisations`. Every row has every field as a finite number in range, with no exemption. `range` is below 1; inherited energy/life bounds must fit positive Float32, and `energySpread` cannot exceed the smallest stored inherited energy or the row energy, so no plant draw is negative. Stored regrowth/yield remain finite and span stays positive in Float32: `share`, `yield`, `regrow`, `toxicity` and `pollenChance` at least 0 (`toxicity` and `pollenChance` at most 1); a row with `share` above 0 needs `yield` and `regrow` above 0; `start` in [0, 1); `span` in (0, 1]; `hue` in [-1, 1]; `fungus` has `share` 0, `yield` 0, `regrow` 0 and `range` 0. The largest item radius (`radius` x `foodRadius`) replaces `highFoodRadius` in the `hashCellSize` check of `../../js/sim/config-validate.js`. `highFoodChance`, `highFoodEnergy`, `highFoodEnergyVariance`, `highFoodRadius`, `foodEnergyVariance` and `foodMaxAge` are deleted; `foodEnergy` and `foodRadius` stay as the reference scales (the temperature gain in `tryEat` and `fitness-v5.js` read `foodEnergy`). Reports written before keep their old keys and stay comparable through `npm run eval:compare -- --allow-config-diff <keys>`.

### Fruiting

`fruitWeight(phase, start, span)` in `js/sim/fruiting.js` is 1 for `span` 1; else, with `u = ((phase - start) mod 1) / span`, it is `sin(pi u)^2` for `u` in [0, 1] and 0 outside, so a species ramps in, peaks mid-window and ramps out. Once per tick `refreshFruiting(world)` writes `patchWeight[i] = patchStock[i] x patchYield[i] x fruitWeight(world.season[1], patchStart[i], patchSpan[i])`. `pickPatch` draws one float and picks from `patchWeight` instead of the stock, in ascending index as today; with a total weight of 0 it returns -1 and `spawnFloraItem` makes the background draw. Items of `life` 60000 (nut) stay alive across a season, so `foodCount` can reach `maxFood`; `spawnFood` then stops spawning as it does today, and the screens read `foodCount` at the checkpoints and lower the nut `yield` if it saturates. The total item rate is unchanged (`areaExpected`); the season moves where the items are and what they are worth.

An item carries its own `foodSpecies`, `foodHue`, `foodTox`, `foodLife` and `foodPatch` (the patch it came from, -1 for background, zone and carcass items) and `foodPatchSerial` (the source generation, 0 without a patch), copied from the patch at spawn, so a patch that later changes does not change items already lying there. `ageFood` removes a plant item older than its `foodLife`. A carcass keeps `carcassTicks`; its `foodHue` is `CARCASS_HUE` (0).

## The hue sense

Each food a blob sees has a hue `h` in [-1, 1], a linear coordinate, not an angle: -1 is green, then yellow, red at about 0.33 and magenta at 1 (the renderer maps `t = (h + 1) / 2` to an HSL hue of `120 - 180 t` degrees). One brain input per food slot carries it and stays in [-1, 1]; a plant hue never wraps.

- **Sensing.** `writeRay` in `../../js/sim/ray-march.js` writes `foodHue[food]` for a food hit into a derived array `rayHue` (`maxAgents x rayCount` floats, outside `worldHash`, zero for every other hit). `poolRays` writes the hue of each of the two strongest food hits into two new pooled values, `FOOD_HUE_VALUES` 2, placed as the [Sensory schema](brain-capacity-and-memory.md) states. An absent hit leaves strength 0 and hue 0. Hue 0 is also a real hue (`CARCASS_HUE`), so the brain tells an absent hit from a hue-0 hit only by the strength input.
- **Layout.** The layout is the flora step of the [Sensory schema](brain-capacity-and-memory.md) (83 inputs); `inputOffsets` and every `wSkip` row follow it. The skip block reads the hue values like any pooled value; `applyInstinct` in `../../js/evo/instinct.js` adds nothing to them. The inactive-input mask (`createInputActivity` in `../../js/sim/input-activity.js`), the instinct offsets, the crossover of the skip block (`../../js/evo/crossover.js`) and `eval.input_active_share` (`../../js/evo/metrics-weights.js`) all read the shifted offsets; T10 tests each.
- **Genome.** The genome sizes are the flora row of the [Sensory schema](brain-capacity-and-memory.md), and `ARCHIVE_VERSION` in `../../js/evo/archive-codec.js` goes from 4 to 5 (an archive or champion of another length or version is refused, naming the field).

## Toxicity and sickness

A toxic item (`foodTox` above 0) makes a dose. Per-agent `dose` (Float32, zero at birth) is the sickness state; it is not a brain input (the body block already reports the slower velocity, the lower energy and the stalled eating).

| Rule         | Behaviour                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Eating       | `ingestToxin` in `js/sim/toxin.js`, called by `tryEat` for a plant item: the energy gained is `value x share x (1 - tox x toxinCost)`; a negative result is removed as a `LOSS_CAUSE.COST`. `dose += tox x toxinDose`                           |
| Sickness     | `isSick`: `dose >= sickDose`. A sick agent's thrust in `actAll` is multiplied by `sickThrust`, `canEatNow` is false, and `stepToxin` removes `sickDrain x dose` energy per tick (`LOSS_CAUSE.COST`)                                             |
| Recovery     | `stepToxin` lowers `dose` by `doseDecay` per tick down to 0                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Death        | `deathCause` in `../../js/sim/population.js` returns the new `DEATH_CAUSE.POISONED` (4) when `dose >= lethalDose`, checked after old age and before the energy test. The body leaves a carcass, a fertile zone and a mycelium mark as any death |
| Learnability | The warning is the hue; nothing pays avoiding it                                                                                                                                                                                                |

Defaults: `toxinDose` 0.45, `toxinCost` 1.2, `sickDose` 0.3, `sickThrust` 0.4, `sickDrain` 0.02, `doseDecay` 0.0008, `lethalDose` 1.0. One bite of a toxic cap (`tox` 0.8) gives a dose of 0.36 and costs 0.96 of the item's energy. Sickness blocks another meal until the dose is below 0.3, so the next accepted cap meal remains below 0.66, never the default lethal threshold 1.0. Direct poison death is unreachable from the starting caps under these defaults, including the E71 lethal thresholds 0.8 to 1.4; sickness can still cause other losses. This is an unresolved E71 specification/power question, also recorded in the world-balance H4 handoff, not a measured scene or an adopted tuning value.

## Pollination and plant evolution

A species with `pollenChance` above 0 is pollinated. Patches become a population that grows, dies, sprouts from a seed bank and evolves.

- **Pollen.** Eating a plant item of a pollinated species whose `foodPatch` is a patch `p` loads pollen with probability `pollenChance`: per-agent `pollenPatch` (`p + 1`, 0 for none), `pollenSerial` (the patch's `patchSerial`) and `pollenTicks` (`pollenTicks`, counted down each tick by `agePollen`). A blob carries one pollen at a time; a new load replaces it. Pollen is void while its source is dead/banked or when `patchSerial[p]` no longer matches. An eaten item loads pollen only if its copied `foodPatchSerial` still identifies that living source, including same-species slot reuse.
- **Visit.** Eating a plant item of patch `q` of the same species while carrying valid pollen from a patch `p` different from `q`, when `q` has not seeded in the last `seedCooldownTicks` and a slot is free (`patchAlive` 0) or becomes free, `seedPatch` in `js/sim/pollination.js` creates a patch there and clears the pollen. The pollen is cleared and `q` is stamped even when no slot is free or local placement fails. A cooldown refusal retains pollen and does not stamp the patch. `SeedingRefused` logs the first refusal reason per agent/tick without suppressing later decisions or draws.
- **Seedling.** Its first centre candidate is at distance `foodPatchRadius x seedRadiusScale` from `q` at a `flora` angle; later bounded retries sample the local disc. Every candidate consumes an angle and radius draw, is clamped within the visited basin and world with patch-radius inset, and must be clear of obstacles at that radius and remain within the local reach. After `placementTries` failures it refuses; there is no global fallback, its stock `seedStock` x `foodPatchCapacity`, its `patchSerial` incremented. The fields it inherits (`yield`, `span`, `toxicity`, `pollenChance`) come from the row of the parents' shared species. Each evolving trait `v` (`energy`, `regrow`, `life`, `hue`, `start`) is one formula: `v = clamp(mean(vA, vB) + N(0, floraMutation x w))` into the row's value +- `w`, where `w` is the row value x `range` for `energy`, `regrow` and `life`, `range` for `hue` (also clamped to [-1, 1]) and `0.25 x range` of a year for `start`. `start` is blended and bounded as an offset from the row's `start` and wrapped mod 1 into [0, 1). `blendTraits` and `mutateTraits` in `js/sim/flora-traits.js` do this.
- **Death and the seed bank.** A patch whose stock has stayed within 1% of its floor for `patchDieTicks` consecutive ticks (`patchLowTicks`) dies: `patchAlive` 0, its stock 0, so it neither spawns nor regrows, and its slot keeps its position and traits as a soil seed bank of `seedBankTicks` (`patchBank`, counted down each tick). Each tick a banked slot sprouts with probability `sproutRate` (one draw from the `flora` stream per banked slot, in slot order): `patchAlive` 1, stock `seedStock` x `foodPatchCapacity`, `patchSerial` incremented, same traits. A bank that runs out frees the slot; a species with no patch alive and no bank is extinct and nothing restores it. Pollination seeds only into free slots (`patchAlive` 0 and no bank).
- **Pool.** The patch arrays hold `floraPatchMax` slots; `foodPatchCount` is the number alive at the start. `pickPatch`, `depletePatchAt`, `regrowPatches`, `createVisitPools` and `patchAt` read `patchAlive`; the visit pool and its bitset use `floraPatchMax` (at most 256 as today).
- **Local spread.** A seedling first tries the `foodPatchRadius x seedRadiusScale` ring about the visited patch, then bounded retries in its local disc; clamping stays in that basin and outside-local or blocked candidates are refused, so pollination spreads a species within a basin and never between basins. Berry, nut and flower reach the other basin only through seed carrying by herds ([megafauna.md](megafauna.md), Phase 3).
- **Selection of plants.** There is no plant fitness. A patch spreads its traits when blobs eat its items and then visit another of its species; a hue that blobs avoid is not eaten and so does not spread; a hue blobs approach spreads along their routes. A berry whose hue drifts towards the toxic cap's draws avoidance and spreads less; one that stays clear is eaten and spreads: a mimic can arise from either side.

Defaults: `floraPatchMax` 32, `patchDieTicks` 36000, `seedBankTicks` 432000, `sproutRate` 0.000005, `pollenTicks` 3000, `seedCooldownTicks` 6000, `seedRadiusScale` 1.5, `seedStock` 0.3, `floraMutation` 0.08.

## Detectors

Observer metrics in `js/evo/scene-flora.js`, direction `watch` unless it says one, no sim state change, no per-tick allocation. Each is `null` with a reason when nothing fed it.

| Metric                              | Scene or rule                | Definition                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `eval.food_species_share.<species>` | Season changes the map       | Food energy eaten from each species over all plant energy eaten after burn-in (six names)                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `eval.seasonal_diet_shift`          | Season changes the map       | The mean over season pairs of half the sum of absolute differences between the two seasons' species shares of food energy eaten (0 when diet is flat, 1 when two seasons share no species)                                                                                 |
| `eval.toxic_death_share`            | Death stays in the world     | Deaths of cause `POISONED` over all deaths after burn-in                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| `eval.toxic_avoidance`              | Signals can be false, rule 9 | Minus Ivlev electivity of toxic items: `(p - r) / (p + r)` where `r` is the toxic share of plant items eaten and `p` the toxic share of plant items alive at the 1000-tick checkpoints. 0 is indifferent, 1 is full avoidance; a `watch` metric, reported with the verdict |
| `eval.sick_share`                   | Condition changes the walk   | Alive agent-ticks with `dose >= sickDose` over all alive agent-ticks                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `eval.pollination_rate`             | Other blobs are the climate  | `PatchSeeded` events per 1000 ticks                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `eval.flora_hue_spread`             | Other blobs are the climate  | At each 1000-tick checkpoint, the mean over pollinated species of the standard deviation of `patchHue` over their alive patches; the mean over checkpoints                                                                                                                 |

`eval.toxic_avoidance` and `eval.food_species_share.*` are `watch` metrics added to `scripts/lib/watch-intent.mjs` as higher; `eval.toxic_death_share` and `eval.sick_share` as lower. `eval:power` measures each before a verdict cites it.

## Phases

### Delivery

Flora is built in four phases, one chunk per branch and draft PR from the latest `origin/main`. Code uses the species path by default and removes the replaced food keys and path. ## Feature evaluation

Every phase is evaluated by the same five steps, in its Runs tasks, and passes only if the whole ecosystem holds and not only its own mechanics:

1. **Baseline.** A run with the feature absent on the same seeds (`E69` for Phase 1 and the previous phase's final defaults after it).
2. **Controlled test.** A staged hand-built scene with a known answer for the new interaction (a death in summer fruiting in the next window, a blob offered a red and a green item, a pollen carrier visiting a second patch).
3. **Existing populations.** The effect per specialisation and per family per basin, and the predator gate (`eval.predators_at_end`, `eval.prey_types_at_end`, `eval.basin_family_effective`), against the baseline.
4. **16-seed compare.** `npm run eval:compare` of the feature enabled against the baseline.
5. **Bench at the maximum.** `npm run bench` at the intended maximum population (`maxAgents` 500, with the patch pool and the mycelium marks full), under the CPU lock.

## Experiments and done criteria

Experiment ids `E69` onward, each recorded in `evals/experiments.json` with its prediction before the run. Screens (`npm run screen`) use 8 seeds x 200000 ticks, on the live policy, and are not verdicts; arms are config overlays on the grid files named below, with exactly one `defaults` reference and all other E70-E72 arms changing exactly one config field. There are no factorial arms. The previous phase defaults are the reference. `flora` is an object keyed by species, which `createConfig` deep-merges key by key, so an arm overlays one field (`"flora": {"berry": {"energy": 1.8}}`); the grids use `arms`, because the `each` shorthand of `scripts/lib/screen-grid.mjs` addresses top-level keys only. A lever advances from its default only when an OAT value improves that lever's named seasonal/diet, toxin, seed or hue signal and passes the relevant local criteria and economy constraints in the Done criteria. Otherwise retain its default; a screen result is not permission to weaken another local gate. Record the inherited natural-selection Phase 4 survival and family measures (`eval.predators_at_end`, `eval.prey_types_at_end`, `eval.basin_family_effective`) on every screen as balancing diagnostics, but they do not block Flora feature selection or completion; the balancing plan owns that shortfall. Keep the diagnostic definitions: at least 7 of 8 seeds with a predator and at least one prey type at the last tick, and per-seed `eval.basin_family_effective` at least 3 on at least 6 of 8 seeds. Families are counted per basin, never as types: `eval.spec_effective` above 1.5 passes nothing alone. The family measure uses the genetic clusters of `scene-families.js` (distance below 0.5, first-founder assignment, inverse Simpson sizes, the smaller qualifying basin's last-quarter checkpoint mean), retaining null when neither basin qualifies. `eval.basin_lineage_effective` is a separate maternal-line diagnostic and cannot substitute.

**Predictions**, recorded in `evals/experiments.json` before `E70`:

- Nuts are the winter food: `life` 60000 keeps autumn nuts on the ground through winter and into spring, so `eval.food_species_share.nut` is the highest share of plant energy eaten in winter (year phase 0.75 to 1) and `eval.seasonal_diet_shift` is carried by nut and fungus against berry and flower.
- Grass does not replace them: it fruits all year but its patches regrow at `winterRegrow` 0.2 and the winter `foodScarcity` is 0.4, so winter `eval.winter_basin_density` rests on nuts and fungus.
- A winter crash is a result to read, and no arm fixes it by making grass richer in winter (a grass `yield`, `regrow` or winter-factor change, or any arm that runs grass at full rate all year). The levers are the nut `energy`, `yield` and `span` and the fungus window.
- A death in summer fruits as fungus in the next window through its mycelium mark, so `eval.food_species_share.fungus` is above 0 in `E70` arms with deaths in any season.

**The toxic cap rule.** If the `share` 3 arm fails the local toxin or economy criteria and `share` 0 passes, the cap stays rare: the adopted `share` is the highest of 1, 2 and 3 that passes those local criteria, and 0 is not adopted unless none does (reported as a partial result). The dose (`toxinDose`, `toxinCost`, `lethalDose`, `sickDose`) is never weakened so that the food instinct can keep eating the cap. E71 keeps toxicity fixed at 0.8 and tests toxinDose .8 and 1.2 as separate OAT arms; the original E71 range could not reach lethal dose with single-factor changes, while dose 1.2 permits a .96 cap hit and a later accepted hit after recovery to exceed lethalDose 1.0. No factorial interaction is introduced.
