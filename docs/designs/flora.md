# Flora

**Status:** Proposed - plant species, fruiting seasons, toxicity, a hue sense and pollination, built in four phases on their own branches after the natural-selection big bang merges; Phase 1 (species and fruiting seasons) first.
**Validated:** e2cd534 (2026-10-30)
**Priority:** 2 - built next after the big bang (natural-selection.md, Priority 1) merges, in phases on their own branches (user, 2026-10-30)
**Depends on:** [natural-selection.md](natural-selection.md), [brain-capacity-and-memory.md](brain-capacity-and-memory.md)

Today the world has one plant food: a 100 energy item with a 10% variant at 140, spawned by 16 regrowing patches (80% of spawns) and a background (20%), scaled by a season factor that changes only the amount, plus carcasses. Every food item looks the same, tastes the same and is there all year. This doc replaces it with plant species whose traits evolve through pollination, each fruiting in its own window of the year, some of them toxic, and gives the blob one new fact about each food it sees: its hue.

The scenes it serves ([principles.md](../principles.md)): **Season changes the map** (the food map differs by season and moves between basins), **Leave a dying patch** (a species that stops fruiting empties a patch), **Scavenge** (decomposers recycle the dead), **Other blobs are the climate** (plants co-evolve with the blobs that eat and carry them), **Signals cost, and they can be false** (a warning colour, a mimic), and **Something unnamed**. It adds no fitness term: every new fact reaches selection only through the world, as [principles.md](../principles.md) "Measure scenes, reward them almost never" requires. A plant that is eaten loses energy, a blob that eats a toxic plant gets sick, and a flower that is visited seeds more flowers.

Scope: the plant model in `js/sim/`, the hue sense in the brain layout, the sickness state, the pollination and plant evolution rules, the detectors, and the look of all of it in `js/app/renderer/`. Out of scope: a plant that moves, grows between ticks as an individual, or has a brain; a second nutrient; a plant defence other than toxicity; carcass changes beyond the decomposer.

## The species

A species is an archetype: a row of the `flora` config table. Every plant item and every patch carries the traits of its archetype.

| Trait           | Meaning                                                                                                                           |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `share`         | Weight in the initial patch mix (the patches are assigned by largest remainder over `foodPatchCount`)                             |
| `energy`        | Item energy as a share of `foodEnergy`; `energySpread` is the +- share around it                                                  |
| `radius`        | Item radius as a share of `foodRadius`                                                                                            |
| `yield`         | Relative item rate of a patch of this species (a patch's spawn weight is stock x `yield` x fruiting)                              |
| `regrow`        | Multiplier on the patch's regrowth rate                                                                                           |
| `life`          | Item lifetime in ticks (replaces `foodMaxAge`)                                                                                    |
| `start`, `span` | The fruiting window: year phase where it opens and its length as a share of the year (1 is all year)                              |
| `hue`           | The hue coordinate (below)                                                                                                        |
| `toxicity`      | 0 to 1; 0 is edible                                                                                                               |
| `pollenChance`  | The chance an item eaten loads pollen (0 for a species that is not pollinated)                                                    |
| `range`         | How far a patch's `hue`, `energy`, `regrow`, `life` and `start` may drift from the row, as a share (hue: x 1 on the [-1, 1] axis) |

Starting rows, in the order the table is written (the flora screens tune them; year phase 0 is the start of spring). `toxiccap` has `share` 0 until T14 raises it to 3; the patch mix over `foodPatchCount` 16 is then 5, 3, 2, 3 and 3 (grass, berry, nut, flower, toxic cap):

- **Grass** fruits all year and fast, and is the background food: the `foodBackgroundShare` of spawns are grass items.
- **Berry** fruits from early summer to early autumn, and its items last about a tenth of a year.
- **Nut** fruits in autumn and its items last 60000 ticks, into the next winter and past the first of spring.
- **Flower** gives small items (nectar) from spring to midsummer and is the main pollen host.
- **Fungus** has no patches. Its items spawn in the fertile zones `reap` leaves where agents died, from late autumn through winter (year phase 0.62 to 0.97). `placeFood` takes the zone branch with probability `fertileZoneSpawnChance` x the fungus fruiting weight; a draw that does not take it is made again as a patch or background draw, so the total item rate is unchanged and a zone feeds blobs in winter only through fungus.
- **Toxic cap** is a patch species with a warning hue and a toxicity of 0.8; its patches exist from T14.

The `flora` table holds exactly these six rows, validated by `checkFlora`, which lives in the new `js/sim/config-flora.js` and is called from `../../js/sim/config-validate.js` for a `flora` rule type, the way `checkSpecialisations` is called for `specialisations`. Every row has every field as a finite number in range, with no exemption: `share`, `yield`, `regrow`, `toxicity` and `pollenChance` at least 0 (`toxicity` and `pollenChance` at most 1); a row with `share` above 0 needs `yield` and `regrow` above 0; `start` in [0, 1); `span` in (0, 1]; `hue` in [-1, 1]; `fungus` has `share` 0, `yield` 0, `regrow` 0 and `range` 0. The largest item radius (`radius` x `foodRadius`) replaces `highFoodRadius` in the `hashCellSize` check of `../../js/sim/config-validate.js`. `highFoodChance`, `highFoodEnergy`, `highFoodEnergyVariance`, `highFoodRadius`, `foodEnergyVariance` and `foodMaxAge` are deleted; `foodEnergy` and `foodRadius` stay as the reference scales (the temperature gain in `tryEat` and `fitness-v5.js` read `foodEnergy`). Reports written before keep their old keys and stay comparable through `npm run eval:compare -- --allow-config-diff <keys>`.

### Fruiting

`fruitWeight(phase, start, span)` in `js/sim/fruiting.js` is 1 for `span` 1; else, with `u = ((phase - start) mod 1) / span`, it is `sin(pi u)^2` for `u` in [0, 1] and 0 outside, so a species ramps in, peaks mid-window and ramps out. Once per tick `refreshFruiting(world)` writes `patchWeight[i] = patchStock[i] x patchYield[i] x fruitWeight(world.season[1], patchStart[i], patchSpan[i])`. `pickPatch` draws one float and picks from `patchWeight` instead of the stock, in ascending index as today; with a total weight of 0 it returns -1 and `placeFood` makes the background draw. Items of `life` 60000 (nut) stay alive across a season, so `foodCount` can reach `maxFood`; `spawnFood` then stops spawning as it does today, and the screens read `foodCount` at the checkpoints and lower the nut `yield` if it saturates. The total item rate is unchanged (`areaExpected`); the season moves where the items are and what they are worth.

An item carries its own `foodSpecies`, `foodHue`, `foodTox`, `foodLife` and `foodPatch` (the patch it came from, -1 for background, zone and carcass items), copied from the patch at spawn, so a patch that later changes does not change items already lying there. `ageFood` removes a plant item older than its `foodLife`. A carcass keeps `carcassTicks`; its `foodHue` is `CARCASS_HUE` (0).

## The hue sense

Each food a blob sees has a hue `h` in [-1, 1], a linear coordinate, not an angle: -1 is green, then yellow, red at about 0.33 and magenta at 1 (the renderer maps `t = (h + 1) / 2` to an HSL hue of `120 - 180 t` degrees). One brain input per food slot carries it and stays in [-1, 1]; a plant hue never wraps.

- **Sensing.** `writeRay` in `../../js/sim/ray-march.js` writes `foodHue[food]` for a food hit into a derived array `rayHue` (`maxAgents x rayCount` floats, outside `worldHash`, zero for every other hit). `poolRays` writes the hue of each of the two strongest food hits into two new pooled values, `FOOD_HUE_VALUES` 2, placed after the 48 ray values and before the body block: hue of the strongest hit at index 48, of the second at 49. An absent hit leaves strength 0 and hue 0. Hue 0 is also a real hue (`CARCASS_HUE`), so the brain tells an absent hit from a hue-0 hit only by the strength input.
- **Layout.** `POOLED_VALUES` becomes 50 and `inputCount` 83 (50 pooled plus 33 body). `inputOffsets.bodyStart` and every `wSkip` row follow the constant. The skip block reads the hue values like any pooled value; `applyInstinct` in `../../js/evo/instinct.js` adds nothing to them. The inactive-input mask (`createInputActivity` in `../../js/sim/input-activity.js`), the instinct offsets, the crossover of the skip block (`../../js/evo/crossover.js`) and `eval.input_active_share` (`../../js/evo/metrics-weights.js`) all read the shifted offsets; T10 tests each.
- **Genome.** `weightCount` grows by `2 x hidden + 2 x geneOutputs` floats. The Genome sizes table of [brain-capacity-and-memory.md](brain-capacity-and-memory.md) gets the row below, and `ARCHIVE_VERSION` in `../../js/evo/archive-codec.js` goes from 4 to 5 (an archive or champion of another length or version is refused, naming the field).

| -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------- | --------- | --------- | --------- | --------- |
| `pooled-v2` top 2, no `SPLIT` genes, `brainSkip` (the layout before flora) | 2,238     | 3,302     | 4,494     | 7,262     | 10,542    |

## Toxicity and sickness

A toxic item (`foodTox` above 0) makes a dose. Per-agent `dose` (Float32, zero at birth) is the sickness state; it is not a brain input (the body block already reports the slower velocity, the lower energy and the stalled eating).

| Rule         | Behaviour                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Eating       | `ingestToxin` in `js/sim/toxin.js`, called by `tryEat` for a plant item: the energy gained is `value x share x (1 - tox x toxinCost)`; a negative result is removed as a `LOSS_CAUSE.COST`. `dose += tox x toxinDose`          |
| Sickness     | `isSick`: `dose >= sickDose`. A sick agent's thrust in `actAll` is multiplied by `sickThrust`, `canEatNow` is false, and `stepToxin` removes `sickDrain x dose` energy per tick (`LOSS_CAUSE.COST`)                            |
| Recovery     | `stepToxin` lowers `dose` by `doseDecay` per tick down to 0                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Death        | `deathCause` in `../../js/sim/population.js` returns the new `DEATH_CAUSE.POISONED` (4) when `dose >= lethalDose`, checked after old age and before the energy test. The body leaves a carcass and a fertile zone as any death |
| Learnability | The warning is the hue; nothing pays avoiding it                                                                                                                                                                               |

Defaults: `toxinDose` 0.45, `toxinCost` 1.2, `sickDose` 0.3, `sickThrust` 0.4, `sickDrain` 0.02, `doseDecay` 0.0008, `lethalDose` 1.0. One bite of a toxic cap (`tox` 0.8) gives a dose of 0.36 and costs 0.96 of the item's energy; a second within about 1100 ticks gives 0.72 less the decay; the third kills.

## Pollination and plant evolution

A species with `pollenChance` above 0 is pollinated. Patches become a population that grows, dies, sprouts from a seed bank and evolves.

- **Pollen.** Eating a plant item of a pollinated species whose `foodPatch` is a patch `p` loads pollen with probability `pollenChance`: per-agent `pollenPatch` (`p + 1`, 0 for none), `pollenSerial` (the patch's `patchSerial`) and `pollenTicks` (`pollenTicks`, counted down each tick by `agePollen`). A blob carries one pollen at a time; a new load replaces it. Pollen is void when `patchSerial[p]` no longer matches (the patch died and its slot was reused).
- **Visit.** Eating a plant item of patch `q` of the same species while carrying valid pollen from a patch `p` different from `q`, when `q` has not seeded in the last `seedCooldownTicks` and a slot is free (`patchAlive` 0) or becomes free, `seedPatch` in `js/sim/pollination.js` creates a patch there and clears the pollen. The pollen is cleared and `q` is stamped even when no slot is free; `SeedingRefused` logs the reason.
- **Seedling.** Its centre is at a distance of `foodPatchRadius x seedRadiusScale` from `q`'s centre at an angle drawn from the `flora` stream (moved to a point inside the world and clear of obstacles by the existing `placeInDisc` rule, at most `placementTries` draws), its stock `seedStock` x `foodPatchCapacity`, its `patchSerial` incremented. The fields it inherits (`yield`, `span`, `toxicity`, `pollenChance`) come from the row of the parents' shared species. Each evolving trait `v` (`energy`, `regrow`, `life`, `hue`, `start`) is one formula: `v = clamp(mean(vA, vB) + N(0, floraMutation x w))` into the row's value +- `w`, where `w` is the row value x `range` for `energy`, `regrow` and `life`, `range` for `hue` (also clamped to [-1, 1]) and `0.25 x range` of a year for `start`. `start` is blended and bounded as an offset from the row's `start` and wrapped mod 1 into [0, 1). `blendTraits` and `mutateTraits` in `js/sim/flora-traits.js` do this.
- **Death and the seed bank.** A patch whose stock has stayed within 1% of its floor for `patchDieTicks` consecutive ticks (`patchLowTicks`) dies: `patchAlive` 0, its stock 0, so it neither spawns nor regrows, and its slot keeps its position and traits as a soil seed bank of `seedBankTicks` (`patchBank`, counted down each tick). Each tick a banked slot sprouts with probability `sproutRate` (one draw from the `flora` stream per banked slot, in slot order): `patchAlive` 1, stock `seedStock` x `foodPatchCapacity`, `patchSerial` incremented, same traits. A bank that runs out frees the slot; a species with no patch alive and no bank is extinct and nothing restores it. Pollination seeds only into free slots (`patchAlive` 0 and no bank).
- **Pool.** The patch arrays hold `floraPatchMax` slots; `foodPatchCount` is the number alive at the start. `pickPatch`, `depletePatchAt`, `regrowPatches`, `createVisitPools` and `patchAt` read `patchAlive`; the visit pool and its bitset use `floraPatchMax` (at most 256 as today).
- **Selection of plants.** There is no plant fitness. A patch spreads its traits when blobs eat its items and then visit another of its species; a hue that blobs avoid is not eaten and so does not spread; a hue blobs approach spreads along their routes. A berry whose hue drifts towards the toxic cap's draws avoidance and spreads less; one that stays clear is eaten and spreads: a mimic can arise from either side.

Defaults: `floraPatchMax` 32, `patchDieTicks` 36000, `seedBankTicks` 432000, `sproutRate` 0.000005, `pollenTicks` 3000, `seedCooldownTicks` 6000, `seedRadiusScale` 1.5, `seedStock` 0.3, `floraMutation` 0.08.

## Detectors

Observer metrics in `js/evo/scene-flora.js`, direction `watch` unless it says one, no sim state change, no per-tick allocation. Each is `null` with a reason when nothing fed it.

| Metric                              | Scene or rule                | Definition                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `eval.food_species_share.<species>` | Season changes the map       | Food energy eaten from each species over all plant energy eaten after burn-in (six names)                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `eval.seasonal_diet_shift`          | Season changes the map       | The mean over season pairs of half the sum of absolute differences between the two seasons' species shares of food energy eaten (0 when diet is flat, 1 when two seasons share no species)                                                                                 |
| `eval.toxic_death_share`            | Death stays in the world     | Deaths of cause `POISONED` over all deaths after burn-in                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| `eval.toxic_avoidance`              | Signals can be false, rule 9 | Minus Ivlev electivity of toxic items: `(p - r) / (p + r)` where `r` is the toxic share of plant items eaten and `p` the toxic share of plant items alive at the 1000-tick checkpoints. 0 is indifferent, 1 is full avoidance; a `watch` metric, reported with the verdict |

## Phases

### Delivery

Flora is built after the natural-selection big bang merges to main, in four phases, each on its own branch from the latest `origin/main` and in its own PR. A numeric value is a config key; a boolean with one chosen value is not a key. Each phase pins the short goldens in each behaviour commit, re-pins the long goldens once at the end of its code chunk (Node, then `npm run browser:hash` in Chromium), and re-runs the survival gate on the phase's final world before its PR. Work is built in chunks as in [natural-selection.md](natural-selection.md): one commit per task, the pre-commit hook and the task's own tests per commit, the full gate and CI once per chunk PR; runs and timing stay out of code chunks.

## Experiments and done criteria

Experiment ids `E69` onward, each recorded in `evals/experiments.json` with its prediction before the run. Screens (`npm run screen`) use 8 seeds x 200000 ticks, on the live policy, and are not verdicts; arms are config overlays on the grid files named below, the previous phase's defaults the reference. `flora` is an object keyed by species, which `createConfig` deep-merges key by key, so an arm overlays one field (`"flora": {"berry": {"energy": 1.8}}`); the grids use `arms`, because the `each` shorthand of `scripts/lib/screen-grid.mjs` addresses top-level keys only. The survival gate is read on each screen's best arm: every seed alive above `floorTrigger` at the end, `eval.predator_presence` at least 0.5 and `eval.spec_effective` above 1.5.

| `E69` | 1     | The merged big-bang defaults, 16 seeds x 200000 ticks, run from a checkout of main before T1 (task T0)                                                                                                                                                                                    | The reference every flora verdict is compared with                                        |

## Decisions

| Decision                                                                                                                                                           | Why                                                                                                                           | By      | Date       |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- | ---------- |
| About six species archetypes whose traits (hue, energy, regrowth, lifetime, fruiting window) evolve through pollination                                            | Real plants differ in kind and drift in detail; fixed species would give a map that changes by season but never by generation | User    | 2026-10-30 |
| Dose-based sickness: a toxic item costs energy and adds a dose; a dose slows the blob, stops eating, decays, and a few bites in a row kill (`POISONED`)            | A warning colour is learnable only if the harm is real, graded and sometimes fatal                                            | User    | 2026-10-30 |
| One hue value per pooled food slot (83 inputs), a linear coordinate in [-1, 1]                                                                                     | It gives the brain the one fact avoidance needs at the smallest layout change; a cos and sin pair would cost 85 inputs        | User    | 2026-10-30 |
| `bench.step_ms@500` within 15% of the big-bang baseline and the browser frame rate held at 4K                                                                      | The sim and the draw already run near their budgets                                                                           | User    | 2026-10-30 |
| Built next after the big bang merges, in phases on its own branches                                                                                                | The big bang's gate and PR finish first; each flora phase is then a single reviewable PR on the merged world                  | User    | 2026-10-30 |
| Fungus is zone-based and does not evolve; toxic cap, grass and nut are not pollinated; flower and berry are                                                        | A decomposer fruits where the dead lie (fertile zones); pollination hosts are the species whose items are nectar and fruit    | Manager | 2026-10-30 |
| Patch death and seeding use slots of one pool; no rescue guard. A dead patch leaves a soil seed bank that sprouts at a low rate and runs out after `seedBankTicks` | Real seed banks persist for years but not forever, so a stand recovers from its soil and a species can still go extinct       | Manager | 2026-10-30 |
| The hue is a linear coordinate and not an angle                                                                                                                    | One input per food slot then carries it inside [-1, 1] with no wrap; a plant hue never crosses the seam                       | Manager | 2026-10-30 |
| A zone draw outside fungus's window is made again as a patch or background draw; the window runs late autumn to winter                                             | The total item rate stays the item rate of the economy, and decomposers fruit through the cold months                         | Manager | 2026-10-30 |
| `toxiccap` starts at `share` 0 and T14 raises it to 3                                                                                                              | Phase 1 has no toxicity, so no patch of a toxic species may exist before the dose does                                        | Manager | 2026-10-30 |
| No dose input and no innate colour instinct                                                                                                                        | The layout grows by the hue only, and a newborn has no colour preference, so avoidance has to be evolved, not scripted        | Manager | 2026-10-30 |
| Experiment ids continue at `E69`                                                                                                                                   | `E67` and `E68` are the last recorded                                                                                         | Manager | 2026-10-30 |
