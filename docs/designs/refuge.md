# Refuge

**Status:** In progress - Phase 1 chunk A built (the eight detectors and the aperture test, T1 to T4, no behaviour change); next the diagnosis run (T5, T6), then three default-off cover mechanisms and one geometry key.
**Validated:** b26e468 (2026-11-06)
**Priority:** 2 - the Refuge scene is absent on the default world (`eval.refuge_use_under_threat` -0.003, E126 and E129), and cover is the classic stabiliser of the boom-and-dip the floor-off worlds show; it queues behind natural-selection.md (Priority 1), whose Phase 4 balancing is the parent body of work
**Depends on:** [natural-selection.md](natural-selection.md)

The scene this doc serves ([principles.md](../principles.md), Refuge): **under threat, prey stack into a rock ring or a
cold margin and then leak back out to feed.** Two rules stay true: **cover blocks rays, and cover is not free food.**
On the default world the scene is absent: rings are 0.94% of the map, give a threatened prey no protection and no pull,
and the detector reads chance. This doc makes the scene visible, then watches whether cover also steadies the
predator-prey loop (the floor-off collapse count is a watched side effect, never a target).

## Principle - the detector first, the guards second

The verdict on every switch is `eval.refuge_use_under_threat` (and its prey-only and episode forms) up with the
`eval:compare` interval excluding 0. A switch that lifts survival or population but leaves the scene absent is deleted.
Nothing here pays fitness for entering a ring; the world makes cover useful and the brain finds it.

- **Hunters keep their rule.** Floor on: hunters alive at the last tick on 12 of 16 seeds or more, and no more seeds with a
  mean population under 80 than the defaults.
- **Cover must not make hunting impossible.** `eval.hunter_kill_conversion_by_age.1k-2k` stays at 80% of the defaults arm
  or more, and `eval.hunter_independent_kill_share` is not down ([hunter-learning.md](hunter-learning.md)).
- **Floor-off collapse is watched.** The count of floor-off seeds that end under 80 agents (E126: 19 of 32 at 200,000
  ticks) is recorded for the best arm and never a target: the floor-off world is a stress test.

### How the detector reads

`eval.refuge_use_under_threat` is built in `sampleTerrain` and `terrainMetrics` in `../../js/evo/scene-terrain.js`. Every
10 ticks after burn-in each alive agent is threatened (a "can bite me" ray hit at `THREAT_PROXIMITY` 0.5 or more) or
calm, and `inRefuge` tests its centre against the rings. The metric is the threatened share inside minus the calm share
inside. Limits: it samples **hunters as well as prey** (`tickStyle` in `../../js/evo/style.js` excludes hunters); it reads
a **state, not an episode** (an entry and exit between two samples is invisible); it ignores the **cold margin**.
Chance is zero, so -0.003 on 16 floor-on seeds says position and threat are unrelated.

### Nature model

| Behaviour in nature                                                                                                                                        | Maps to                                                                                      |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A refuge that protects a fixed share of prey stabilises the pair: the predator cannot drive prey to zero, so the oscillation damps (McNair 1986; Sih 1987) | The bite guard and concealment lower the take at low prey density, where the Allee edge sits |
| Cover breaks the predator's line of sight and slows its strike (Lima and Dill 1990)                                                                        | `coverConceals` and `coverBiteGuard`                                                         |
| Refuge costs foraging: animals leave cover to feed, trading safety against food (Lima and Dill 1990; the landscape of fear, Laundre et al. 2001)           | `coverFoodFree`: nothing to eat inside                                                       |
| Groups in cover dilute risk per animal (Hamilton 1971)                                                                                                     | `eval.refuge_crowding`                                                                       |
| A size refuge: a burrow admits the vole and not the fox                                                                                                    | `refugeSizeMax` and `refugeSlack` set who fits                                               |

## Config keys and options

Validated in `../../js/sim/config-rules.js` and `config-validate.js`, defaulted in `../../js/sim/config-defaults.js`;

| Key               | Type                 | Default | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ----------------- | -------------------- | ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `coverConceals`   | boolean              | false   | Phase 2. A ray cast from outside a ring skips an agent whose centre is inside it; a ray from inside the same ring sees everything. Rays only (scent, calls, contact unchanged)                                     |
| `coverBiteGuard`  | boolean              | false   | Phase 2. A bite on a victim inside a ring, by a biter outside that ring, moves `coverBiteFactor` of the energy                                                                                                     |

| `coverFoodFree`   | boolean              | false   | Phase 2. Plant placement rejects a point inside a ring: `placeClear` and `placeInDisc` in `spawn.js` and the flora placement in `flora-placement.js`. Carcasses, dung and dropped seeds still land where they fall |
| `refugeSlack`     | number, 0 or more px | 0       | Phase 2. Widens every aperture: `ringRadius` becomes `(REFUGE_ROCK_RADIUS + refugeSizeMax + refugeSlack) / sin(pi / 8)`                                                                                            |

## Detectors

New `eval.*` metrics from the eval observer at the 10-tick sample of `sampleTerrain`, none a reward.

| Metric                              | Direction | Reads                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------------------------- | --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `eval.refuge_prey_use_under_threat` | higher    | The existing metric over non-hunting agents only                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `eval.refuge_episode_entry`         | watch     | An episode opens at the first threatened sample after a calm one. Share of episodes with the agent inside a ring at any sample within `REFUGE_WINDOW` (200 ticks), minus the same share for episodes opened at calm samples |
| `eval.refuge_dwell_ticks`           | watch     | Median ticks from a ring entry to the exit, non-hunting agents                                                                                                                                                              |
| `eval.refuge_exit_fed`              | watch     | Share of ring exits followed by a meal within `REFUGE_WINDOW` ticks (the leak to feed)                                                                                                                                      |
| `eval.refuge_crowding`              | watch     | Mean non-hunting agents inside a ring that holds a threatened agent (the stack)                                                                                                                                             |
| `eval.refuge_bite_deficit`          | watch     | Bites per prey-tick inside a ring over bites per prey-tick outside; below 1, cover protects                                                                                                                                 |
| `eval.refuge_eligible_share`        | watch     | Share of non-hunting agent-ticks with body radius at most `refugeSizeMax` minus the clearance the geometry test names                                                                                                       |
| `eval.cold_margin_use_under_threat` | watch     | The refuge metric for the two cold bands                                                                                                                                                                                    |

`REFUGE_WINDOW` and the minimum sample counts are named exports of `js/evo/scene-refuge.js`.

## Phases

### Phase 1 - Detectors and diagnosis

**Targets:** none - the new metrics are instruments (`watch`) and no existing metric moves; the phase ends with a written diagnosis.

Done means: the eight metrics run, `npm run eval:power` has rated each, and a [sim-notes.md](sim-notes.md) entry answers
four questions with numbers from the 16-seed floor-on run: (1) is the geometry usable (`eval.refuge_eligible_share`,
entry rate by body radius, agents that fit at once); (2) do prey meet cover and threat together (rock hits on rays while
threatened, `eval.refuge_episode_entry` against its calm control); (3) does cover help when used
(`eval.refuge_bite_deficit`); (4) is the detector sound (prey-only against all-agent form, the cold-margin form). The
answers trim the Phase 2 arms and become the screen's prediction.

**Targets:** eval.refuge_use_under_threat, eval.refuge_prey_use_under_threat, eval.refuge_episode_entry, eval.refuge_bite_deficit, eval.refuge_exit_fed, eval.refuge_crowding, eval.refuge_eligible_share, eval.predators_at_end, eval.prey_types_at_end, eval.population_mean, eval.hunter_kill_conversion_by_age.1k-2k, eval.hunter_independent_kill_share, eval.boom_bust_count, eval.prey_extinctions

- **The scene appears.** `eval.refuge_use_under_threat` and `eval.refuge_prey_use_under_threat` up by an absolute difference of at least 0.05 (five
  times the area share; read on the per-seed difference interval, not `--min-effect`, which is a fraction of a before mean near 0), interval excluding 0, above zero on 12 of 16 seeds. T5's `eval:power` run can raise the bar.
- **Stack then leak.** `eval.refuge_episode_entry` positive with the interval excluding 0, `eval.refuge_exit_fed` above
  0.3, `eval.refuge_crowding` at least 2.

- **Guards** (Principle, through `eval:compare --min-effect 0.05`): the hunter keep rule; `eval.hunter_kill_conversion_by_age.1k-2k` at least 80% of the defaults;
  `eval.hunter_independent_kill_share` not down; `eval.prey_types_at_end` and `eval.population_mean` not down;
  `eval.prey_extinctions` and `eval.boom_bust_count` not up.

  first summer dip.
