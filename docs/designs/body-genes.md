Today a genome holds four trait genes (`TRAIT_INDEX` in `js/sim/brain-layout.js`: `SIZE`, `VISION`, `CHILD_SHARE`, `LEARN`), each moving its trait by at most 30% through `traitValue` in `js/sim/traits.js`, the same for every specialisation. Every other body number is a global config key (`maxVelocity`, `maxThrust`, the temperature bands, `fatSlowShare`, `sprintCost`, ...) or a per-spec multiplier no gene reaches. Genes shape how an agent thinks and hardly at all how it is built.

This doc keeps the five specialisations (`SPECIALISATION_KEYS` in `js/sim/config-spec-rules.js`) and gives them a body that genes can change. A spec's table holds its values (speed, thrust, heat, fat, burn, size); a spec's gene menu names which traits its genes may shift and by how much; each heritable trait is paired with a cost, so no gene is free. The existing four genes are the first menu entries. Thirteen brain inputs let an agent read what bodies physically show (size, speed, whether they are closing, and how much of the view a kind fills), so a hunter can pick the small, the slow and the spent and avoid what is large.

Scenes served: **Same spec, different custom** (a lineage of fast, lean or fat bodies inside one spec), **A real chase** (a faster predator is a hotter one), **Other blobs are the climate**, and the trait **A body is a budget** ([principles.md](../principles.md)). [role-budget.md](role-budget.md) reads the spec table and the gene ranges to say whether a role is possible for the best and the worst body of a spec.

## The lane catalogue

A lane is one number about one body, fixed for its life: its speed cap, its bite, its call radius, its age of maturity. Every setting that describes a body is a lane. A lane's base is its global config key times the spec's value for it; a trait gene may shift it; a reader reads the lane, never the config key.

`js/sim/body-lanes.js` holds `LANES`, the catalogue: for each lane its `name` (the global key's name), its `family`, its base, and its `kind` (`ratio`, or `offset` for the heat band). The config holds 230 top-level keys: 108 describe a body, 110 the world or the medium, 12 the brain. With the 16 per-spec fields of `SPECIALISATION_DEFAULTS` that is 124 body settings. They make 110 lanes, because a spec field that scales a key joins that key's lane (`turnMultiplier` is a factor of `maxTurn`, not a lane of its own: eight fields join), and six settings are not lanes (`canHunt`, which stays a flag, `sprintMultiplier`, `temperatureMax` and `temperatureStart`, and the two `scout*Multiplier` keys, which become the scout's `body` entries in Phase 5; `NOT_LANES` in `js/sim/body-lanes.js` lists each with its reason). The three new lanes (`hearing`, `smokeSight`, `nerve`) make 113, and the two heat keys the costs add (`heatSpeedCap`, `sprintHeat`, T13) make 115. The inventory, key by key with its default, readers and Rust use, is [settings-inventory.md](../../evals/decisions/body-genes/settings-inventory.md).

A lane's value for one agent is one product, computed in this order:

`lane = key x field x body x (each trait's ratio ^ power) x (each paired cost's ratio ^ exponent)`

| Factor  | Is                                                                                                                                           | 1 when                                                        |
| ------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `key`   | the global config key the lane is named after (`maxTurn`, `biteEnergy`)                                                                      | the lane has no global key (`visionRange`, `rearVisionShare`) |
| `field` | the spec's existing field for that lane (`turnMultiplier` for `maxTurn`, `biteMultiplier` for `biteEnergy`, `visionRange` for `visionRange`) | the spec has none                                             |
| `body`  | the spec's `body.<lane>` override                                                                                                            | not set                                                       |
| ratio   | `r = 1 + span x tanh(gene)` of each trait whose `effects` or `costs` name the lane                                                           | the trait's span is 0 for the spec, or its gene is 0          |

A lane is named after its global key where one exists, else after the spec field. `laneBases` returns `key x field x body`. The six temperature thresholds are the one sum: `threshold = key + body.heatBand + heatBandShift`, where `heatBandShift` is the `heatBand` trait's `span x tanh(gene)` in degrees; `heatBand` is an override and a trait, not a lane.

- **One storage.** Every lane is a per-slot `Float64Array` of `world.lanes`, written once per birth by `applyTraits`; a double, so a lane at its base is the same number the reader computes today. 115 lanes for 500 agents are under 500 KB, so no lane is kept as a per-spec table or a scalar to save space. The per-spec tables of today (`visionBySpec`, `maxEnergyBySpec`, `turnBySpec`, `sprintBySpec`, the store's drain table and the rest) and the per-call name loop of `specParams` go as their readers move. A kernel that reads a lane takes its pointer (`maxVelocity` joins `sprintFactor` in `move_all`).
- **One override.** A spec's `body` field is an object from a lane name to a positive number (a multiplier, default 1), plus `heatBand` in degrees (default 0). The `bodyMap` rule in `js/sim/config-spec-rules.js` refuses a name that is not in `LANES`, and refuses an override of a lane whose `moved` field is false: its sim readers still read the config key, so the override would change the calculator and not the world. `moved` is set as each family's readers move, and the field is deleted with T44. The 16 per-spec fields of today (`visionRange`, `biteMultiplier`, `sprintBoost` and the rest) keep their names and are the base of their lanes. Rules written today as global keys for one spec become `body` entries of that spec, and the keys go: `scoutCallRadiusMultiplier` and `scoutAlarmCostMultiplier` (the scout's `body.callRadius` and `body.alarmCost`), and the `canHunt` tests on `huntUpkeep`, `juvenileBurn`, `predatorRestTicks` and `strikeMissCost` (0 in every non-hunter's `body`).
- **Lane or state.** A lane is constant for a life. What changes within a life is an effect of [status-effects.md](status-effects.md), which multiplies a lane each tick: the fat factor (`fatFactor`), the heat efficiency and the passive multiplier (`js/sim/temperature.js`), grazing, handling, the rest after a kill and sickness (`thrustOf`), satiation and digestion (`canEatNow`), the juvenile burn by age, the sprint store's level, the reward scale. Birth reflexes write the genome, and merit, mate rank and the proof rule are selection rules; none is a lane.
- **Detectors, drills and the app** read the global key as a normaliser (`maxVelocity` has 99 such reads) and keep doing so.
- **`laneBases(config, specName)`** returns every lane's base without a world. [role-budget.md](role-budget.md) reads it, so the calculator sees all 110 from the day the catalogue lands, whether or not a sim reader has moved yet.

Readers move to lanes in two steps. Phase 1 moves the lanes a trait or the calculator's first rows need: movement, heat, and the energy lanes of the table below (about 25). Phase 5 moves the rest, a family to a commit; until a family moves, its readers read the config key and its lanes are written and unread. A reader that takes the lane in place of a product it already computed in the same order keeps every golden `worldHash`; where the move changes the order of a multiplication the last bits change, and the goldens are re-pinned in their own commit beside an eval report whose metrics agree to 1e-9. The dead key `foodSpawnRate` is deleted.

Phase 1 moved (T3) `maxVelocity` (the kernel's per-slot cap, `KERNEL_LANES` in `js/sim/lane-state.js`), `maxThrust`, `maxTurn`, `sprintCost`, `sprintDrain`, `sprintRefill`, `fatSlowShare`, `visionRange`, `maxEnergy`, `passiveLoss`, `temperatureGainMove`, `temperatureLossPassive` and the six thresholds, which the threshold functions of `js/sim/temperature.js` now read as `(world, slot)`. Every `maxEnergyBySpec` read in `js/sim` and the inspector reads the `maxEnergy` lane; the table stays as the detectors' normaliser (`js/evo` scenes read it, as the bullet on detectors says), and `visionBySpec`, `turnBySpec` and `sprintBySpec` stay for the oracles and the kernel's boost until their lanes move. Moving `maxTurn` and `passiveLoss` changes the order of a multiplication, and `visionOf` returns the double lane in place of a `Float32` ratio times the table, so T3 re-pins the goldens for rounding.

## The trait system

A trait is one entry of a registry. The layout, the birth, the mutation, the inspector, the detectors and the calculator read the registry and loop over it; none names a trait. Adding or changing a trait is an edit to its entry and its numbers.

### The registry (`js/sim/body-traits.js`)

`TRAITS` is a frozen list of the 18 named traits. `TRAIT_INDEX` is built from it, and `TRAIT_GENES` is `TRAIT_SLOTS` (24: the named traits and six spare slots) (`SIZE` 0, `VISION` 1, `CHILD_SHARE` 2, `LEARN` 3, `SPEED` 4, `SPRINT_STORE` 5, `FAT` 6, `HEAT_BAND` 7, `BURN` 8, `HEARING` 9, `NOSE` 10, `SMOKE_SIGHT` 11, `EYES_OR_EARS` 12, `CLEAR_OR_SMOKE` 13, `AGILE_OR_FAST` 14, `WIDE_OR_FAR` 15, `GUT` 16, `NERVE` 17), so the layout follows the registry.

| Field        | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `name`       | the trait's name, the key of a spec's menu and of every report                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `index`      | the gene's offset from `traitStart`; indices are append-only, and a removed trait leaves its index unused until the next layout version                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `kind`       | `ratio`: the gene gives `r = 1 + span x tanh(g)` and multiplies its lanes. `offset`: it gives `span x tanh(g)` in the trait's unit and is added (only `heatBand`, to the six thresholds). `raw`: the reader takes the gene itself and the menu's span for it is not read, so `traitRatio` returns 1 (only `learn`: `hebbian_step` in `wasm/src/brain.rs` reads the gene from the weight row through `det_sigmoid`) |
| `effects`    | a list of `{lane, power}`: each per-slot lane the trait multiplies by `r^power` (a `ratio` trait) or shifts (an `offset` trait)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `costs`      | a list of `{lane, exponentKey, sign}`: each lane the trait's paired cost multiplies by `r^(sign x config[exponentKey])`, `sign` 1 or -1; an exponent of 0 is no cost. A cost that needs a number no lane holds adds that lane to the catalogue (`heatSpeedCap` and `sprintHeat` are heat lanes)                                                                                                                    |
| `inherit`    | `either` (the child takes the gene whole from one parent) or `blend` (the parents' mean); default `either`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| `sigmaScale` | the trait's mutation step as a multiple of `traitMutationSigma`; default 1                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| `budget`     | whether [role-budget.md](role-budget.md) reads the trait (`traitCorners`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `describe`   | one line for the inspector and the generated table                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |

A trait's `effects` and `costs` name lanes of the catalogue (`LANES` in `js/sim/body-lanes.js`), so any body setting can be made heritable by an entry here. `applyTraits(world, slot)` multiplies the lanes `writeLaneBases` just wrote at their bases by `r^power` for every trait that names them (a trait whose span is 0 for the spec is skipped), so each lane is computed once per birth as `base x product of r^power over the traits that name it`, and `applyGenes` in `js/sim/traits.js` calls both in that order (a world holds `traitSpans`, the spans of every spec, built by `spanTable`); nothing is computed per tick. With every gene at 0, or every span at 0, each lane equals its base. The existing genes write lanes like the rest: `size` the `baseSize` lane, `vision` the `visionRange` lane in px (`world.vision`, a ratio today, goes and `visionOf` returns the lane), `childShare` the `childEnergyShare` lane; `learn` writes none.

A genome holds `TRAIT_SLOTS` (24) trait genes, of which the registry names 18; the six spare slots are 0 and read by nothing. A trait added later takes a spare slot, so it changes no genome layout and refuses no saved genome; only the twenty-fifth trait needs a new layout version. `TRAIT_GENES` is `TRAIT_SLOTS`.

**A trade between two abilities is one trait.** A trait whose `effects` name two lanes with opposite `power` moves them against each other on one gene: more of one is less of the other, with no cost key to tune. Four are registered, in slots 12 to 15, at a span of 0 until Phase 3 screens them:

| Trade trait    | Effects                                                   | Reads as                                                                    |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `eyesOrEars`   | `visionRange` power 1, `hearing` power -1                 | a body that sees further hears less far, and the reverse                    |
| `clearOrSmoke` | `visionRange` power 1, `smokeSight` power -1              | a body that sees less far is less blinded by smoke                          |
| `agileOrFast`  | `maxTurn` power 1, `maxVelocity` and `maxThrust` power -1 | a body that turns tighter is slower in a line: the jinker and the sprinter  |
| `wideOrFar`    | `rearVisionShare` power 1, `visionRange` power -1         | a body that sees all round sees less far ahead: the watcher and the stalker |

Whether an ability is priced by a cost (`hearing` with `hearingCost`) or by a trade (`eyesOrEars`) is a choice Phase 3 screens: the two forms are arms of the same screen.

Two more traits take slots 16 and 17:

| Trait   | Effects                                                                                                                                                                          | Paired cost                                                                                                                                               |
| ------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `gut`   | `plantFoodValue` power 1, `meatFoodValue` power -1                                                                                                                               | built in: a gut that takes more from plants takes less from meat, so a lineage leans to grazing or to scavenging                                          |
| `nerve` | the `nerve` lane (new, base 1), which multiplies the strength of the adrenaline effects of [status-effects.md](status-effects.md) (`rallied`, `alert`, `panicked`) for this body | built in: those effects' burn and heat are multiplied by the same lane, and the `shaken` slump after a panic lasts longer; a jumpy lineage and a calm one |

Screened later and apart, since each changes how a population grows and not only what a body can do: the age of maturity, the length of life, and the number of children against the energy each is given.

### The menu and the numbers

A spec's `genes` field maps a trait name to a span in `[0, MAX_TRAIT_SPAN]` (0.5); `heatBand` is in degrees. A span of 0 fixes the trait for that spec, so the menu is the switch: no flag turns the system on. The `geneMenu` rule in `js/sim/config-spec-rules.js` refuses a name the registry lacks (`geneMenuProblem` in `js/sim/body-traits.js`), and a span above `MAX_TRAIT_SPAN`, or above `MAX_OFFSET_SPAN` (20 degrees) for the `offset` trait `heatBand`. A partial menu is filled from the defaults, so a validated config holds a span for every named trait.

| `hearingCost`        | number | 0       | standing-loss share of the hearing cost above ratio 1; 0 is off                                                                                                           |
| `noseCost`           | number | 0       | standing-loss share of the nose cost above ratio 1; 0 is off                                                                                                              |
| `smokeSightRange`    | number | 0       | exponent of the smoke-sight-to-range cost; 0 is off                                                                                                                       |
| `traitMutationSigma` | number | 0.1     | the step of a trait gene's mutation (`TRAIT_SIGMA` today, a constant in `js/evo/mutation.js`)                                                                             |
| `traitGeneClamp`     | number | 3       | a trait gene is clamped to plus or minus this after mutation; `tanh(3)` is 0.995, so a gene cannot drift into the flat part of the curve where a mutation changes nothing |

### Inheritance (`js/evo/trait-inherit.js`)

A birth builds its genome in `childBrain` in `js/evo/policies/natural-birth.js`: the sigma, the cross (`crossNeurons` in `js/evo/crossover.js`), the birth reflex, then the mutation (`mutateWeights` in `js/evo/mutation.js`). Today `crossNeurons` ends with a loop over the trait genes and `mutateWeights` steps the trait and leak genes with the constant `TRAIT_SIGMA`. The trait genes move to two functions, each called once per birth from `childBrain`.

| Function                                            | Called                                                                                                                                            | Does, for each trait in index order                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `crossTraits(child, mother, father, {layout, rng})` | by `crossNeurons` in place of its trait loop, at the same point in the draw order (after the neurons, before `crossSkip`); not called for a split | `either`: one `nextFloat`, the mother's gene below 0.5, else the father's (today's rule). `blend`: the mean of the two, no draw. `raw` traits inherit as `either`                                                                       |
| `mutateTraits(row, layout, rng, {rate, config})`    | by `childBrain` straight after `mutateWeights`, for every birth                                                                                   | when `rate` is under 1, one `nextFloat` and the gene is skipped at or above `rate` (the rule `mutateWeights` applies per gene); then add `traitMutationSigma x sigmaScale x nextGaussian`; then clamp to plus or minus `traitGeneClamp` |

- `mutateWeights` skips the genes from `traitStart` to `leakStart`. The leak genes keep `TRAIT_SIGMA` and `WEIGHT_CLAMP`, in `mutateWeights`, as today.
- A genome made with no parent (`randomWeights`, the seeder of `js/evo/policies/map-elites-seed.js`, `scripts/learn-probe.mjs`) starts every trait gene at 0; a mutated copy of an archive genome passes through `mutateTraits`.
- `inheritTraits(child, mother, father, {layout, rng, rate, config})` calls the two in order and is what the contract tests drive (the lint limit is 4 parameters, so `crossTraits` takes the `{layout, rng}` context `crossNeurons` has, and the three functions take an optional `traits` registry for tests). `childBrain` and the seeder call `mutateTraits` with the birth's `mutation` options, which hold the `rate` and the `config`.
- The draws come from the birth's RNG stream in this order, so a world replays from seed and config. The twenty more slots are twenty more draws per mating and per mutation from T7 on (a spare slot draws like a trait, so a trait added later moves no draw). What T33 moves is the order of the mutation draws (the trait block after every weight and leak gene, not between the skip block and the leak genes) and the clamp, 3 in place of `WEIGHT_CLAMP` (4) on the trait genes: every golden is re-pinned by this change.

### Adding a trait

1. Add the registry entry (the next free index, its effects, its costs).
2. Its lanes are already in the catalogue. If a lane's family has not moved yet (Phase 5), move that lane's readers from the config key to the lane in the same change.
3. Add its span to each spec's menu (0 where the spec does not vary) and its cost exponent key.
4. Bump `BRAIN_LAYOUT_NAME`, add the `library/seasons.json` entry, and re-pin the goldens in their own commit.
5. Run the contract tests and `npm run traits -- --write`.

## The body sense

`agentChannel` in `js/sim/ray-march.js` sorts a hit on an agent into kin, threat (it could bite the caster), prey (the caster could bite it) or other, and the channel holds the hit's proximity. That is all a brain reads of another body: a hunter sees a slow, spent reproducer, a fast scout and a large defender that shoves as the same `prey` signal at the same distance, cannot tell a body that is closing from one that is leaving, and reads the two nearest of a kind whether two or twenty are in view.

Thirteen new inputs let a brain read what bodies physically show: for a body, its size, its speed and whether it is closing; for a kind, how much of the view it fills. No agent carries a label; a spec is told apart as far as its body differs. The inputs are always on: there is no switch.

### Raw lanes (JS)

`writeRay` in `js/sim/ray-march.js` writes three per-ray lanes beside `rayHue`, at `slot x rayCount + ray`. The sense kernel (`wasm/src/sense.rs`) finds the nearest hit and is unchanged: the hit agent's slot is `key - kind x KIND_STRIDE`.

| Lane         | Written for an agent hit                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Else |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---- |
| `raySize`    | `tanh(size[hit] / size[caster] - 1)` (`detTanh`): 0 for an equal body, negative for a smaller one                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | 0    |
| `raySpeed`   | `min(1, hypot(vx[hit], vy[hit]) / (maxVelocity x SPEED_SENSE_SPAN))` with `SPEED_SENSE_SPAN` 2, so a body at the cruise cap reads 0.5 and a sprinting one more                                                                                                                                                        | 0    |
| `rayClosing` | the rate the gap shrinks, `-((vx[hit] - vx[caster]) x dx + (vy[hit] - vy[caster]) x dy) / d` with `(dx, dy)` from the caster to the hit and `d` its length, divided by `maxVelocity x SPEED_SENSE_SPAN` and clamped to plus or minus 1: above 0 when the two are closing, below 0 when the gap opens, 0 when `d` is 0 | 0    |

Each is a `Float32Array(maxAgents x rayCount)` lane of `world` (`js/sim/world.js`), in `BRAIN_FIELDS` and `arenaSpecs` of `js/sim/wasm-arena.js`, as `rayHue` is. `bodySenseOf(world, caster, hit, out)` in `js/sim/body-sense.js` writes the three numbers into `out` and allocates nothing.

### Pooled values (Rust)

`pool` in `wasm/src/brain.rs` keeps two hits a channel as `[strength, cos, sin]` and carries each food hit's hue with it through the demotion. It carries the size, the speed and the closing of a prey hit and of a threat hit the same way, and it counts the rays each agent channel wins.

| Pooled index | Value                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

| 57, 58, 59   | size, speed and closing of the second prey hit                                                                                                                       |
| 60, 61, 62   | size, speed and closing of the strongest threat hit                                                                                                                  |
| 63 to 66     | the view share of threat, prey, other and kin: the rays whose nearest hit is of that channel, over `rayCount`                                                        |
| 67, 68       | the two food hues (`foodHueStart = POOLED_VALUES - FOOD_HUE_VALUES`, and `hue_start = pv - 2` in `pool`, both still hold)                                            |
| 69 to 105    | the 37 body inputs: the 36 of today and `reserve`, the sprint store's level, last ([status-effects.md](status-effects.md), "What the body feels"); `EXTRA_INPUTS` 37 |

## Detectors

Computed by the eval observer; none is a reward. The three choice metrics are built (`js/evo/scene-hunter-choice.js`): a bite start is a landed bite on a body the hunter was not biting in the last 3 ticks; a prey in view is a body the hunter `couldBite` inside `visionOf`; the size and speed of the bitten prey are read before the bite (a bite shrinks and kicks it), against the mean of the other prey in view; defender exposure is sampled every 5th tick. A bite that kills in its first tick is not seen. The size and speed choices are null under 30 starts with two prey in view.

| Metric                     | Direction | Reads                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------------- | --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `eval.gene_spread.<trait>` | watch     | Standard deviation of the trait ratio across living agents of each spec after burn-in                                            |
| `eval.gene_edge_share`     | watch     | The share of living agents with any menu gene at or beyond 90% of its span: a menu that is too narrow or a cost that is too weak |

Registered in `js/evo/metric-registry/` and computed in `js/evo/scene-genes.js`. The names avoid `eval.trait_spread`, which stays the spread of the four original genes.

## The calculator reads the menu

[role-budget.md](role-budget.md) resolves a body through `roleParams(config, specName)`. Phase 2 adds `roleParams(config, specName, traits)` with a ratio per trait, and `traitCorners(config, specName)` in `js/evo/role-budget-genes.js`, which enumerates the corners of the menu traits a row reads (`vision`, `speed`, `sprintStore`, `fat`, `size`, `burn`, `heatBand`; at most 2^7 corners). The `--genes` flag of `npm run role:budget` takes `none` (default), `best-vs-worst` (the performer at its best corner against the pressure at its worst), `worst-vs-best` and `corners` (the minimum and maximum slack of every cell and the corner that gives each). "The best defender against the worst predator" is `--genes best-vs-worst --rows defender:predator`.
