# Natural selection

**Status:** In progress - the big bang is merged (PR #217) and Phase 4's instruments, checks and speed chunks are merged (#220, #221, #222). Its screens (E81, E82, E85 to E87) found no predator, life-history or space value that keeps hunters to the end, and the family gate could not pass on maternal lines, so balancing pauses (user, 2026-10-31): the shark rule and the clonal cap are on by default, families are counted as genetic clusters, the step budget is 3.32 ms, and Phase 4 reopens after [flora.md](flora.md) and [megafauna.md](megafauna.md) land, starting with the predator balance and E88.
**Validated:** 7396097 (2026-11-01)
**Priority:** 1 - the default world fails the principles (spinning, a scout monoculture, lucky lives that breed, dead genomes that return), and one big-bang build with every change on by default avoids flags, dual paths and a re-pin per tuning round (user, 2026-10-29)
**Depends on:** [fitness-and-selection.md](fitness-and-selection.md) (Phases 1 to 3 built, T48h and T48i built)

Right now a lucky life can still breed and a dead failure can still come back. [principles.md](../principles.md)
(version 2) states the rules: death is final, a lucky life does not found a line, strength is who remains after the bad
season, incompetence kills before fertility ranks, and a payoff must return to the actor's offspring. This doc builds
the changes that enforce them, and the detectors that show whether the scenes appear.

As planned, each change was a config key or policy option that defaulted to off, Phase 2 turned the chosen set on in one
PR, and Phase 3 attributed the effect of each change with leave-one-out arms. The big bang (below) replaced that: every
change is the default, with no flag and no old path, and the attribution arms are not run. Where this doc still says
"default off", "flag" or names an arm, read it as the plan the big bang overrode.

Out of scope: new specialisation types (the five stay; only their budgets change), new sensors, new brain layouts beyond `pooled-v2`, the brain experiments
([brain-capacity-and-memory.md](brain-capacity-and-memory.md) follows this doc), and any reward for a scene
(principles.md, "Measure scenes, reward them almost never").

### The proof rule

An agent is **proven** when its own `deliberateIntake` reaches `splitDeliberateMin` and its deliberate meal share
reaches `splitDeliberateShareMin` (the split gate, without the population bypass). It is evaluated each time a meal is
credited, so it can happen at any age. A genome is **established** when its bearer is proven and at least one child of
that bearer is proven too: the skill was repeated without anyone paying for it. Rule 2 applies to every birth:

- **Split and mate children both count.** For a mate birth, the child counts for both parents, so both lines may become
  established by it.
- **Archive (`archive`).** A death enters the archive only if the dead genome is established. A parent that dies before
  its child is proven is held in a pending table (its death-log row and genome, at most `maxAgents` entries) and
  archived when the child proves; the entry is dropped when the child dies unproven.
- **Split brake (`split`).** A proven parent with two children alive that are not yet proven (from splits or mate births)
  cannot split: the first unproven child does not brake it, so one lucky parent can try a second, and the second does. A
  child that dies releases the parent at once. Mating itself is not blocked.
- **Both rules are always on.** The key `establishedRule` and its `off`, `archive` and `split` values were never kept: the big bang built `both` as the only behaviour.

Per-slot arrays `proven` and `unprovenKids`, the parent genome ids and the pending table are derived state beside the
split-gate counters, kept always (the detectors read them): zeroed by `freeAgent`, outside `worldHash`, no RNG, no allocation per tick.

### The E65 prediction

Recorded by T16 before the run, with these parts fixed here:

- **Floor episodes and the split brake.** The brake lets a proven parent split once more before it holds. If `all-on`
  exceeds `eval.floor_episodes` 3 per seed and `no-proof` does not, the response is to loosen the brake (a wider count of
  unproven children, or release when the first child is half-proven), never to restore the floor or the timers.
- **Heritability.** Mutation is dense (every weight is kicked at every birth, `mutationRate` 1), which may cap
  `eval.skill_heritability` however well selection works. A heritability miss routes to the brain plan's mutation
  experiment `E50` and is not a reason to revert `establishedRule`, `mateRank` or `pooled-v2`.
- **Alarms.** `eval.alarm_rate` falls well under `E64`'s 6 to 8 per 1000 agent-ticks, possibly under the 0.5 floor of
  the Done table; precision and response are read only above it.
- **Monoculture.** Mate rank does not fix it: splits sustain a monoculture whatever mates are chosen. Expected movers
  are the economy and the floor guard; `eval.mate_skill_gap` is expected positive and `eval.spec_dominance` is not
  predicted to fall because of the rank.
- **Budgets and world levers.** `no-spec-budgets` is expected to raise `eval.spec_dominance` (scouts return) and lower
  `eval.spec_effective`; `no-lifespan` to raise `eval.births_per_1k` and lower `eval.fed_median_lifespan`; `no-cover` to
  lower `eval.refuge_use_under_threat`; `no-gaps` to raise `eval.gap_crossers`, since the gaps are wider. The narrower

- **Behaviour axis.** `no-descriptor-v7` is expected to show a lower `eval.style_separation` and a lower

**The clonal limits (user, 2026-10-30).** `E81` found splits are 40% of births (8 to 78% per seed), surging on the seeds where hunters boomed and then vanished: a predator splits at 540 energy and one kill pays `predatorKillEnergy` 600, and a split child may split again once it earns its own `splitDeliberateMin`, so a clonal line never has to mate. Nature runs a few clonal generations and then goes sexual (aphids, water fleas); a line that never mates carries its mutations forward (Muller's ratchet). `splitClonalMax` caps the asexual generations in a row, `splitMaxPerLife` caps a life's splits, and the predator's `splitMinEnergyMultiplier` of 1 puts its split above one kill's meal. `E85` screens them, alone and together, against the defaults; each is off by default until it is adopted.

**Riding along.** The group-hunt probe runs with `wallDamage` 0 or food in its arena, so the prey no longer dies on the wall and an idle hunter does not win. `pearson` returns NaN for a series that is constant to within rounding.
