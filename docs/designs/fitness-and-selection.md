# Fitness and selection

**Status:** In progress - Phases 1 and 2 built: the metric audit and new metrics (E35), the predator fix (E36, E47) and in-world merit (off by default); Phases 3-4 (`fitnessV6`, the archive role, tuning) are still to build.
**Validated:** ed6dd9c (2026-10-23)
**Priority:** 1 - the worlds sit in a scout monoculture where behaviour stops improving, and every later tuning night, the trainer and the champions measure a fitness this doc replaces
**Depends on:** [world-balance.md](world-balance.md) (Phase 1, built), [evolution-evals.md](evolution-evals.md), [evolution-rework.md](evolution-rework.md)

Blobs reward luck. In the current worlds 88% of food is eaten by chance contact, population sits at the cap, one
specialisation holds the world, predators are extinct, and fitness reaches the world only through a trickle of archive
immigrants. This doc replaces the score and the way it feeds back into selection.

- A per-agent **merit** score is computed in `js/sim/` from sim state only. It weights mate choice and the energy a child is born with, and gates splitting.
- **`fitnessV6`** in `js/evo/` rewards four things: skill over luck, lineage success, diversity and watchability.
- The **archive** takes a larger role: more frequent immigrants, ranked by `fitnessV6`, placed by descriptors that include behaviour and body plan.
- The eval metrics are **audited** against their definitions first, and every term of the fitness is tied to a metric that can show it working.

Scope: the fitness score and how it feeds back into selection. The world rules (food, terrain, eras, combat) stay as
built, with one exception: Phase 2 diagnoses why predators go extinct and changes the world rule that keeps a minimum predator presence alive. The scout monoculture is judged as an outcome, not edited directly.

This doc replaces predator-prey.md Phase 3 (tasks T20 to T26 there; see Supersession below).

## Metric audit rules

Phase 1 checks every `eval.*` metric in the Metrics table of [evolution-evals.md](evolution-evals.md) against its
listed definition.

- Each metric gets a test that builds the smallest event sequence or world state whose value is computable by hand, and asserts the metric equals it. A `null` metric also gets a test of the exact condition and `reason` that makes it `null`.
- A mismatch is a bug. The fix starts with the failing test, lands in the audit task's commit and is recorded in [sim-notes.md](sim-notes.md).
- A metric that is `null` on every seed of a real run gets a diagnosis: the hand-built case shows a non-`null` value (the metric works and the behaviour is absent) or it does not (a bug). `eval.pack_convergence`, which is `null` on 16 of 16 baseline seeds, is the first case: the audit shows which of two causes applies: no pack kill occurs (`packKills` is 0 once predators are extinct) or `intentMetrics` miscounts.
- The audit tests live in `test/evo/metric-audit-core.test.js`, `metric-audit-combat.test.js`, `metric-audit-intent.test.js` and `metric-audit-selection.test.js`.

## In-world merit

Merit is a per-slot running score in `js/sim/`. It is a pure function of sim state: no RNG, no wall clock and no
observer input. It resets when a slot is allocated.

```
merit = DELIBERATE x deliberateIntake + meritChanceWeight x chanceIntake
        + meritMateBonus x matings + meritChildBonus x maturedChildren + meritEscapeBonus x escapes
```

`DELIBERATE` is 1. Intake is the energy from food and bites, classified at the moment of the meal or bite by the
tracker in `merit-state.js`.

| Term               | Credited when                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Function        |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| `deliberateIntake` | A meal or bite lands while the tracker is deliberate: a sighting on a food (or, for `canHunt`, prey) ray within `INTENT_WINDOW` (30) ticks, at least `CLOSING_MIN` (6) of the last `CLOSING_BITS` (10) ticks closing, and the agent turned towards it within the window or never saw it off the dead-ahead ray (the rules of `isDeliberate`) | `creditIntake`  |
| `chanceIntake`     | A meal or bite lands while the tracker is not deliberate                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | `creditIntake`  |
| `matings`          | `startMating` runs: both parents are credited                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | `creditMating`  |
| `maturedChildren`  | A child's `ageTicks` reaches `maturityTicks`: its living mother and father (found through `parentSlotA` and `parentSlotB`, valid while the slot's `genomeId` equals the child's `parentA` and `parentB`) are credited once                                                                                                                   | `creditMatured` |
| `escapes`          | An agent bitten by a `canHunt` agent is alive `ESCAPE_TICKS` (300) after the first bite of the episode and its speed exceeded `FLEE_SPEED` x `maxVelocity` within `RESPONSE_WINDOW` (30) ticks of it                                                                                                                                         | `creditEscape`  |

Merit decays with half-life `meritHalfLife` ticks, applied lazily: each credit and each read multiplies by
`0.5 ^ ((tick - stamp) / meritHalfLife)` through `detExp` and restamps. `meritNow(world, slot)` is the decayed value.

Signalling credits (alarm, hunt call) and waste charges need the observer's call queues and stay in `fitnessV6`; merit
holds only the terms that are O(1) per event on sim state.

### Mate choice

With `sexesEnabled` and `meritEnabled`, `bestInCell` ranks candidate males by
`mateScore = (1 - meritMateWeight) x energy / maxEnergy + meritMateWeight x m` (`m` as in Birth energy) (ties to the lower slot). `meritMateWeight`
0 reproduces the energy rule exactly. The sexes-off `findMate` is unchanged.

### Birth energy

`giveBirth` multiplies the gift of a mating birth (`childShareOf` x the mother's energy) and of a split (half the
mother's energy) by `birthFactor = 1 + meritBirthBoost x (2m - 1)`, where `m = merit / (merit + meritHalf)`, and caps the
gift at 0.9 of the mother's energy. The `childEnergyMin` check applies to the scaled gift. `meritBirthBoost` 0 reproduces
the current gift exactly.

### Split gate

`readyToSplit` adds `splitGateOpen`, true when `meritEnabled` is off, `splitDeliberateMin` is 0, the population is below
`meritSplitFloorPop`, or `deliberateIntake[slot] >= splitDeliberateMin`. Mating is not gated. A blocked split costs
nothing and the agent stays ready; a child starts with 0 deliberate intake and must forage on purpose before it splits; `eval.split_gated_share` counts it.

### Config

All keys are in `createConfig`, `config-validate.js` (through `FEATURE_RULES` in `config-rules.js`) and `rulesHash`.
Defaults are the Phase 2 values (the feature off); T48b sets the tuned values.

| Key                  | Type | Default | Bound                                                    | Meaning                                                                |
| -------------------- | ---- | ------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `meritEnabled`       | bool | false   | -                                                        | Master switch: no tracking, no credit, every rule below inactive       |
| `meritChanceWeight`  | num  | 0.25    | 0 to 1                                                   | Weight of chance intake against deliberate intake                      |
| `meritMateBonus`     | num  | 150     | at least 0                                               | Merit per mating                                                       |
| `meritChildBonus`    | num  | 300     | at least 0                                               | Merit per child that reaches maturity                                  |
| `meritEscapeBonus`   | num  | 60      | at least 0                                               | Merit per escape                                                       |
| `meritHalfLife`      | int  | 20000   | at least 0 (0 is no decay)                               | Ticks for merit to halve                                               |
| `meritHalf`          | num  | 1000    | above 0                                                  | Merit at which `m` is 0.5                                              |
| `meritMateWeight`    | num  | 0       | 0 to 1                                                   | Share of the mate score taken from merit                               |
| `meritBirthBoost`    | num  | 0       | 0 to 0.5                                                 | Swing of the birth gift: 1 - boost at merit 0, 1 + boost at high merit |
| `splitDeliberateMin` | num  | 0       | at least 0                                               | Deliberate intake a blob needs before it may split; 0 is no gate       |
| `meritSplitFloorPop` | int  | 80      | at least 0 (at or above `maxAgents` the gate stays open) | Below this population the split gate is open                           |

`FEATURES_OFF` pins `meritEnabled` false and the other keys at the defaults above; `FEATURES_ON` restates the tuned
values (a key pinned off and retuned must be restated, [sim-notes.md](sim-notes.md)).

## Fitness v6

`fitnessV6(observer, counters, slot, config)` (`fitnessName` `v6`) is computed at death and for the living at the end,
as `fitnessV1` is. It has four groups, each term tied to a metric that shows it working.

```
intake   = deliberate + CHANCE_WEIGHT x chance
gate     = min(1, deliberate / GATE)
skill    = intake + gate x (survival + role + credits)
positive = skill + BORN x children + gate x (lineage + watch)
fitness  = (positive - min(waste, WASTE_CAP x positive)) x diversityFactor
```

`deliberate` is deliberate food plus deliberate bite energy, `chance` the chance food plus chance bite energy (the
intent counters). `survival = SURVIVAL_RATE x min(age, SURVIVAL_CAP, SURVIVAL_PER_DELIBERATE x deliberate)`. `role` is
the role term of the table, capped at `intake`. `credits` is the sum of the four credits, each capped at
`CREDIT_CAP x intake`. `waste = IDLE_SIGNAL x idleSignal + UNDEFENDED x undefendedBite`. `positive` is never negative, so
`fitnessV6` is at least 0. The terms derive from `fitnessV2` of draft PR 120.

| Group     | Term                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Value                                              | Metric it moves                                                                                                     |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Skill     | Deliberate intake in full, chance intake at `CHANCE_WEIGHT`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | `deliberate + CHANCE_WEIGHT x chance`              | `eval.deliberate_food_share`, `eval.deliberate_food_trend`, `eval.pursuit_bite_share`                               |
| Skill     | Survival, ramped by the gate and capped at `SURVIVAL_PER_DELIBERATE` x deliberate                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | `SURVIVAL_RATE x min(age, SURVIVAL_CAP, ...)`      | `eval.fed_median_lifespan`                                                                                          |
| Skill     | Role term: forager `deliberateFood`, predator `deliberateKills`, reproducer `children`, scout `cells300`, defender `kinBitesAbsorbed`, capped at `intake`                                                                                                                                                                       | the `fitnessV1` weights                            | `eval.pursuit_kill_share`, `eval.catch_rate`                                                                        |
| Skill     | Escape, converged kill, alarm and hunt-call credits, each capped at `CREDIT_CAP x intake`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | `ESCAPE`, `CONVERGED_KILL`, `ALARM`, `HUNT_CALL`   | `eval.evasion_skill`, `eval.evasion_trend`, `eval.pack_convergence`, `eval.hunt_call_answer`, `eval.alarm_response` |
| Charge    | Waste: idle calls and smoke, undefended bites                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | `IDLE_SIGNAL`, `UNDEFENDED`, capped at `WASTE_CAP` | `eval.idle_signals_per_1k`, `eval.undefended_bite_share`                                                            |
| Base      | Born children, ungated                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | `BORN x children`                                  | `eval.births_per_1k`                                                                                                |
| Lineage   | Matings                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | `MATING x matings`                                 | `eval.mating_share`                                                                                                 |
| Lineage   | Children that reached maturity                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | `MATURED x maturedChildren`                        | `eval.child_survival`, `eval.offspring_per_life`                                                                    |
| Lineage   | Family longevity: descendants alive at the agent's death                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | `FAMILY x min(descendantsAlive, FAMILY_CAP)`       | `eval.lineage_lifespan_p50`, `eval.lineages_alive`                                                                  |
| Watch     | Chase: ticks inside a deliberate-bite chain                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | `CHASE x min(chaseTicks, CHASE_CAP)`               | `eval.pursuit_bite_share`, `eval.catch_rate`                                                                        |
| Watch     | Pack: kin within `callRadius` of a converged kill (the killer and the previous biter are paid by the skill credit)                                                                                                                                                                                                              | `PACK x packKills`                                 | `eval.pack_convergence`, `eval.pack_kill_share`                                                                     |
| Watch     | Migration: distinct food patches visited                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | `MIGRATE x min(patchesVisited, MIGRATE_CAP)`       | `eval.migration_index`                                                                                              |
| Diversity | `diversityFactor = 1 + DIVERSITY x (1 - niche share x niche count)`, clamped to `[1 - DIVERSITY / 2, 1 + DIVERSITY]`; the niche is the specialisation and the tercile of the size trait (cut points 0.9 and 1.1), 15 niches (5 with `traitsEnabled` off); the niche share is its share of alive agents at the latest checkpoint | a rare niche pays up to `1 + DIVERSITY`            | `eval.spec_dominance`, `eval.spec_effective`, `eval.body_plan_coverage`, `eval.trait_spread`                        |

Temperature, terrain and era adaptation have no fitness term: they act through survival, which the skill group
already pays, and are read from `eval.cold_stress_share`, `eval.terrain_death_share` and `eval.winter_pop_ratio`. The
arms race (`eval.pred_prey_lag_corr`) is read from the metric; no per-agent term pays it.

| ------------------------- | ----- | ------------- | ----- |

| `GATE`                    | 500   | `MATING`      | 100   |

intake only.
