# Worker and renderer

**Status:** In progress - Phase 1 built: the sim core and the live policy run in a Web Worker behind a validated message protocol; Phases 2 (renderer core) and 3 (interface) code merged and conformance-reviewed (T19, T27), awaiting their dev-machine budget measurements and manual walks; Phase 4 wake lock (T32), generative audio (T31), screenshot/clip capture (T55), director moments/captions (T28) and director shot planning/state machine (T29) built; Phase 6 (Look: tasks T57–T72, T74 and T75) built and reviewed in a browser: rock obstacles, aurora background, terrain frost and refuge glows, agent motion cues, courtship threads, carcass decay, finish layer and click opens inspector; T73's `--look` flag is built but its per-item cost table and `--look all` run on the dev machine's GPU are still to do.
**Validated:** 1f2a4e7 (2026-10-29)
**Priority:** 8 - comes before the trainer so world tuning can be judged by watching it, not only by scorecards (user, 2026-10-16)
**Depends on:** [headless-sim-core.md](headless-sim-core.md), [evolution-rework.md](evolution-rework.md), [predator-prey.md](predator-prey.md), [body-temperature.md](body-temperature.md), [dev-workflow-and-gate.md](dev-workflow-and-gate.md), [quality-gates-and-test-tiers.md](quality-gates-and-test-tiers.md)

The browser app runs the simulation in a dedicated worker and only draws on the main thread. It runs unattended on a TV
for hours:

- The worker owns the world, the live policy (`LIVE_POLICY`), the observer and the archive. It steps on a fixed
  timestep and posts snapshots as transferable buffers. There is no `SharedArrayBuffer`.
- The main thread interpolates between the two newest snapshots and draws at the display's refresh rate with
  `three` (WebGL2): instanced meshes, custom shader materials and bloom. Draw code allocates nothing per frame.
- The look is a bioluminescent abyss: a near-black background with a very dim, slowly moving aurora, rock outcrops with glowing lichen, and agents that glow like deep-sea life.
  Shape is the specialisation, colour is the lineage (the family) and brightness is energy.
- The HUD is a thin top bar and an event ticker. Charts and the inspector open on demand. After 30 s without input the
  HUD fades in every mode, with the director on or off; after 10 s without input the director (when on) takes the camera.
- A first-time viewer gets a title card with a legend, a plain-language status line and a hover peek; the director shows
  moments that the worker's deliberate-behaviour signals confirmed, not chance contacts.
- Sound is optional and generative, off by default.
- It replaces the legacy app (`js/*.js`), deleted in Phase 5, and the defects recorded in [legacy-audit.md](legacy-audit.md) under
  LOOP-1, LOOP-2, LIFE-4, REN-1 to REN-4, SEL-6, DET-2, DET-3 and DET-5.

Target displays are two 16:9 monitors: the 4K 240 Hz OLED (3840x2160, the main one) and a 2560x1440. Static bright UI on an OLED panel is a burn-in risk, so the HUD rules in Interface apply in every mode. The world is
16:9 at any size (7680x4320, 11520x6480 or 14400x8100); the renderer reads the size from the worker and nothing in
`js/app/` names a world size.

## Look

All colours are linear RGB values written once in `colors.js` (`PALETTE`, `FAMILY_PALETTES`); the bloom pass makes values above 1 glow. The three scent hazes, the food teal and the alarm and eaten reds are the semantic colours; no family slot, and none of the Phase 6 scenery colours (aurora, lichen, frost, refuge glow), comes within the distances of the Interface colour test of any of them. **Scenery stays below the agents:** the aurora's luminance ceiling `AURORA_MAX_LUMINANCE` is 0.010 (linear Y), at most half the dimmest agent body (about 0.04: the neutral family at zero energy, brightness 0.7, body fill 0.5 in the middle), and every other scenery colour peaks at `SCENERY_MAX_LUMINANCE` 0.012 or less, so the aurora and the strongest scenery layer together stay at or under 0.022, below the dimmest agent body (smoke can dim a body further; that is the intended look); all sit below the bloom threshold (0.85), so scenery is never lifted.

**Legibility.** `packAgents` writes an agent's drawn radius as `max(radius, minPx / (2 x zoom))`, with `zoom` in device px per world unit, `minPx` 24 for a hunter (a specialisation with `canHunt`) and 20 for the rest; `pickAgent` tests against the same radius. Below 24 device px across, the halo is 4 px, the nucleus glow is off and a hunter gets a hot white-red tip at the nose. At the fit zoom on a 3840 or a 2560 wide canvas every hunter is at least 24 device px across.

## Worker loop

- **Buffers.** The worker allocates the snapshot buffers (`SNAPSHOT_POOL_SIZE`: three, four from Phase 4) of `workerLayout(config).bytes` (the sim parts from
  `snapshotLayout(config)` plus the effects and inspect parts) and two field buffers at `init`. After a slice that ran a
  tick (from Phase 4: after each tick inside a slice), and only when `snapshotDue` says at least 8 ms of wall time have passed since the last snapshot, it writes
  `writeSnapshot`, `writeEffects` and `writeInspect` into a buffer it holds and transfers it. The main thread keeps the
  previous and the newest snapshot; when a new one arrives it returns the previous one at once. So the worker always
  holds at least one buffer (two from Phase 4); if it holds none, it skips that snapshot, and the effects stay in the log for the next one. The client returns a field buffer as soon as `packField` has copied it into the texture, so the worker's two field buffers cycle and ten `field` messages in a row all arrive; a field posted with no buffer free is skipped and logged.
- **Effects.** After every tick `collectEffects` turns that tick's event records into effects and scans the alive slots:
  an agent with `bittenTimer` equal to 1 (as `updateLifeCounters` reads it) yields a `BITE` with `lastBiter` as the
  biter, and every alive slot's position is cached to give a later death, which carries none, a place. The log holds
  `EFFECT_CAPACITY` records; when it is full the oldest are dropped and counted in the effects header.
- **Pacing.** Each slice adds `(now - last) x ticksPerSecond / 1000` to the ticks owed, caps owed at
  `2 x maxTicksPerSlice` (counting the excess as dropped), and runs up to `maxTicksPerSlice` (8). `ticksPerSecond`
  is a request: stats report the actual rate, and the UI shows both.
- **Speed ladder.** One worker runs the bench world at 500 agents at 240 ticks/s (4.17 ms a tick; [sim-notes.md](sim-notes.md), 2026-10-16), and the host adds about 3%. `browser.worker_tps@500` records the live value.

| Request | Ticks/s | Tick budget | Sustained at 500 agents                               |
| ------- | ------- | ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| x1      | 60      | 16.7 ms     | Yes                                                   |
| x2      | 120     | 8.3 ms      | Yes                                                   |
| x4      | 240     | 4.17 ms     | At the limit: held on a quiet machine, not under load |
| x8      | 480     | 2.08 ms     | No: about 240 ticks/s, half the request               |
| x16     | 960     | 1.04 ms     | No: about 240 ticks/s, a quarter of the request       |

The top bar shows the actual rate beside the request, so the shortfall is visible. The worker cost scales with population, so x8 holds at about 250 agents and x16 at about 125 (estimated from the per-agent step cost).

- **Seeking (Phase 4).** `seek` runs the pacer's owed ticks as unlimited: slices of `maxTicksPerSlice` back to back through the `MessageChannel`, a snapshot at most every 250 ms, `stats.seekTarget` set to the target tick until it is reached. At the target the host pauses and posts a snapshot. `seek` to a tick at or below the current one calls `reset` with the same seed first. Any `reset`, `pause` or `resume` ends a seek. The world after a seek is the world the same ticks would give at any speed.
- **Scheduling.** While ticks are owed, the next slice is posted through a `MessageChannel`; when none are owed, it
  is a `setTimeout` for the time until one is due; while paused, nothing is scheduled.
- **Errors.** On an exception the host posts `error`, logs `WorkerError` and stops stepping.
- **Selection.** `select` finds the slot through the observer's `slotOf`; the inspect part is written with each
  snapshot while the agent is alive. When it dies the host posts `selection` with `gone` and clears it. While paused, `select` and `setLenses` each write and post a fresh snapshot so the inspector and the ray fans update. After every stats message `drainObserverLogs` resets the observer's death and checkpoint logs, so no structure in the worker grows with run length.

| Stat                                                                | Holds                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `tick`, `ticksPerSecond`, `requestedTicksPerSecond`, `droppedTicks` | Progress and pace                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| `season`, `seasonProgress`                                          | `world.season[0]` and the position within the season, 0 to 1                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| `population`, `bySpec`                                              | Counts                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `predators`, `callsPerKiloTick`, `largestSpec`                      | T15j: alive agents whose specialisation has `canHunt`; `AlarmCalled` plus `PackCalled` events over the last 1000 ticks, per 1000 ticks (a ring of 1000 counters, smoke not counted); `{ index, share }` of the most common specialisation among alive agents, share 0 when none are alive |
| `bornShare`                                                         | `(mate + split) / all` over `observer.birthsAfterBurnIn`, as `eval.born_share`; `null` before any entry after burn-in                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `births`, `birthsByCause`, `deaths`, `deathsByCause`                | Since the last stats message                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |

## Renderer

- `three` is upgraded to its current release in T11 and pinned exactly; the version and API changes go into
  [sim-notes.md](sim-notes.md). Bloom comes from `three/examples/jsm/postprocessing`; no other graphics package is
  added.
- **Size and pixels.** The canvas is the `devicePixelContentBoxSize` of its `ResizeObserver` entry (else
  `clientWidth x devicePixelRatio`, rounded), with the ratio capped at 2. `fitZoom(canvas, world)` is
  `min(canvasWidth / worldWidth, canvasHeight / worldHeight)`: on 16:9 it fills the canvas with no letterbox.
  `snapZoom(zoom)` moves a settled zoom to the nearest 1/k or k (k an integer) within 2% of it, which puts a 7680 or
  11520 wide world on a 3840 canvas on whole pixels; other sizes keep the exact fit.
- **Layer order**, back to front (Phase 6 adds the aurora first, the terrain after the haze, the rock rim pass before the rock body pass and the mate threads after the sparks): aurora, territory, haze, terrain, food, obstacles, agents (five meshes), vision fans, smoke, call
  rings, sparks, overlays; then bloom over the frame; then the ambient motes; then the finish quad. Before the agents, the smoke layer renders each cloud's alpha into a quarter-resolution target; the agent shader multiplies an agent's brightness by `1 - smokeAlpha` at its position, so smoke dims the glow before the bloom threshold. The puffs then draw with normal blending over the agents.
- **Agents.** `familyColor` (slots from `createFamilySlots`, reassigned at each `stats`) and `energy / maxEnergy` fill the instance colour; `packAgents` applies the Legibility radius; the shader reads `temperature` for the
  temperature lens and `flags` for the jaw wedge and the ate and bitten flashes. `interpolateInto(prev, next, alpha,
out)` matches agents by slot and `genomeId` through a preallocated slot map, blends positions, `size`, energy,
  temperature and angle (shortest arc), and places new agents at their newest position. `alpha` is `(playhead - prev.tick) / (next.tick - prev.tick)`, clamped to 0..1. The playhead is a render tick, not a wall-clock arrival time: `stepPlayhead` advances it each frame by the frame time times the tick rate estimated from the last 8 snapshots' tick deltas against wall time, and pulls it by 10% of the error per frame towards `latest.tick` minus the mean snapshot tick delta, clamped between `prev.tick` and `next.tick`. With one snapshot, or after no snapshot for 250 ms (paused), the playhead is the latest tick and `alpha` is 1. `simTickAt(playhead)` is the tick that effects, rings and sparks are timed by.
- **Haze.** `packField` converts the three grids of a `field` message into the half-float texels of a `cols x rows`
  texture; the layer keeps the previous and newest texture and the shader cross-fades them over 250 ms. Each channel
  maps through `1 - exp(-1.6 x value / pheromoneMax)` to a veil of at most 0.018 of its colour in linear light, about 15% once encoded (0.07 under the lens), added to the frame. The shader
  displaces the sample point with two octaves of value noise moving at 6 world units per second.
- **Smoke.** A cloud's `life` is its third snapshot word. `smokeShape(life)` gives `age = 1 - life`,
  `radiusScale = 0.5 + 0.5 x easeOut(min(age / 0.25, 1))` and `alpha = 0.55 x (1 - smoothstep(0.55, 1, age))`. The
  puff is three noise-warped lobes inside `smokeRadius x radiusScale`.
- **Rings.** `playEffects` spawns a ring for `ALARM` and `PACK_CALL`. `worker-client.js` appends each snapshot's effects to a queue (2048 records) when the message arrives, so effects are not read from the two held snapshots and none is lost when several snapshots arrive between frames. A ring's age is `playhead - effect.tick` over `callTicks`, so rings freeze when the sim is paused. `ringAt('alarm', age, callRadius)` is radius
  `callRadius x easeOut(age)`, alpha `0.35 x (1 - age)^2` and width 1.5 device px. `ringAt('hunt', age, callRadius)` is
  radius `callRadius x (0.25 + 0.35 x fract(3 x age))`, alpha `0.35 x (1 - age) x (0.6 + 0.4 x sin(6 PI x age))` and
  width 2 device px. The pool recycles its oldest ring when full.
- **Sparks.** A `BITE` spawns 8 streaks at the biter's jaw point (the biter's interpolated position plus 0.9 x its
  radius along its heading; the victim's position when the biter is gone) lasting 12 ticks. A `BORN` spawns a ring and 6
  motes in the child's colour lasting 30 ticks. A `DIED` spawns 10 motes lasting 40 ticks, plus a 6-tick red flash when
  the cause is `eaten`.
- **Food and obstacles** are one `InstancedMesh` each from the snapshot, with `count` set per frame. The haze is one
  world quad.
- **Picking.** `pickAgent` tests the click against interpolated positions and sizes (with a tolerance of 12 device px
  in world units) and returns the nearest agent's `genomeId`.
- **Trails** keep the last 32 positions of up to 64 agents (the selected agent and the leading lineage's) in a
  preallocated ring; the follow marker is a thin ring around the followed agent.
- **Quality.** `stepQuality` reads `frameReport`, whose budget is the measured refresh interval (`refreshHz` from `__perf`): after 5 s with p95 above 1.5 x the budget it moves one level down, after 30 s below 0.6 x the budget one level up. Intervals recorded while the tab is hidden and intervals over 250 ms are ignored. After two reversals within 10 minutes the wait before stepping up doubles each time, to at most 10 minutes. Level 0 is everything on; level 1 halves the bloom resolution and
  the motes and applies the level-1 column of the Phase 6 table below; level 2 turns the bloom and the motes off and applies its level-2 column. `?quality=<n>` pins a level; the perf script pins 0. `?scale=<0.25 to 1>` pins the render scale and overrides `SOFTWARE_RENDER_SCALE`; the screenshot script pins 1.
- `index.html` keeps `#canvas-container` as the canvas parent. The worker starts with
  `new Worker(new URL('../worker/sim-worker.js', import.meta.url), { type: 'module' })`, and `vite.config.js` sets
  `worker.format` to `es`.
- In dev builds `window.__perf()` returns `{ app: 'v6', refreshHz, frameMs: { p50, p95, p99 }, drawMs: { p50, p95, p99 },
gpuMs: { p50, p95, p99 } | null, frames, agents, ticksPerSecond, droppedTicks, heapMb, gpu: { geometries, textures, programs } }`,
  where a frame is the interval between `requestAnimationFrame` callbacks, `frames` is how many intervals the
  percentiles cover, `drawMs` is the time spent in the callback, `gpuMs` is the GPU time from the timer query (`null`
  without it), `agents` is the worker's population and `gpu` is `renderer.info`.

## Director (Phase 4)

The director runs on the main thread, owns the camera while it is active and never sends a command that changes the
world. Its random draws come from `createPrng(seed xor 0x9e3779b9)`.

**Idle.** `createIdleTracker` (`js/app/idle.js`) records the time of the last input. After 10 s idle, with the director on, the cursor hides, the selection clears and the director starts; any input returns the camera at once, restores the viewer's speed and restarts the idle clock. The HUD fade is the 30 s rule in Interface and does not depend on the director. `T` starts the director and hides the HUD without waiting. In TV mode the world border is off. `?tv=1` starts in TV mode with the wake lock on. For a TV, launch Chrome fullscreen: `chrome --kiosk "http://localhost:5173/?tv=1"` (`--start-fullscreen` keeps the address bar reachable); `Shift+F` toggles fullscreen in a normal window.

**Moments.** Every 500 ms `findMoments` scores candidates from the newest snapshot, the effects of the last 5 simulated seconds (ticks / 60) and the ticker. A moment of combat needs a deliberate signal from the worker, built by [world-balance.md](world-balance.md) Phase 1 (`lastBiteDeliberate`, the escape episode's `responded`, the closing and threat latches) and carried in the effect and flag bits of the Snapshot section; a chance contact scores below the 45 threshold.

| Kind            | Detected when                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Score                                                                                                                          | Focus                        |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------- |
| `chase`         | A hunter with the `hunting` flag has a smaller non-hunter inside its jaw arc (`biteArc`) within 400 world units, the gap shrank by at least 20% over the last 1.5 s of snapshots, and the prey's heading is within 90 degrees of the hunter-to-prey bearing | `40 + 30 x sprinting hunter + 20 x sprinting prey + 10 x (1 - distance / 400)` (sprinting: `speed` above 1.05 x `maxVelocity`) | The midpoint of the pair     |
| `pack-kill`     | A `DIED` effect with the pack bit and the deliberate bit within the last 4 s                                                                                                                                                                                | `90 x (1 - age / 4 s)`                                                                                                         | The death position           |
| `kill`          | A `DIED` effect of cause `eaten` within the last 4 s                                                                                                                                                                                                        | `80 x (1 - age / 4 s)` with the deliberate bit, else `30 x (1 - age / 4 s)`                                                    | The death position           |
| `escape`        | An `ESCAPED` effect with the responded bit within the last 5 s                                                                                                                                                                                              | `65 x (1 - age / 5 s)`                                                                                                         | The victim                   |
| `smoke-escape`  | An `ESCAPED` effect with the responded and in-smoke bits within the last 5 s                                                                                                                                                                                | `85 x (1 - age / 5 s)`                                                                                                         | The victim                   |
| `smoke-screen`  | A `SMOKE` effect with the was-bitten bit within the last 5 s                                                                                                                                                                                                | `55 x (1 - age / 5 s)`                                                                                                         | The cloud                    |
| `alarm-chorus`  | At least 3 `ALARM` effects within 800 world units in the last 3 s                                                                                                                                                                                           | `50 + 5 x calls`, at most 70                                                                                                   | The centroid of the callers  |
| `birth-cluster` | At least 6 `BORN` effects within 600 world units in the last 4 s                                                                                                                                                                                            | `35 + 5 x births`, at most 55                                                                                                  | The centroid of the births   |
| `family-lead`   | A `new-leader` ticker item within the last 10 s                                                                                                                                                                                                             | 60                                                                                                                             | The centroid of that lineage |
| `extinct`       | An `extinct` ticker item within the last 10 s                                                                                                                                                                                                               | 75                                                                                                                             | The item's `x` and `y`       |
| `last-stand`    | A `last-stand` ticker item within the last 10 s                                                                                                                                                                                                             | 70                                                                                                                             | The item's `x` and `y`       |

Each score is multiplied by 0.6 when the same kind was shown in the last 30 s. The director takes the highest score plus a draw of up to 5, and acts only above 45.

**Shots.** The state machine runs `wide`, `glide`, `linger`, `pullback`, then `wide` again:

| State      | Camera                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Ends                                                                                                                                 |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `wide`     | The zoom that fits the box around the middle 80% of agents in x and in y (the 10th to 90th percentile, padded 10%, never below the fit zoom) x (1 to 1.15), the centre drifting on a Lissajous path of 3% of the world width with periods of 90 s and 140 s       | After 8 s at the earliest, when a moment scores above 45; else after a random 20 to 40 s                                             |
| `glide`    | Smootherstep from the current pose to the moment's pose in 3 s (2 s for `pack-kill` and `kill`); the zoom frames 1600 world units across for `chase`, `pack-kill`, `kill` and `smoke-screen`, 2400 for `alarm-chorus` and `birth-cluster`, 3600 for `family-lead` | On arrival                                                                                                                           |
| `linger`   | Follows the focus, found again at each decision, with `followStep` smoothing                                                                                                                                                                                      | After 8 s, extended by 4 s at a time up to 20 s while the moment's score stays at 45 or more; earlier when it stays under 30 for 2 s |
| `pullback` | Smootherstep back to the wide pose in 4 s                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | On arrival                                                                                                                           |

`captionFor` names the moment ("Chase - predator after forager", "Pack kill", "Kill", "Escape", "Smoke escape", "Smoke screen", "Alarm chorus", "Birth cluster of 6", "Velari takes the lead", "Velari has died out", "Last stand of Velari", "First pack kill", "Record hunt streak", "Oldest blob yet"); the caption fades in as `glide` starts and out as `pullback` starts. The director logs `DirectorShot` for each `glide`.

**Pacing (while idle).** Besides the viewer, the director is the only sender of `setSpeed`; it sends none while the viewer has input control, and the world is unchanged by it. At start it keeps the viewer's speed `s0`. `paceFor(state, s0, workerMsPerTick)` gives `wide` and `pullback` the speed `max(s0, 4)` capped by `maxSustainedSpeed(workerMsPerTick)` (the largest ladder speed whose tick budget, `1000 / (60 x speed)` ms, is at least 1.05 x `workerMsPerTick`), and gives `glide` and `linger` the speed `min(s0, 1)`. Any input restores `s0` at once. The top bar shows `auto` while the director sets the speed. A close shot frames 1600 units on 3840 px, about 2.4 px per unit, so a blob at `maxVelocity` crosses 1150 px per second at x1.

**Era card.** In TV mode, every 10 minutes of simulated time and outside a `linger`, `eraCardFor(history)` shows a 10 s card from `history.js`: the leading family and how many minutes it has led, the catch share (`caught / (caught + escaped)`) over the last 10 minutes against the 10 before, and `generationMax`.

**Measurement.** `npm run director:dry -- --seed <n> --ticks <t>` (default 72000 ticks, `--json`) runs `createSimHost` over a fake port in Node and feeds every snapshot, effect and ticker item to `findMoments` and the shot machine on a simulated clock (decisions every 500 ms, time is ticks / 60). It prints one line per shot (`tick`, `kind`, `score`, outcome) and three metrics per seed: `director.chase_precision` (the share of `chase` shots whose pair has a `BITE` or `ESCAPED` effect within the linger; at least 0.3), `director.max_kind_share` (the largest share of the shots one kind holds; at most 0.4) and `director.shots_per_hour` (shots per simulated hour; reported). Phase 4's done criteria are the first two at seeds 1 to 4.

## Audio (Phase 4)

Off by default. The `AudioContext` is created or resumed only on the first `keydown` or `pointerdown`, even when the stored preference or `?audio=1` says on; until then the top bar shows `audio: waiting for a key`, and afterwards `on`, `off` or `suspended`. `padParams(season, populationShare,
predatorShare)` returns the pad's root note (MIDI 50, 55, 45 and 40 for spring, summer, autumn and winter), low-pass
cutoff `300 + 1700 x populationShare` Hz, gain `0.05 + 0.1 x populationShare` and detune `12 x predatorShare` cents. The
pad is four slow triangle oscillators (root, fifth, octave, major tenth) through the filter; a season change
cross-fades to the new root over 8 s and a 0.05 Hz oscillator moves the cutoff by 15%.

| Cue (from an effect)    | Sound                                                                                        |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `BORN`                  | A soft sine pluck on the pentatonic note `genomeId mod 5` two octaves above the root, 400 ms |
| `DIED` of cause `eaten` | A muted sine thud falling 80 to 50 Hz, 250 ms                                                |
| `ALARM`                 | Two quick falling triangle blips, 660 to 520 Hz                                              |
| `PACK_CALL`             | A low pulse at the root, 300 ms                                                              |

`createCueLimiter` allows 6 cues a second and one cue per kind per 120 ms. A cue's stereo pan is its effect's x
position in the view, clamped to -0.8..0.8, and a cue outside the view plays at half gain. All sound goes through a
master gain (default 0.3) and a `DynamicsCompressorNode`. `mountAudio` suspends the context while the tab is hidden and

## Stability

- Draw code allocates nothing per frame; every layer preallocates its buffers for `maxAgents`, `maxFood`,
  the obstacle slot count and `maxClouds`, and exports `dispose`. Snapshots and fields reuse their pooled buffers.
- Every ring, pool, history and queue has a fixed bound, named in the Modules tables and the Interface section.
- The `uPlot` instances exist only while the charts drawer is open; closing it calls `destroy` on each.
- **Supervisor.** `createSupervisor` expects a `stats` message at least every 5 s while the sim is running. A missed
  heartbeat, an `error` message, an `error` or `messageerror` event on the worker and a message that fails validation
  each count as a failure. It terminates the worker and starts a new one with a fresh seed after 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 s; from the sixth failure within an hour it retries every 10 minutes and never gives up. Every restart shows a toast naming the new seed and keeps the last debug bundle for download. Until Phase 5 a restart loses the run and its archive; from Phase 5 the new worker loads the stored archive.
- **Context loss.** `createScene` prevents the default on `webglcontextlost`, stops drawing, and rebuilds every layer on
  `webglcontextrestored`.
- **Hidden tab.** `requestAnimationFrame` stops; the client keeps answering messages and returning buffers, the worker
  keeps stepping, and audio suspends.
- **Wake lock.** `createWakeLock` requests `navigator.wakeLock` for `screen` while enabled and the tab is visible,
  requests it again on `visibilitychange`, and ignores a refusal after logging it. It is off by default and on with `?tv=1`.

**1. Rock obstacles** (`rocks`).

- _Outline._ `rockShape(seed, slot, out)` fills three harmonic amplitudes, three phases and a lichen seed from `createPrng` seeded by the world seed and the obstacle slot index, so a rock keeps its shape for the life of the world (the slot, not the position, because a wandering obstacle moves). `rockOutline(shape, angle)` is the radius in units of the collision radius: `1 - 0.12 x (0.5 + 0.5 x (a1 sin(2 angle + p1) + a2 sin(3 angle + p2) + a3 sin(5 angle + p3)))` with the amplitudes summing to at most 1, so it stays between 0.88 and 1.0 and no drawn rock is larger than its collision circle. The vertex shader receives the shape as the instance attribute `iShape` (vec4 packed from the 7 values by `packRockShape`), built once per world and re-uploaded only when the slot count changes. The fragment shader evaluates the same harmonics.
- _Shading and lichen._ The body is `PALETTE.rockBody` darkened towards the middle and lit slightly towards the rim by a 2-octave world-space simplex term (`noise-glsl.js`). Lichen is a hash-cell speckle in the outer 25% of the radius: about 4% of cells light, each at `PALETTE.lichen` times 0.5 to 1.0, breathing on a 6 to 12 s sine offset by the cell hash, at most `SCENERY_MAX_LUMINANCE`. A soft contact ring of `PALETTE.rockContact` (near-black, alpha 0.5 falling to 0) reaches 1.08 radii.
- _One ridge._ The layer draws the instances twice: the rim and contact pass first (every rock's outline widened by the 2 px rim, plus the contact ring), then the body pass over it (the outline inset by the rim). Where two rocks overlap or touch, one rock's body covers its neighbour's rim, so the union of bodies has one rim around it and no line between rocks. Shading and lichen read world position, so they continue across the join.

Pure tests: `rockOutline` stays in 0.88 to 1.0 for 1000 seeds and 360 angles; `rockShape` is the same for the same seed and slot, differs between slots and seeds, and fills the array it is given; the amplitudes sum to at most 1.

**2. Aurora** (`aurora`).

- _Ribbons._ A layer first in the main scene. It renders 3 ribbons into a target `ceil(canvas / 4)` on a side (RGBA8; the values are tiny, so the target holds the ribbon intensity in the alpha-free R channel and the tint is applied at upsample). A ribbon is a vertical Gaussian band whose centre line follows a slow simplex noise of the world x coordinate, with a second noise for its brightness along its length. `auroraPhase(tSeconds, out)` returns the three noise time offsets, advancing at 0.011, 0.017 and 0.023 noise units per second, so the pattern does not repeat inside 10 minutes. `auroraGain(tSeconds)` wanders between 0.55 and 1.0 on a 40 s sine.
- _Upsample and dither._ One quad over the canvas samples the target bilinearly, multiplies by `auroraTint(season, seasonProgress)` and the gain, and adds the dither in linear light: `(interleavedNoise(gl_FragCoord.xy, frame) - 0.5)` times the size of one 8-bit sRGB step at that value, so banding breaks up after `OutputPass` encodes the frame. `interleavedNoise` is the finish noise (`finish-glsl.js`).
- _Ceiling._ The largest luminance the layer can output is `AURORA_MAX_LUMINANCE` (0.010): each tint stop is scaled to that luminance at gain 1. `colors.test.js` asserts the ceiling is at most half the dimmest agent body, and `AURORA_MAX_LUMINANCE + SCENERY_MAX_LUMINANCE` is below it, over the three palettes (computed from `NEUTRAL_FAMILY`, `ENERGY_FLOOR`, and the exported `BODY_CENTRE_FILL` 0.34 and `BRIGHTNESS_FLOOR` 0.7) and below `BLOOM.threshold`, so the bloom pass does not lift it.
- _Hue._ `AURORA` holds one stop per season (`spring`, `summer`, `autumn`, `winter`, indexed like `stats.season`). Starting values, in linear RGB before scaling to the ceiling: winter `0.08, 0.18, 0.55` (indigo-blue), spring `0.10, 0.30, 0.50` (steel blue), summer `0.34, 0.14, 0.48` (dusky plum), autumn `0.30, 0.26, 0.10` (dull gold). The final values are the ones that pass the test below, and the warmth test (red minus blue) compares summer against winter. `colors.test.js` measures each stop at its displayed (ceiling-scaled) colour and at unit lightness against every semantic colour with the existing `colorDistance`: at least 15 apart in normal vision and 10 under the three simulations at unit lightness, and a hue angle at least 25 degrees away from each semantic colour's.
- _Season._ `auroraTint(season, progress, out)` returns the current season's stop, blended towards the next season's by `smoothstep(0.7, 1, progress)`, so it is continuous across a season change and across the year. The red-minus-blue difference of the summer stop is above the winter stop's. `season` and `seasonProgress` come from the newest `stats` (`stats.season`, `stats.seasonProgress`); `Frame` gains both fields, written by the frame loop (0 and 0 until the first `stats`).
- _Never static._ The three phases advance every frame by wall time (not by the sim tick, so the aurora moves when the sim is paused). A test steps `auroraPhase` and the reference centre line over 60 s and asserts that every ribbon moves by at least 0.5% of the canvas height per second and that the gain differs by at least 0.1 between some two instants in any 40 s window.
- _Order._ First layer, before the territory, so everything else draws over it; it replaces the clear colour only where its value is above 0.

**3. Terrain** (`terrain`).

- _Data._ `ready.info.terrain` already carries `layout`, `coldMarginWidth`, `wallX`, `rockFirst` and `rockCount`; the rocks, wall gaps included, arrive as obstacles, so the wall and its gaps need nothing new. The refuges are not in `ready` and are not recoverable from `rockFirst`/`rockCount` without guessing, so `terrainInfo` gains `refuges: {x, y, radius}[]` (empty on `open`): the static rocks after the wall rocks come in groups of `REFUGE_ROCKS`, a refuge's centre is the mean of its group and its radius is the ring radius plus `REFUGE_ROCK_RADIUS`. This is an addition to `ready.info`; `ready.info` is validated only as an object, so `protocol.js` is unchanged; a test passes a `ready` carrying `refuges` through `validateMessage`, and `worldInfo`'s terrain test is updated.
- _Frost._ Two world-space quads, `y` from 0 to `coldMarginWidth` and from `worldHeight - coldMarginWidth` to `worldHeight`. The fragment reads a static 256 x 32 R8 tile made once by `frostTexture(seed, out)` (4 octaves of horizontally tileable value noise from `createPrng`), tiled along the band, and multiplies it by a profile `(1 - d / coldMarginWidth)^2` from the world edge `d`, a slow shimmer `0.85 + 0.15 sin(t / 7 + x / 90)` and `PALETTE.frost`, peaking at `SCENERY_MAX_LUMINANCE`.
- _Refuges._ One instanced quad per refuge, a radial falloff in `PALETTE.refugeGlow` breathing on an 8 s sine, peak 0.5 x `SCENERY_MAX_LUMINANCE` at the centre, 0 at the radius.
- Pure tests: `frostTexture` is deterministic per seed, tiles (the first and last columns differ by no more than neighbouring columns), and spans a useful range; `terrainInfo` refuges for hand-built basins configs (the count, the centres on the ring and the radius); an `open` layout draws nothing.

**4. Agent motion** (`motion`). `interpolateInto` already blends `speed` and keeps the previous and next `angle`. It gains `turn[i]`, the shortest-arc angle change per snapshot tick (0 for an agent new in this snapshot). `packAgents` writes a new instance attribute `iMotion` (vec4): `speed01` = `speed / maxVelocity` clamped to 0..1, `turn01` = `|turn| / TURN_FULL` clamped (`TURN_FULL` 0.2 rad per tick) smoothed at 25% per frame in a per-slot array, `phase` = `fract(genomeId x 0.6180339)` and 0. In `AGENT_FRAGMENT` the sample point `p` is displaced before the signed-distance call: `p.y += 0.08 x speed01 x sin(uTime x (4 + 10 x speed01) + 6.2831853 x phase + 2.5 x p.x) x smoothstep(0.2, -1.0, p.x)` (the rear half swings, the nose does not), then `p *= vec2(1.0 / (1.0 + 0.12 x turn01), 1.0 + 0.12 x turn01)` (squash along the heading, volume kept). The radius, `pickAgent` and the Legibility rule are unchanged. Uniform `uMotion` is 1 at level 0, 0.5 at level 1 (the wobble term is skipped) and 0 at level 2. Pure tests: `motionOf` clamps, the shortest arc across plus and minus pi, 25% smoothing converges, the phase is stable per `genomeId` and in 0..1, and the packed `iMotion` is the same array on a second call.

**5. Courtship thread** (`threads`).

- _Effect log._ A mating birth is the wrong cue: `BORN` carries only the child (`genomeId`, `lineageId`, `spec`, cause) and arrives `pregnancyTicks` after the mating, with neither parent. The sim event `AGENT_BORN` has the parents, but the mating itself (`startMating`) pushes no event. The worker detects it: `collectEffects` adds a `MATED` effect (kind 8, after `ESCAPED` at 7; see the Snapshot table) for each alive slot whose `pregnancy` was 0 at the start of the tick and equals `pregnancyTicks - 1` after it (`mate` runs before `advancePregnancies` in `step`, so a new pregnancy has been counted down once; a pregnancy held at 1 by `capBirthsEnabled` or the merit cap is never new, so it writes nothing; with `pregnancyTicks` 1 the birth is in the same tick and the effect is written from the `AGENT_BORN` event with cause `MATE` instead). The sim, its events and `worldHash` do not change; the effect-log hash self-pin of `test/worker/sim-host.test.js` is re-pinned in its own commit.
- _Thread._ `effects-player.js` spawns a thread into `createMateThreadPool` (32 records, oldest recycled). Each frame the pool looks both genomes up in the interpolated agents (the BITE lookup) and, while both live, writes `threadCurve(ends, t, out)` (`ends` is one reused `{ax, ay, bx, by, sign}` scratch, so no call allocates; the sign is the side the curve bulges to` (a quadratic curve whose control point is offset from the midpoint by 0.18 x the length, perpendicular, alternating side by thread index) as 12 segments into a preallocated line buffer (`line-buffer.js`, `line-mesh.js`). `threadAt(age01)`is the alpha:`smoothstep(0, 0.15, age) x (1 - smoothstep(0.6, 1, age))`; the age is `playhead - effect.tick`over`MATE_TICKS` (45), so threads freeze when paused. When one agent is gone the thread fades over its last 6 ticks from its last positions. The colour is the mother's family colour mixed 40% towards white at intensity 1.1, so the bloom lifts it. Width 1.5 device px.
- Pure tests: `threadAt` is 0 at age 0 and 1, rises and falls monotonically, and peaks at 1; `threadCurve` hits both endpoints exactly, bulges perpendicular, and is finite for two agents at one point; the pool recycles the oldest at 33; `MATED` is written once per mating on a hand-built world with `pregnancyTicks` 5 and with 1.

**6. Carcass decay** (`decay`). `writeFood` writes `age01` as the fourth food word and `FOOD_STRIDE` becomes 4 (`ready.layout.foodStride` follows, `snapshot-view.js` reads it); obstacles keep 3 words, so `packBodies` takes the stride. The food layer adds a float instance attribute `iAge` and the vertex shader scales the sparkle by `carcassLook(age01).scale` and the fragment by `.dim`: `dim = 1 - 0.65 x smoothstep(0.1, 1, age01)` (down to 0.35) and `scale = 1 - 0.4 x age01` (down to 0.6). Plant food ignores `age01`. Pure tests: `carcassLook` at 0, 0.1, 0.5 and 1 and monotone in between; `writeFood` writes `foodAge / carcassTicks` for meat and 0 for plants; `packBodies` at both strides.

**7. Finish** (`finish`). `finish-layer.js` draws one full-canvas quad last, over the motes, with premultiplied alpha blending (`ONE`, `ONE_MINUS_SRC_ALPHA`). The vignette is `FINISH.vignette` (0.18) x `smoothstep(0.55, 1.2, 2 x distance from the centre in canvas heights per width)`; the grain is `(interleavedNoise(gl_FragCoord.xy, frame) - 0.5) x 2 x FINISH.grain` (1.5 / 255, in encoded space), split into a premultiplied add for the positive part and extra alpha for the negative part. The noise is the same function the aurora dither uses, advanced each frame, so the grain never sits still. Pure tests: `vignetteAt` is 0 at the centre, at most 0.18 anywhere and non-decreasing outwards; `interleavedNoise` (JS reference of the GLSL constants) lies in 0 to 1, has mean 0.5 within 0.02 over 4096 pixels and differs between frames; the GLSL string contains the same constants.

**8. Click opens the inspector.** `pointerOptions.onPick` in `main.js`, after it selects, calls `iface.openInspectorOnPick()`, which dispatches `{ type: 'open', drawer: 'inspector' }` to the drawer state when the pick id is non-zero, the drawer is `null` or `'inspector'`, and the HUD is not hidden with `H` (`ctx.hidden` in `interface.js`; the click itself ends an idle fade). Pure test on the rule as a function `shouldOpenInspector(drawer, id, hudShown)`: opens on null and on inspector, not on `charts` or `help`, not for id 0, not with the HUD hidden by `H`; `drawerReducer` is unchanged.

**Data changes.** `ready.info.terrain.refuges`; snapshot food stride 4; effect kind `MATED`; `Frame.season` and `Frame.seasonProgress`; `Interpolated.turn`; the `iMotion` and `iShape` and `iAge` instance attributes; `?look=`; `PALETTE` gains `rockBody`, `rockRim`, `rockContact`, `lichen`, `frost`, `refugeGlow` (the old `obstacleBody` and `obstacleRim` are replaced), and `colors.js` exports `AURORA`, `AURORA_MAX_LUMINANCE`, `SCENERY_MAX_LUMINANCE` and `ENERGY_FLOOR`; `agents-shader.js` exports `BODY_CENTRE_FILL` and `BRIGHTNESS_FLOOR`, and its GLSL is templated from them.
