**Validated:** 830e60a (2026-10-17)
**Priority:** 1 - the `fitnessV2` terms in predator-prey.md Phase 3 are built on the D1 to D8 counters this doc's Phase 1 adds (user, 2026-10-17)
**Depends on:** [predator-prey.md](predator-prey.md), [worker-and-renderer.md](worker-and-renderer.md), [evolution-evals.md](evolution-evals.md)

The world is tuned until it passes the world gate in [evolution-rework.md](evolution-rework.md) Phase 3, then the
trainer runs. This doc holds the open balance issues found on the tuning night of 2026-10-16 (experiments `E19` and
`E20`, sweep files in `.claude/worktrees/tune-night-2026-10-16/`), the measures of deliberate behaviour, the method
each tuning night follows, and the biology features that change the balance again. Phase 1 needs only predator-prey.md
Phases 1 and 2; the renderer is needed from Phase 2, whose tuning is judged by watching it.

## Deliberate behaviour

Fitness and the tuning scorecard reward what an agent meant to do, not what happened to it (user, 2026-10-17).
Random contact - drifting into food, a bite on a prey that blundered into the jaws, a call or smoke with no threat
near - earns little or nothing, and wasted signals cost. Every measure below is computed from the sim's own state
and events (rays, headings, timers, `heard`, positions), draws no randomness, and is credited only when the agent's
recent actions show intent.

| #   | Behaviour            | Counts as deliberate                                                                                                                                                      | Counts as chance or waste                                                                                                                     |
| --- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| D1  | Foraging             | Food eaten after the agent saw it on a ray and closed on it (turned towards it and the distance fell over the preceding ticks)                                            | Food eaten with no food on any ray in the preceding ticks, or while turning away from it                                                      |
| D2  | Hunting              | A bite or kill after the hunter held the prey ahead (inside `biteArc` on its rays) and closed the gap over a pursuit window                                               | A bite on prey that entered the jaws without a pursuit                                                                                        |
| D3  | Pack hunting         | Kills where two or more same-lineage hunters converged on the prey within `packWindow`, or a hunt call was heard and answered by a pursuit bite                           | Two hunters biting the same prey by coincidence with no convergence                                                                           |
| D4  | Evasion              | An escape after the prey saw the hunter, turned away or sprinted, or entered smoke                                                                                        | Surviving because the hunter never pursued                                                                                                    |
| D5  | Grouping             | Prey of one lineage staying within call range of each other while a hunter is near, and alarm hearers moving away from the threat                                         | Clumping around one food patch with no threat                                                                                                 |
| D6  | Alarm and hunt calls | A call made while a threat (for alarms) or a prey (for hunt calls) is on the caller's rays, followed by a measurable response from hearers                                | A call with no threat or prey in sight: earns nothing and its energy cost stands; repeated idle calls are penalised                           |
| D7  | Smoke                | Smoke released within a short window of being bitten or with a hunter ahead, followed by an escape                                                                        | Smoke with no hunter near: earns nothing, and the release counts against the agent                                                            |
| D8  | Defence              | Being bitten after the agent saw the hunter and still fled, turned away, released smoke, called or (for a defender) turned to face it; such bites are not held against it | Being bitten with the hunter on its rays and no response: counts against fitness. A bite from a hunter it never saw is luck and counts little |

- Fitness: the fitness that replaces `fitnessV1` pays mostly for D1 to D8, discounts chance intake, and charges
  wasted calls and smoke. It is `fitnessV2` in [predator-prey.md](predator-prey.md) Phase 3, built on the per-agent
  intent counters below.
- The D8 response "turned to face it" is a bite back: no specialisation defends by other means.

### Intent state

`js/evo/observer-intent.js` keeps a per-slot intent state. Each tick `observe` in `js/evo/observer.js` runs
`observeCombat`, then `updateIntent`, then `noteIntentEvents` (calls, smoke and `eaten` deaths), then `applyEvents`
and `countTick`. `updateIntent` reads the state the step leaves behind: `world.inputs` (the rays the agent saw this
tick), `world.outputs` (what it set), `vx`, `vy`, `angle`, `turnRate`, `bittenTimer`, `foodEnergy`, `biteEnergy`,
`spec`, `lineageId`, `x` and `y`. It draws no randomness and allocates nothing per tick. `summariseRays(world, slot,
out)` in `js/evo/ray-summary.js` reads each agent's ray block once per tick into a preallocated `Float64Array`: the
nearest food ray and proximity, the nearest smaller-class hit on an arc ray, the nearest bigger-class hit and its
side, and whether any smoke-flagged hit sits at proximity 1. It reads ray channels from the existing `CH_*`
constants in `js/evo/scripted-parts.js`. Ray `k` points `2 * PI * k / rayCount` from the heading; the **arc rays**
are those within `biteArc` of the heading (rays 0, 1, 2, 14 and 15 at the defaults), computed once at creation by
`arcRays(config)`: ray `k` is an arc ray when the ray angle, wrapped to -PI..PI, is within `biteArc + 1e-9` of 0,
and every ray is one when `biteArc` is at least PI. A smaller-class hit on an arc ray stands in for prey in the jaws;
the jaws themselves test the victim's centre.

| Constant             | Value | Meaning                                                                                                  |
| -------------------- | ----- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `INTENT_WINDOW`      | 30    | Ticks a sighting stays current                                                                           |
| `CLOSING_BITS`       | 10    | Ticks of closing history read                                                                            |
| `CLOSING_MIN`        | 6     | Of those ticks, how many must be closing ticks                                                           |
| `CLOSING_SPEED`      | 0.25  | Fraction of `maxVelocity` the agent's own velocity towards the target ray must exceed for a closing tick |
| `RESPONSE_WINDOW`    | 30    | Ticks after the first bite of an episode in which a response still counts                                |
| `RESPONSE_LIFT`      | 0.25  | How far the response share under threat must exceed the agent's unthreatened response share              |
| `THREAT_PROXIMITY`   | 0.5   | Proximity of a threat hit that makes an agent threatened for the grouping sample                         |
| `GROUP_SAMPLE_TICKS` | 10    | Ticks between grouping samples                                                                           |
| `SIGNAL_LEVEL`       | 0.5   | An output above this counts as sprinting, calling, releasing smoke or biting                             |

Per slot, the state holds:

- **Sightings:** the last tick with food on any ray, with a smaller-class hit on an arc ray (prey ahead), and with a
  threat, plus the side of the nearest threat. A **threat** is a bigger-class hit whose nearest `canHunt` agent of
  another lineage lies within the agent's `visionRange` (from its specialisation) and within half a ray spacing of that ray's direction, found by a scan of
  the alive `canHunt` slots, listed once per tick into a preallocated `Int32Array` and scanned only for agents with a
  bigger-class hit. Once Phase 2's honest classes land, a threat is a "can bite me" hit and the scan is removed.
- **Closing histories:** two 16-bit shift registers, for the nearest food ray and the nearest arc-ray prey; each tick
  shifts in 1 when the agent's own velocity component along that ray's direction exceeds `CLOSING_SPEED x
maxVelocity`.
- **Turn marks:** the last tick on which the agent turned towards the nearest food side and towards the nearest prey
  side (`turnRate` beyond `TURN_THRESHOLD x maxTurn`, side as `turnTowards` gives), and whether the target has sat on
  ray 0 on every tick since its sighting.
- **Response counts:** response ticks and ticks while threatened (a threat sighting within `INTENT_WINDOW` ticks),
  and response ticks and ticks while unthreatened. A **response tick** has the agent turning away from the threat's
  side, sprinting, releasing smoke, biting with the threat on an arc ray, or sitting inside smoke; an alarm call is
  not a response. With no threat in sight, a response tick is any tick with one of those outputs.
- **Energy marks:** `foodEnergy` and `biteEnergy` at the previous tick, so a rise is a meal or a bite this tick.
- **Bite record (on the victim):** `lastBiteDeliberate` and `prevBiteDeliberate`. When an agent is bitten this tick
  (`bittenTimer` 1), its biter is `observer.slotOf` of its `lastBiter`; the biter's bite is classified, and
  `prevBiteDeliberate` takes the old `lastBiteDeliberate` whenever `prevBiter` changes, as the sim does for
  `prevBiter`. A biter that cannot be found counts as chance.
- **Episode latch (agents without `canHunt`):** `episodeFirst` (the tick of the first bite of the open episode),
  `threatAtFirst` (a threat was seen within `INTENT_WINDOW` ticks before that bite) and `responded`. An intent episode
  opens on a bite when none is open, and closes when `noteEscape` or the agent's death ends it. At
  `episodeFirst + RESPONSE_WINDOW` the episode is classified once: `responded` is set when the response share over the
  ticks from the threat sighting to `episodeFirst + RESPONSE_WINDOW` exceeds the agent's unthreatened response share
  by at least `RESPONSE_LIFT` (the unthreatened share counts as 0 below 30 unthreatened ticks); the episode counts
  `unseenBite` without `threatAtFirst`, and `undefendedBite` with it and `responded` clear.

A meal is **deliberate** when food was on a ray within `INTENT_WINDOW` ticks, at least `CLOSING_MIN` of the last
`CLOSING_BITS` food-closing bits are set, and the agent turned towards the food side within `INTENT_WINDOW` ticks or
the food sat on ray 0 since its sighting. A bite is **deliberate** under the same rule on the prey sighting, prey
closing history and prey turn mark. A killer or biter that died this tick is skipped. The whole observer feed stays
under 0.15 ms per tick at 500 agents (0.053 ms before Phase 1).

### Intent counters

`createIntentState(config)` also holds per-slot counters, cleared with the rest of the slot's intent state by
`resetIntentSlot(intent, slot)`, which `resetSlot` in `js/evo/observer-events.js` calls. `fitnessV2` reads them.

| Counter           | Counts                                                                                               |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `deliberateFood`  | Food energy from deliberate meals                                                                    |
| `chanceFood`      | Food energy from the other meals                                                                     |
| `deliberateBite`  | Bite energy from deliberate bites                                                                    |
| `chanceBite`      | Bite energy from the other bites                                                                     |
| `respondedEscape` | Bite episodes on this agent that resolved `escaped` with the episode latch's `responded` set         |
| `preciseSignal`   | Alarm calls with a threat seen, hunt calls with prey ahead, smoke while bitten or with a threat seen |
| `idleSignal`      | Calls and smoke releases that fail that rule                                                         |
| `undefendedBite`  | Bite episodes on this agent with the threat seen and no response                                     |
| `unseenBite`      | Bite episodes on this agent with no threat seen                                                      |

(`observeCombat` receives `observer.intent`);
`js/evo/observer-intent-events.js` classifies `AlarmCalled`, `PackCalled` and `SmokeReleased` events and the `eaten`
deaths for D3.

### Seed minimum per metric

`METRIC_MIN_SEEDS` and `minSeedsFor(metric)` in `js/evo/compare-seeds.js`, read by `compareMetric` in
`js/evo/compare.js`, set 32 seeds for `eval.floor_episodes`, `eval.lineages_alive`,
`eval.lineage_turnover` and `eval.predator_share_cv`; every other metric keeps `MIN_SEEDS` (16). Below a metric's
minimum, `compareMetric` gives no verdict and the note `needs 32 seeds`, and the rest of the report compares as
before. `evals/seeds.json` holds 64 seeds, so a 32-seed calibration compares seeds 1-32 against 33-64. `scripts/eval-power.mjs` (`npm run eval:power -- <report.json>`) resamples one 64-seed report into 1000 random
16-v-16 and 32-v-32 splits and prints, per metric, the false-positive rate of `eval:compare`'s verdict and the
smallest change it detects at 80% power, with no new runs. A metric whose false-positive rate exceeds twice the
nominal 5% at its seed minimum becomes `watch` in `DIRECTIONS`; the calibrations `E23` and `E24` are recorded
alongside.

### Chance-or-waste controllers

`js/evo/scripted-intent.js` exports `INTENT_CONTROLLERS`, spread into `SCRIPTED_CONTROLLERS`, so the anti-exploit
suite runs them too. The arm `random` is not a scripted controller: it runs the brain phase on `natural-v1`'s
random first genomes, so every chance column is also checked against untrained brains.

| Controller        | Does                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `sloppyForager`   | Runs `foodSeeker` on half its 10-tick decision windows and `wanderer`'s drift on the rest, the choice drawn from its `rng` |
| `jawSitter`       | Holds still with `BITE` set: bites only what enters its jaws                                                               |
| `huntCallSpammer` | Runs `hunter` and sets the `HUNT` output every tick                                                                        |
| `grouper`         | Runs `fleer`, and with a threat on its rays steers towards the nearest kin hit instead of straight away                    |
| `cruiser`         | Holds `SEEK_THRUST` straight ahead and never turns: eats only what lies in its path                                        |

`test/evo/deliberate-controllers.slow.test.js` runs each pair for 5000 ticks on seeds 1 and 2, in
`evals/micro-world.json` for the forage pair and `evals/predator-world.json` (predators on even slots, foragers on
odd) for the rest. The chance arm's metric is at most 0.5 x the skill arm's (for a `lower` metric, the skill arm's is
at most 0.5 x the chance arm's); a `null` on the chance arm counts as 0, and a `null` on the skill arm fails the test. `eval.group_cohesion_under_threat`
is a difference, so a negative chance value counts as 0 and the skill arm must be above 0. The D3 pairs run 20000
ticks on seeds 1, 2 and 3, since pack kills need kin hunters born by splitting. `test/evo/helpers/predator-world.js`
takes the predator and prey controllers by name.

### Skill ladder

`npm run ladder` (`scripts/ladder.mjs`) runs every rung of `LADDER_RUNGS` in `js/evo/ladder.js` in the default world
with `minAgents` 0, every agent driven by the rung's controllers, for 10000 ticks on seeds 1, 2 and 3, on the worker
pool `npm run eval` uses. `runControllerSeed` in `js/evo/controller-run.js` runs one rung and seed and returns its
metrics. Options: `--ticks`, `--seeds`, `--config <file.json>`, `--out <path>` (default
`evals/ladder/<id>.json`), `--workers <n>`.

| Ladder | Rungs, lowest first                                                                                                      |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Forage | `random` (`natural-v1`'s random first genomes, in-world births only), `wanderer`, `sloppyForager`, `foodSeeker`, `fleer` |
| Combat | predators `jawSitter` with `foodSeeker` prey, `hunter` with `foodSeeker` prey, `hunter` with `fleer` prey                |

Each rung records the final population, `eval.food_energy_per_1k`, `eval.births_per_1k` and the D metrics, as the
mean over its seeds. `ladderSummary(results, config)` gives, for each pair of neighbouring rungs, the ratio of their
mean final populations, and flags a rung `saturated` when its mean final population reaches 0.9 x `maxAgents`. Phase
1 records the ladder; Phase 2's tuning night gates on it.

## Tuning-night method

1. Prediction first, written to the night's `predictions.md`.
2. 4-seed screens (seed offset 28, 200000 ticks, the live policy) ranked against a defaults screen on the same seeds.
3. The best 3 to 5 go to 16 seeds against the reference report, then to fresh seeds 17-32 with `--allow-seed-diff`.
4. The skill ladder (random brains, a sloppy forager, `foodSeeker`, `fleer`; no floor; 10000 ticks; seeds 1-3) runs for
   every finalist.
5. Ranking uses the deliberate-behaviour metrics (D1 to D8) alongside food, births and fed lifespan; a world where
   chance does nearly as well as skill is rejected.
6. A setting is kept only with a mechanism and a confirmed 16-seed result; the adopted override is recorded as an
   experiment in `evals/experiments.json` and learnings go to `sim-notes.md`.

## Biology features

Phase 3 builds six features in `js/sim/` (and their genes in `js/evo/`). Each is switched by a config key; each
lands switched off so the golden hashes hold while they are built, and T41 switches them all on together. The
brain-layout changes all land first, in T30. A feature that is off makes no RNG draw and writes no state that is
hashed: `hashWorld` includes `fastOut` only when `hebbianRate` is above 0, `vision` and `baseSize` only with
`traitsEnabled`, `sex` only with `sexesEnabled`, and the patch arrays only when `foodPatchCount` is above 0.

`applyGenes(world, slot)` in `js/sim/traits.js` runs after every write of a weight row (births after `childBrain`,
initial, floor and immigrant spawns after the brain is written, and any archive or seeder path that writes
`world.weights`). It derives the per-slot vision, base size and child share from the trait genes (1 when
`traitsEnabled` is false) and copies the hidden-to-output block into `fastOut`. Freeing a slot zeroes `fastOut`,
`vision`, `baseSize`, `sex` and the slot's outputs row.

`crossNeurons` in `js/evo/crossover.js` carries leak gene `h` with hidden neuron `h`'s source parent, and takes each
trait gene whole from the mother or the father by one draw per gene from the stream it already uses; `bOut` is
unchanged. `encodeGenome`, `decodeGenome`, `assertGenomeFits` and the archive codec count the trait and leak genes.
`mutateWeights` takes the layout's `traitStart` and applies `TRAIT_SIGMA` from it to the end of the row, with the
same number of Gaussian draws as before.

### Brain layout v2 (T30)

| Constant       | Before | After | Adds                                                                                                |
| -------------- | ------ | ----- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `OUTPUT_COUNT` | 8      | 11    | `SPLIT` (8), `MEMORY_A` (9), `MEMORY_B` (10)                                                        |
| `EXTRA_INPUTS` | 32     | 35    | body input 32 `sex` (0 female, 1 male), 33 and 34 last tick's `MEMORY_A` and `MEMORY_B`             |
| `TRAIT_GENES`  | -      | 4     | the trait block appended to the weight row: `sizeGene`, `visionGene`, `childShareGene`, `learnGene` |
| `LEAK_GENES`   | -      | 32    | one time-constant gene per hidden unit, appended after the trait block                              |

`brainLayout(config)` gives `weightCount = inputCount x H + H x H + H + H x OUT + OUT + TRAIT_GENES + H` (H is
`brainHidden`). At the defaults `inputCount` is 179 and `weightCount` 7183 (6952 before). `assertGenomeFits`, the archive codec and
`validateChampion` already compare the layout, so every older genome, archive and champion is rejected.

### Memory: leaky hidden units and memory outputs

- `hiddenStep` in `js/sim/brain.js` computes `h = (1 - a) x hOld + a x tanh(acc)` per hidden unit, with
  `a = LEAK_MIN + (1 - LEAK_MIN) x sigmoid(leakGene)` and `LEAK_MIN` 0.05, so a unit keeps its value for about
  1 to 20 ticks. `leakEnabled` false (the off state) uses `a = 1`, the current rule exactly.
- `MEMORY_A` and `MEMORY_B` are written each tick and read back as body inputs 33 and 34 on the next tick; they cost
  nothing and do nothing else. With `leakEnabled` false, inputs 33 and 34 are 0.
- Leak genes start at 3 (`a` about 0.95) in `randomWeights`.

### Hebbian learning

- Each agent has a plastic copy of its hidden-to-output block, `fastOut` (`H x OUT` floats per slot, a new world
  array), that starts equal to the genome's block at birth. `outputStep` reads `fastOut`; the genome's weights in
  `world.weights` never change during a life, so children inherit the genome, not what the parent learned.
- After each tick's outputs, `hebbianStep` updates `fastOut[j, o] += eta x (h_j x out_o - HEBB_DECAY x
(fastOut[j, o] - w[j, o]))`, with `eta = hebbianRate x sigmoid(learnGene)` and `HEBB_DECAY` 0.01, clamped to
  +-`HEBB_CLAMP` 4.
- `hebbianRate` (default 0.01, on) is the config key; 0 skips `hebbianStep`.
- The archive, offers and the observer read the genome row in `world.weights`, never `fastOut`.

### Evolvable body traits with costs

Each trait gene maps through `traitValue(gene) = 1 + TRAIT_SPAN x tanh(gene)`, `TRAIT_SPAN` 0.3, so a trait runs
from 0.7 to 1.3 of its default; genes start at 0. `traitsEnabled` false holds every trait at 1.

| Trait            | Changes                                                                                                                     | Costs                                                                            |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `sizeGene`       | `baseSize` for this agent, so bite size, the hunt and mate size ratios and collisions follow                                | passive loss scales with size, as it does today                                  |
| `visionGene`     | the agent's `visionRange` (ray length), read in `castRays` from a new per-slot `vision` array                               | passive loss x `visionCost` x (vision trait - 1), `visionCost` 0.5, only above 1 |
| `childShareGene` | `childEnergyShare` for this mother (0.35 to 0.65), in `giveBirth` and in `canMatePair`'s `childEnergyMin / share` threshold | the mother keeps less energy                                                     |
| `learnGene`      | the Hebbian rate, as above                                                                                                  | none beyond step time                                                            |

`mutateWeights` mutates the trait and leak genes with their own sigma, `TRAIT_SIGMA` 0.1, rather than the weight sigma.
The hunt and mate size ratios compare trait-scaled sizes, so a small body is cheaper to run and easier to bite. The
spatial-hash query radius covers 1.3 x the largest `visionRange`.

### Patchy food that regrows

- `foodPatchCount` (default 16) permanent patches are placed at world creation from the `food` stream, each with a
  radius `foodPatchRadius` (400) and a stock from 0 to `foodPatchCapacity` (1).
- `spawnFoodItem` keeps the fertile-zone draw first, unchanged; when it picks no zone, it places the item in a patch
  chosen with weight equal to its stock with probability `1 - foodBackgroundShare` (0.2), else uniformly as today.
  Placement in a patch uses `placeInZone`'s obstacle-clear retries and its fallback to `placeClear`.
- Patches start at full stock. Each food item eaten inside a patch takes `foodPatchDepletion` (0.02) from the stock of
  the nearest patch whose disc holds it (lowest index on ties); stock regrows logistically,
  `stock += foodPatchRegrow x stock x (1 - stock)` per tick, with `foodPatchRegrow` 0.0005, and is clamped to
  0.05 to `foodPatchCapacity` (`Float64Array`). The stock target rule in `spawnFood` is unchanged: patches move where
  food appears and how fast each place refills, not how much food the world holds.
- New world arrays `patchX`, `patchY`, `patchStock` are hashed; the snapshot header carries the patches for the
  renderer (`worldInfo` gains `patches`).
- `foodPatchCount` 0 is the off state.

### Lower floor

`minAgents` falls from 100 to 40, and `floorRescueAtZero` (default false) makes the floor spawn only a
specialisation that has 0 alive when true. Both are tuned on the Phase 4 night.

### Mate choice by genetic similarity

- `geneticDistance(world, a, b)` in `js/sim/genetics.js` is the mean over `SIMILARITY_DIMS` (64) weight indices of
  `|w_a - w_b| / blockInitSd`, the indices fixed per layout as `(k x 7919) mod traitStart` for k = 0 to 63 (7919 is
  prime, so the samples spread over every hidden unit; the trait and leak genes are excluded). Clones score 0 and two
  unrelated random genomes about 1.13.
- A pair can mate only when the distance is below `mateDistanceMax` (default 0.8; `Infinity` is the off state); the
  Phase 4 night sets its value.

### Two sexes with female choice, and splitting as a choice

- `sex` (a new `Uint8Array` world array, snapshot flag bit 14) is drawn 50/50 from the `lifecycle` stream at every
  entry when `sexesEnabled`; with it false there is no draw, every agent is female and any pair may mate, as today.
- With sexes on, `mate` walks the ready females (`MATE` above 0) by slot ascending. For each it scans every ready male
  (`MATE` above 0) of her specialisation in the cells covering `mateRange`, after the existing cell-scan dedupe,
  within `mateSizeRatio` and `mateDistanceMax`, and picks the one with the most energy (ties to the lower slot); then
  one `lifecycle` draw against the season's chance decides whether they mate. The female is the mother whatever her
  slot: `startMating`, the energy check in `canMatePair` and the `fatherWeights` copy key on sex. A male is limited by
  `mateCost` and his cooldown. `test/sim/helpers/mate-search-reference.js` follows the same rule.
- Splitting needs the `SPLIT` output above 0.5, a female, and energy above `splitMinEnergy + splitCost`; `splitCost`
  (default 50) is removed first with `LOSS_CAUSE.COST`, then the gift is given as today. A split child draws a fresh
  sex. With sexes off, splitting stays automatic as today.
- Floor and immigrant spawns draw sex 50/50, so a specialisation whose agents are all male recovers through the floor.
- `AGENT_BORN` field 9 carries the child's sex.
