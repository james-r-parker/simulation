# Hunter learning

**Status:** Proposed - hunters have time to learn to kill: Phase 1 (parental provisioning, a juvenile dependency with a kin pull, and the hunter reflex on a spec-flip child, with the detectors that show them) is built first; Phase 2 adds reward-gated lifetime learning.
**Validated:** 2683cb6 (2026-11-05)
**Priority:** 2 - after natural-selection.md, whose Phase 4 is nearly done; the self-sustaining world after E112 keeps hunters alive on about 10 of 16 seeds but a hunter's life has no kill in 80 to 92% of cases, and every budget, placement and scent lever has been spent (user, 2026-11-05)
**Depends on:** [natural-selection.md](natural-selection.md)

On the world after E112 (`maxFood` 3600, short-lived toxic caps) hunters live on carcasses and chance bites and starve:
the median `eval.hunter_kills_per_life` is 0 on all 20 arms of E113 and `eval.hunter_zero_kill_share` is 0.80 to 0.92.
This doc teaches hunters to kill the way nature does: a parent feeds its young while they learn, the young stay near it
and burn less, the motor pattern is present at birth, and practice refines it inside a life. Each mechanism is a
default-off switch, screened on its own and adopted or deleted.

## Principle - survival is not competence

Keeping a hunter alive long enough to learn is not the same as giving it the ability to learn. Provisioning, a lower
burn and a kin pull all lengthen a juvenile's life whether or not the juvenile ever hunts better; a fed child that
shadows its parent survives on the parent's kills. Survival and energy metrics (`eval.hunter_zero_kill_share`,
`eval.hunter_juvenile_death_share`, `eval.predators_at_end`, `eval.population_mean`) are guards and signs of life, never
the verdict.

- **Every adoption shows hunting competence improved.** A verdict to adopt a switch needs the age-matched kill
  conversion (`eval.hunter_kill_conversion_by_age.1k-2k`) up with the `eval:compare` interval excluding 0, and the
  independent kill share (`eval.hunter_independent_kill_share`) not down. A switch that only moves survival or energy
  is deleted.
- **Rewarded learning also beats its controls.** Phase 2 adopts `rewardedHebb` only if it beats a shuffled-reward arm
  (the same rewards at unrelated times) on the age-matched kill conversion; beating the defaults alone proves nothing,
  because a trace can change the plastic weights without teaching a link from act to meal.
- **A hunter that wipes out its prey is not a better world** ([principles.md](../principles.md), "Other blobs are the
  climate"). Every screen and confirmation reads the ecological guards (prey extinctions, boom and bust, population
  volatility) beside the competence metrics.

## Context

### Nature model

| Behaviour in nature                                                                                                                                                    | Maps to                                                                                                                                                                |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Wolf pups are fed meat by the pack, carried in the stomach and regurgitated, until they travel with the pack (Mech 1970); lion and tiger cubs eat at the mother's kill | Provisioning: a hunter hands its young a share of each meal                                                                                                            |
| A cheetah mother releases live, weakened gazelle fawns for her cubs to catch; a domestic cat mother brings live prey home (Caro 1994; Caro 1980)                       | The young practise on prey the parent has already brought down; the share is paid from the parent's own meal                                                           |
| Meerkat adults give pups disabled scorpions, then live ones, as the pups age (Thornton and McAuliffe 2006)                                                             | Provisioning runs for a bounded window (`juvenileTicks`), then stops                                                                                                   |
| Predator young stay with the mother for months to years (lion 2 years, cheetah 18 months, wolf 10 months) while their skill grows                                      | Juvenile dependency: a lower burn during the window, and a pull towards kin (heritable, not age-gated; adults follow kin too, a pack-like side effect that is watched) |
| The stalk, chase, pounce and bite sequence is innate and refined by practice; kittens raised without their mother pounce but kill less often (Caro 1980)               | The hunter reflex at birth (`applyInstinct`, and `applyHunterReflex` for a flip child); reward-gated plasticity of the hidden-to-output path (`hebbianStep`)           |
| Practice is reinforced by the meal, not by the attempt                                                                                                                 | A reward trace gates the Hebbian update; no fitness term pays for a kill                                                                                               |

### Selection

[principles.md](../principles.md) rule 7 credits "kin that were fed" to the feeder, and rule 4 forbids restocking a
role. Provisioning moves energy a hunter has already earned to its own young; nothing enters the world. "Measure
scenes, reward them almost never" refuses a fitness or merit term for kills: the doc adds detectors, never a reward.

## Config keys and options

Every switch defaults to false and every behaviour key to its no-op, so the golden hashes do not move until a phase
adopts a switch.

| Key                  | Type    | Default | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| -------------------- | ------- | ------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `provisionEnabled`   | boolean | false   | Phase 1. A hunter that stores energy from a kill, a bite or a carcass hands `provisionShare` of it to its young in range                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `provisionShare`     | number  | 0.3     | Share of the stored energy moved, in [0, 1]                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `provisionRange`     | number  | 120     | Largest centre distance in world units between giver and young                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| `juvenileTicks`      | integer | 3000    | A `canHunt` young is juvenile while `ageTicks` is below this; the age bands of the detectors also use it                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `juvenileDependency` | boolean | false   | Phase 1. A juvenile `canHunt` body pays `juvenileBurn` of its cost, and every `canHunt` child gets the kin pull floor in `childBrain`                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `juvenileBurn`       | number  | 0.6     | Multiplier on the standing, movement and turn cost of a juvenile hunter, in [0, 1]                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `kinPullWeight`      | number  | 0.5     | The kin thrust and turn cells of a hunter are raised to at least `kinPullWeight` x `instinctWeight` while `juvenileDependency` is on, in [0, 4]                                                                                                                                                                |
| `flipReflex`         | boolean | false   | Phase 1. A child whose specialisation `canHunt` while its mother's does not gets the hunter prey reflex added to its row                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `rewardedHebb`       | boolean | false   | Phase 2. A reward trace scales the Hebbian rate: `eta x (rewardBase + rewardGain x trace)`                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `rewardGain`         | number  | 4       | Gain of the trace, in [0, 10]                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `rewardBase`         | number  | 0.25    | Share of the plain rate that applies with no reward, in [0, 1]                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| `rewardDecay`        | number  | 0.02    | Per-tick decay of the trace, in [0, 1]                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| `rewardSource`       | enum    | `meat`  | Phase 2. Which events feed the reward trace: `kill` (a fatal bite only), `kill-bite` (a fatal or a non-fatal bite), `meat` (any bite and any carcass meal). Received provisioning never feeds it, whatever the value                                                                                           |
| `rewardShuffle`      | boolean | false   | Phase 2 control, deleted at the verdict. A reward is queued and delivered to the same hunter `rewardShuffleMin` to `rewardShuffleMax` ticks after it was earned (a uniform draw from the stream `reward-shuffle`), so the count and size of rewards per life are kept and their timing is unrelated to the act |
| `rewardShuffleMin`   | integer | 300     | Shortest delivery delay in ticks of a shuffled reward, at least 6 x `1 / rewardDecay` so the trace of the act has gone                                                                                                                                                                                         |

The six switches (the booleans `provisionEnabled`, `juvenileDependency`, `flipReflex`, `rewardedHebb` and `rewardShuffle`, and
the enum `rewardSource`) are entered in `scripts/flag-ledger.json` with this doc and the task that adopts or deletes each
(T10, T11, T12 and T22 below). The no-plasticity control needs no key: it is `hebbianRate` 0.

## Detectors

New `eval.*` metrics, all computed by the eval observer from `HunterKill`, `HunterLedger`, the alive hunters at the last
tick and world counters, none a reward.

| Metric                                     | Direction | Reads                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `eval.hunter_first_kill_age`               | watch     | Median age in ticks at a hunter's first deliberate kill, over hunters that killed. Survivor-biased (a fall can mean fewer hunters kill at all), so it is read beside the censored time below and never alone  |
| `eval.hunter_kill_rate_by_age.<band>`      | watch     | Kills per 1,000 life ticks in the band (`juvenile` below `juvenileTicks`, `young` below 4 x `juvenileTicks`, `adult` beyond). Hunters alive at the last tick count at their end age (censored lives included) |
| `eval.hunter_juvenile_death_share`         | lower     | Share of hunter deaths with an age below `juvenileTicks`                                                                                                                                                      |
| `eval.provisioned_energy_share`            | watch     | Energy the young received by provisioning (`provisionTotals`) over the energy hunters stored from all sources                                                                                                 |
| `eval.hunter_kill_rate_by_source.<source>` | watch     | Kills per 1,000 life ticks by birth source (`HUNTER_BIRTH_NAMES` of `scene-hunters.js`), censored lives included as above                                                                                     |

### Competence detectors (Phase 1)

These do not depend on survival: each has the hunters that never kill, or never hunt, in its denominator.

An **encounter** is a chase as `trackApproaches` already defines it: it opens at the first closing sighting of prey by a
`canHunt` agent and resolves caught, escaped or given up. Eligibility is built in (a hunter, prey in view and closing).
The chase tracker is reused, not replaced; the new scene reads each resolved chase with the hunter's age at its opening
and the hunter's slot. A chase cut short by the hunter's death, or whose victim dies of another cause, is dropped, as
there. **Kill conversion** is caught over caught plus escaped plus given up. Run-wide it equals `eval.chase_catch_share`;
the new metrics split it by the hunter's age at the opening of the chase, so an arm is judged on hunters of the same age
and not on lifetime kills.

| Metric                                      | Direction | Reads                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------- | --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `eval.hunter_kill_conversion_by_age.<band>` | higher    | Kill conversion of the chases that open in the age band: `under1k` (age below 1000), `1k-2k` (1000 to 1999) and `over2k`. `1k-2k` is the primary target; the other two are `watch`. Null when no chase opened in the band                                                                                         |
| `eval.hunter_chases_by_age.<band>`          | watch     | Resolved chases in the band, so a conversion rests on a count and `eval:power` can size it                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `eval.hunter_first_kill_km_median`          | lower     | Kaplan-Meier median age at first kill. Every hunter born after burn-in is an observation: the event is the first deliberate kill, and a hunter that dies without one, or is alive at the last tick without one, is censored at its age. Null with the reason `median not reached` while the curve stays above 0.5 |
| `eval.hunter_first_kill_rmst`               | lower     | Restricted mean of the same Kaplan-Meier time up to the horizon `2 x juvenileTicks`: the area under the no-kill-yet curve. Always defined when a hunter was born, so it is the cited number while the median is unreached                                                                                         |
| `eval.hunter_independent_kill_share`        | higher    | Share of deliberate kills made by an independent hunter: one that received no provisioning in the last `INDEPENDENT_TICKS` (1000) ticks and has no living parent within `provisionRange`. The `HunterKill` payload carries the bit                                                                                |
| `eval.juvenile_parent_distance`             | watch     | Mean distance, at the 1000-tick checkpoints after burn-in, from each living juvenile hunter to its nearest living parent                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `eval.juvenile_sibling_distance`            | watch     | The same juveniles' mean distance to their living siblings (same `parentA` and `parentB`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| `eval.juvenile_parent_pull`                 | watch     | `eval.juvenile_sibling_distance` over `eval.juvenile_parent_distance`: above 1 the juveniles stay nearer their parent than their siblings, near 1 the kin pull is a pull towards any kin. Null without a juvenile that has both                                                                                   |

### Ecological guards (every screen and confirmation)

| Metric                                                                   | Direction | Reads                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `eval.prey_extinctions`                                                  | lower     | Count, at the 1000-tick checkpoints after burn-in, of prey specialisations (not `canHunt`) that go from at least one alive to none. The seed frequency is the share of seeds with a count above 0, read from the per-seed values        |
| `eval.boom_bust_count`                                                   | lower     | Count of boom-bust episodes at the same checkpoints: hunters reach at least twice their last trough, then prey fall to at most half their level at that trough, then hunters fall to at most half their peak, all inside 20 checkpoints |
| `eval.predator_share_cv`, `eval.basin_pop_cv`, `eval.pred_prey_lag_corr` | watch     | Existing: population volatility of the hunters and the basins, and the lag between the two populations                                                                                                                                  |

A guard fails when `eval:compare` shows an increase of at least 5% of the before mean with the interval excluding 0 for a
`lower` or `watch` metric of this table, or a seed frequency of prey extinction above that of the reference arm.

## Phases

### Phase 1 - Provision, depend and flip

**Targets:** eval.hunter_kill_conversion_by_age.1k-2k, eval.hunter_first_kill_rmst, eval.hunter_independent_kill_share, eval.hunter_zero_kill_share, eval.hunter_juvenile_death_share, eval.hunter_kill_rate_by_source.spec-flip, eval.predators_at_end, eval.prey_types_at_end, eval.population_mean, eval.prey_extinctions, eval.boom_bust_count

Better means, on 16 seeds with `floorTrigger` 0. Competence first (the principle above): the age-matched
`eval.hunter_kill_conversion_by_age.1k-2k` up with the `eval:compare` interval excluding 0 and `--min-effect 0.10` (the
baseline is read from the `E115` defaults arm before the prediction is written); `eval.hunter_first_kill_rmst` lower with
the interval excluding 0; `eval.hunter_independent_kill_share` not down. Then survival, as signs of life that do not count
without the competence rows: `eval.hunter_zero_kill_share` down at least 0.10 from about 0.85;
`eval.hunter_juvenile_death_share` down at least 0.10; `eval.hunter_kill_rate_by_source.spec-flip` at least 0.15 per 1,000
ticks (from 0.060); `eval.predators_at_end` at least 1 on 12 of 16 seeds; `eval.prey_types_at_end` and
`eval.population_mean` not worse. Ecological guards, each not failing as the guard rule in Detectors defines it:
`eval.prey_extinctions` (and its seed frequency), `eval.boom_bust_count`, `eval.predator_share_cv`, `eval.basin_pop_cv`,
`eval.pred_prey_lag_corr`. `eval.hunter_first_kill_age` and `eval.juvenile_parent_pull` are read, not gated.

Mechanisms:

- **Provisioning** (`provisionEnabled`). `provisionYoung` in a new `js/sim/provision.js` runs once per kill or meal when a
  `canHunt` agent stores energy in `consumeFood` (meat) or `applyBite`. It calls `cellRange` on the spatial hash and copies
  `hash.range` into locals, as `eatAround` does, then scans for agents that are the giver's offspring (`parentA` or
  `parentB` equals the giver's `genomeId`; the id is a per-birth serial, so the match is unique to one life with no
  generation or slot check, and a split child that names its mother twice is counted once), `canHunt`, below
  `juvenileTicks` and within `provisionRange`. It stamps each young's `provisionedTick` (a per-slot `Int32Array`, -1 at
  spawn and in `freeAgent`, derived and outside `worldHash`) with the tick of the transfer. It splits
  `provisionShare` of the stored energy equally among them, caps each part by the young's headroom, and calls
  `transferEnergy` with the capped amount; `transferEnergy` clamps to the giver's energy and throws only on a non-finite or
  negative amount. The giver pays only what the young store. Nothing is created. `provisionTotals` (a two-slot
  `Float64Array` on the world: energy handed away, energy received) counts both sides; the two are equal by construction
  and `assertInvariants` checks it. The hunter income kinds (`INCOME`) are unchanged: the parent's meal is already income
  once, and a fifth kind would count the same energy twice in `eval.hunter_income_share.*`.
- **Juvenile dependency** (`juvenileDependency`). `juvenileBurnOf` in a new `js/sim/juvenile.js` returns `juvenileBurn` for
  a `canHunt` body below `juvenileTicks` and 1 otherwise. `tickCost` in [energy.js](../../js/sim/energy.js) multiplies the
  base cost and the nominal standing, movement and sprint terms it stores for the ledger (`nominal[0..2]`) by it, so the
  ledger split stays consistent. The kin pull is `applyHunterReflex` raising the `kin` strength-to-thrust and
  sine-to-turn cells to at least `kinPullWeight` x `instinctWeight` (a floor, so repeated calls do not accumulate).
  `childBrain` calls it for every `canHunt` child, and `applyInstinct` calls it for a founder. The pull is not age-gated
  (the age input is `ageTicks / maxAgeTicks`, about 0.04 at 3000 ticks): adults follow kin too, a pack-like cohesion that
  `eval.hunter_kill_rate_by_age.adult` and the population guards watch. The juvenile window applies to the burn only.
- **Hunter reflex on a flip** (`flipReflex`). `applyInstinct` splits its `canHunt` branch into `applyHunterReflex`.
  `childBrain` in [natural-birth.js](../../js/evo/policies/natural-birth.js) calls it with the prey terms, at
  `instinctWeight`, on the child's row before mutation, when the child's specialisation `canHunt` and its mother's does
  not (the trigger is the specialisations, not `birthSource`, so mating and split children are covered alike). The terms
  are added on top of the copied or crossed row and clamp at `WEIGHT_CLAMP`; the flee terms the row carries from a
  non-hunter mother stay and evolution sorts them out. The call draws nothing.
- **Competence detectors** (Detectors, no switch). A new `js/evo/scene-competence.js` reads the chase tracker: `trackApproaches`
  calls a `noteChase(slot, outcome, openedTick)` hook in the scene when a chase resolves, and the scene bins the outcome by
  the hunter's age at the opening (`ageTicks` minus the ticks since `openedTick`), from burn-in on. It also keeps, per hunter
  life, the first-kill age or the censoring age for the Kaplan-Meier time (a `HunterKill` with count 1, a `HunterLedger`
  death, and the alive hunters at the last tick). The independent-kill tally reads the `dependent` bit of each
  `HunterKill`, added with provisioning. The bit is computed
  in `noteLedgerKill` by `isDependentKiller` in `provision.js`: the killer's `provisionedTick` is within
  `INDEPENDENT_TICKS` of the kill, or a parent (found by matching `parentA` and `parentB` to a living `genomeId`, as
  `slotOfGenome` does) is alive within `provisionRange`. The bit adds a field to the `HunterKill` payload; no new event.
- **Ecological guards** (no switch). A new `js/evo/scene-guards.js` samples, at the 1000-tick checkpoints after burn-in, the
  alive count of each prey specialisation and the hunter count, and computes `eval.prey_extinctions` and
  `eval.boom_bust_count` with the thresholds named at the top of the module (twice, half, 20 checkpoints). The three
  existing volatility metrics are read as they are.
- **Kin attraction detector** (no switch). The `kin` channel covers parents, children and siblings, so the kin floor of
  `juvenileDependency` pulls towards any kin. `js/evo/scene-kin-proximity.js` samples, at the 1000-tick checkpoints, each
  living juvenile hunter's distance to its nearest living parent and its mean distance to its living siblings, and reports
  `eval.juvenile_parent_pull`. A pull to the parent shows as a ratio well above 1; a ratio near 1 says the juveniles follow
  any kin, and the verdict on `juvenileDependency` reads it.
- Brain layout, `inputCount` and `ARCHIVE_VERSION` do not change.

### Phase 2 - Practice

**Targets:** eval.hunter_kill_conversion_by_age.1k-2k, eval.hunter_conversion_gain, eval.hunter_kills_per_life, eval.hunter_zero_kill_share, eval.predators_at_end, eval.prey_types_at_end, eval.population_mean, eval.prey_extinctions, eval.boom_bust_count

Better means, on 16 seeds with `floorTrigger` 0 and the Phase 1 defaults as the reference. Competence first: the best
reward arm beats the **shuffled-reward arm** (`rewardShuffle` on, the same `rewardSource`) on
`eval.hunter_kill_conversion_by_age.1k-2k` by `eval:compare` with the interval excluding 0 and `--min-effect 0.10`, and
beats the **no-plasticity arm** (`hebbianRate` 0) on the same metric in the same way; `eval.hunter_conversion_gain` is
above 0 with the interval excluding 0 and above the shuffled arm's. A win against the Phase 1 defaults alone does not adopt
the switch. Then survival, as signs of life: `eval.hunter_zero_kill_share` down at least 0.10 from the Phase 1 result;
median `eval.hunter_kills_per_life` not below the Phase 1 result; `eval.predators_at_end` at least 1 on 12 of 16 seeds;
`eval.prey_types_at_end` and `eval.population_mean` not worse. Ecological guards as in Phase 1 (`eval.prey_extinctions`
and its seed frequency, `eval.boom_bust_count`, `eval.predator_share_cv`, `eval.basin_pop_cv`, `eval.pred_prey_lag_corr`).

Mechanism:

- **Reward-gated learning** (`rewardedHebb`). A new `js/sim/reward.js` keeps a per-slot `rewardTrace`: `noteReward` adds
  `min(1, stored / foodEnergy)` when a source the `rewardSource` option admits stores energy, and `hebbianStep` multiplies `eta` by
  `rewardBase + rewardGain x trace` and decays the trace by `rewardDecay`. `hebbianStep` changes only `fastOut`, the
  hidden-to-output block, so practice refines the hidden-to-output path and does not rewrite the skip-weight reflex of
  Phase 1. The genome row stays unchanged, so a child does not inherit the practice, as `hebbian.js` states.
- **Reward sources** (`rewardSource`). The trace is not fed by "any energy gain": `noteReward` takes the source. `applyBite`
  in `feeding.js` already knows `fatal` and `gained`, and calls it with `kill` when the bite is fatal and `bite` when it is
  not; `consumeFood` calls it with `scavenge` for a carcass meal. `kill` admits `kill` only, `kill-bite` admits `kill` and
  `bite`, `meat` admits all three. `provisionYoung` never calls `noteReward`: received provisioning feeds no trace, and the
  `provisioned` slot of `rewardCounts` (a four-slot `Float64Array` on the world, derived and outside `worldHash`) stays 0, a
  tripwire that `assertInvariants` checks. Each source's count rises at the call whether or not the option admits it, so
  `eval.reward_events.<source>` reads the same in every arm. The kill-linked arm (`kill`) is screened as its own arm.
- **Learning controls** (screen arms). The **shuffled-reward** arm sets `rewardShuffle`: `noteReward` queues the reward it
  would have added in a fixed per-slot queue of four entries (due tick, magnitude) instead of adding it, the due tick is
  `tick + rewardShuffleMin + floor(u x (rewardShuffleMax - rewardShuffleMin + 1))` with `u` drawn from the world stream
  `fork(rng, 'reward-shuffle')`, and `hebbianStep` adds the entries due this tick. Each life keeps the count and the size
  of its rewards (those still queued at death are dropped and counted in `eval.reward_events.shuffle_dropped`), at a time
  unrelated to the act. A full queue drops the new reward, counted the same way. The **no-plasticity** arm is `hebbianRate`
  0 with the same seeds and config, so the founders are the same and nothing a life does changes its weights.
- **Within-lifetime change** (no switch). The competence scene of Phase 1 also bins resolved chases at ages 3000 to 3999;
  `eval.hunter_conversion_gain` is the per-hunter late minus early conversion over hunters with at least 2 resolved chases
  in each band.
- Brain layout does not change. A pooled "carcass" or "weakened prey" input would change the layout; it is outside this doc.

## Verification

1. `scripts/check.sh` is green per chunk PR, including the golden test, `bench-check`, `eval-smoke` and the shuffled tests.
2. `npm run replay -- --seed 1 --ticks 20000 --level debug --policy map-elites-v6 --config <file with the switches on> --check-invariants`
   logs `HunterKill` and `Provisioned` events and no error.
3. `npm run eval:power -- <report>` prints a false-positive rate and a detectable change for each new metric; a metric
   with no power is not cited in a verdict.
4. `E116` and `E118`: `npm run eval:compare -- <before.json> <after.json>` meets the Targets of the phase, the competence rows
   first; `E118` compares the best reward arm with the shuffled-reward and no-plasticity arms, and every confirmation reads
   the ecological guards.
5. After an adopt task: `npm run browser:hash` prints the re-pinned hashes from a Chromium worker.
6. Manual browser walk: `npm run dev`, open http://localhost:5173, start the live policy at 500 agents and watch for 10
   minutes. Name a life aloud for each: a young hunter staying near its parent, a parent's kill followed by its young
   eating, a young hunter making its first kill, a juvenile that starved after its parent died. The console is free of
   errors.
