# Hunter learning

**Status:** Proposed - hunters have time to learn to kill: Phase 1 (parental provisioning and a juvenile dependency, with the detectors that show them) is built first; Phase 2 adds the hunter reflex on a spec flip and reward-gated lifetime learning.
**Validated:** 68d670c (2026-11-05)
**Priority:** 1 - the self-sustaining world after E112 keeps hunters alive on about 10 of 16 seeds but a hunter's life has no kill in 80 to 92% of cases, and every budget, placement and scent lever has been spent (user, 2026-11-05)
**Depends on:** [natural-selection.md](natural-selection.md)

On the world after E112 (`maxFood` 3600, short-lived toxic caps) hunters live on carcasses and chance bites and starve:
the median `eval.hunter_kills_per_life` is 0 on all 20 arms of E113 and `eval.hunter_zero_kill_share` is 0.80 to 0.92.
This doc teaches hunters to kill the way nature does: a parent feeds its young while they learn, the young stay near it
and burn less, the motor pattern is present at birth, and practice refines it inside a life. Each mechanism is a
default-off switch, screened on its own and adopted or deleted.

## Context

### Nature model

| Behaviour in nature                                                                                                                                                    | Maps to                                                                                                      |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Wolf pups are fed meat by the pack, carried in the stomach and regurgitated, until they travel with the pack (Mech 1970); lion and tiger cubs eat at the mother's kill | Provisioning: a hunter hands its young a share of each meal                                                  |
| A cheetah mother releases live, weakened gazelle fawns for her cubs to catch; a domestic cat mother brings live prey home (Caro 1994; Caro 1980)                       | The young practise on prey the parent has already brought down; the share is paid from the parent's own meal |
| Meerkat adults give pups disabled scorpions, then live ones, as the pups age (Thornton and McAuliffe 2006)                                                             | Provisioning runs for a bounded window (`juvenileTicks`), then stops                                         |
| Predator young stay with the mother for months to years (lion 2 years, cheetah 18 months, wolf 10 months) while their skill grows                                      | Juvenile dependency: a lower burn and a pull towards kin during the window                                   |
| The stalk, chase, pounce and bite sequence is innate and refined by practice; kittens raised without their mother pounce but kill less often (Caro 1980)               | The hunter reflex at birth (`applyInstinct`) and reward-gated plasticity (`hebbianStep`)                     |
| Practice is reinforced by the meal, not by the attempt                                                                                                                 | A reward trace gates the Hebbian update; no fitness term pays for a kill                                     |

### Selection

[principles.md](../principles.md) rule 7 credits "kin that were fed" to the feeder, and rule 4 forbids restocking a
role. Provisioning moves energy a hunter has already earned to its own young; nothing enters the world. "Measure
scenes, reward them almost never" refuses a fitness or merit term for kills: the doc adds detectors, never a reward.

## Config keys and options

Every switch defaults to false and every behaviour key to its no-op, so the golden hashes do not move until a phase
adopts a switch.

| Key                  | Type    | Default | Meaning                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------- | ------- | ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `provisionEnabled`   | boolean | false   | Phase 1. A hunter that stores energy from a kill, a bite or a carcass hands `provisionShare` of it to its young in range  |
| `provisionShare`     | number  | 0.3     | Share of the stored energy moved, in [0, 1]                                                                               |
| `provisionRange`     | number  | 120     | Largest centre distance in world units between giver and young                                                            |
| `juvenileTicks`      | integer | 3000    | A `canHunt` young is juvenile while `ageTicks` is below this; the age bands of the detectors also use it                  |
| `juvenileDependency` | boolean | false   | Phase 1. A juvenile `canHunt` body pays `juvenileBurn` of its base cost and `applyInstinct` adds the kin pull             |
| `juvenileBurn`       | number  | 0.6     | Multiplier on the standing, movement and turn cost of a juvenile hunter, in [0, 1]                                        |
| `kinPullWeight`      | number  | 0.5     | Weight of the kin thrust and turn terms `applyInstinct` adds for a hunter while `juvenileDependency` is on, in [0, 4]     |
| `flipReflex`         | boolean | false   | Phase 2. A child whose specialisation flips to `canHunt` from a non-hunter mother gets the hunter reflex added to its row |
| `rewardedHebb`       | boolean | false   | Phase 2. A reward trace scales the Hebbian rate: `eta x (rewardBase + rewardGain x trace)`                                |
| `rewardGain`         | number  | 4       | Gain of the trace, in [0, 10]                                                                                             |
| `rewardBase`         | number  | 0.25    | Share of the plain rate that applies with no reward, in [0, 1]                                                            |
| `rewardDecay`        | number  | 0.02    | Per-tick decay of the trace, in [0, 1]                                                                                    |

The four booleans are entered in `scripts/flag-ledger.json` with this doc and the task that adopts or deletes each
(T9, T10, T17 and T18 below).

## Detectors

New `eval.*` metrics, all computed by the eval observer from `HunterKill`, `HunterLedger` and world counters, none a
reward.

| Metric                                     | Direction | Reads                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `eval.hunter_first_kill_age`               | lower     | Median age in ticks at a hunter's first deliberate kill, over hunters that killed                                                               |
| `eval.hunter_kill_rate_by_age.<band>`      | watch     | Kills per 1,000 life ticks in the band (`juvenile` below `juvenileTicks`, `young` below 4 x `juvenileTicks`, `adult` beyond), from dead hunters |
| `eval.hunter_juvenile_death_share`         | lower     | Share of hunter deaths with an age below `juvenileTicks`                                                                                        |
| `eval.provisioned_energy_share`            | watch     | Energy moved by provisioning over the energy hunters stored from all sources                                                                    |
| `eval.hunter_kill_rate_by_source.<source>` | watch     | Kills per 1,000 life ticks by birth source (the `HUNTER_BIRTH_NAMES` of `scene-hunters.js`), Phase 2                                            |

Phase 2 also reads the existing `eval.hebb_drift` and `eval.hebb_drift_clamped_share` of `metrics-hebb.js`.

## Phases

### Phase 1 - Provision and depend

**Targets:** eval.hunter_zero_kill_share, eval.hunter_juvenile_death_share, eval.hunter_first_kill_age, eval.predators_at_end, eval.prey_types_at_end, eval.population_mean

Better means, on 16 seeds with `floorTrigger` 0: `eval.hunter_zero_kill_share` down at least 0.10 from about 0.85;
`eval.hunter_juvenile_death_share` down at least 0.10; `eval.hunter_first_kill_age` lower; `eval.predators_at_end` at least 1
on 12 of 16 seeds; `eval.prey_types_at_end` and `eval.population_mean` not worse.

Mechanisms:

- **Provisioning** (`provisionEnabled`). `provisionYoung` in a new `js/sim/provision.js` runs when a `canHunt` agent stores
  energy in `consumeFood` (meat) or `applyBite`. It scans the spatial-hash cells around the giver (`cellRange`) for
  agents that are its offspring (`parentA` or `parentB` equals the giver's `genomeId`), `canHunt`, below `juvenileTicks`
  and within `provisionRange`, and moves `provisionShare` of the stored energy to them, split equally and capped by each
  young's headroom, with `transferEnergy`. The giver pays only what the young store. Nothing is created.
- **Juvenile dependency** (`juvenileDependency`). `tickCost` in [energy.js](../../js/sim/energy.js) multiplies a juvenile
  hunter's base cost by `juvenileBurn` through `juvenileBurnOf` in a new `js/sim/juvenile.js`. `applyInstinct` adds a kin
  term for `canHunt` bodies (`kin` channel strength to thrust, sine to turn, `kinPullWeight` x weight); the term is part of
  the heritable prior, so selection can remove it from adults.
- Brain layout, `inputCount` and `ARCHIVE_VERSION` do not change.

### Phase 2 - Reflex and practice

**Targets:** eval.hunter_kills_per_life, eval.hunter_zero_kill_share, eval.hunter_kill_rate_by_source.spec-flip, eval.predators_at_end, eval.prey_types_at_end, eval.population_mean

Better means, on 16 seeds with `floorTrigger` 0 and the Phase 1 defaults as the reference: median `eval.hunter_kills_per_life`
at least 1; `eval.hunter_zero_kill_share` at most 0.50 (a further 0.15 down); `eval.hunter_kill_rate_by_source.spec-flip`
at least 0.15 per 1,000 ticks (from 0.060); `eval.predators_at_end` at least 1 on 12 of 16 seeds; `eval.prey_types_at_end`
and `eval.population_mean` not worse.

Mechanisms:

- **Hunter reflex on a flip** (`flipReflex`). `applyInstinct` splits its `canHunt` branch into `applyHunterReflex`; `childBrain`
  in [natural-birth.js](../../js/evo/policies/natural-birth.js) calls it with `instinctWeight` on the child's row, before
  mutation, when the child's specialisation `canHunt` and its mother's does not. The call draws nothing.
- **Reward-gated learning** (`rewardedHebb`). A new `js/sim/reward.js` keeps a per-slot `rewardTrace`: `noteReward` adds
  `min(1, stored / foodEnergy)` when a bite or a meat meal stores energy, and `hebbianStep` multiplies `eta` by
  `rewardBase + rewardGain x trace` and decays the trace by `rewardDecay`. The genome row stays unchanged, so a child does
  not inherit the practice, as `hebbian.js` states.
- Brain layout does not change: both mechanisms write existing weights and read existing outputs. A pooled "carcass" or
  "weakened prey" input would change the layout; it is outside this doc.
