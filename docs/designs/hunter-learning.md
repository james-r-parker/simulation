# Hunter learning

**Status:** Proposed - hunters have time to learn to kill: Phase 1 (parental provisioning, a juvenile dependency with a kin pull, and the hunter reflex on a spec-flip child, with the detectors that show them) is built first; Phase 2 adds reward-gated lifetime learning.
**Validated:** 68d670c (2026-11-05)
**Priority:** 2 - after natural-selection.md, whose Phase 4 is nearly done; the self-sustaining world after E112 keeps hunters alive on about 10 of 16 seeds but a hunter's life has no kill in 80 to 92% of cases, and every budget, placement and scent lever has been spent (user, 2026-11-05)
**Depends on:** [natural-selection.md](natural-selection.md)

On the world after E112 (`maxFood` 3600, short-lived toxic caps) hunters live on carcasses and chance bites and starve:
the median `eval.hunter_kills_per_life` is 0 on all 20 arms of E113 and `eval.hunter_zero_kill_share` is 0.80 to 0.92.
This doc teaches hunters to kill the way nature does: a parent feeds its young while they learn, the young stay near it
and burn less, the motor pattern is present at birth, and practice refines it inside a life. Each mechanism is a
default-off switch, screened on its own and adopted or deleted.

## Context

### Nature model

| Behaviour in nature                                                                                                                                                    | Maps to                                                                                                                                                                |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Wolf pups are fed meat by the pack, carried in the stomach and regurgitated, until they travel with the pack (Mech 1970); lion and tiger cubs eat at the mother's kill | Provisioning: a hunter hands its young a share of each meal                                                                                                            |
| A cheetah mother releases live, weakened gazelle fawns for her cubs to catch; a domestic cat mother brings live prey home (Caro 1994; Caro 1980)                       | The young practise on prey the parent has already brought down; the share is paid from the parent's own meal                                                           |
| Meerkat adults give pups disabled scorpions, then live ones, as the pups age (Thornton and McAuliffe 2006)                                                             | Provisioning runs for a bounded window (`juvenileTicks`), then stops                                                                                                   |
| Predator young stay with the mother for months to years (lion 2 years, cheetah 18 months, wolf 10 months) while their skill grows                                      | Juvenile dependency: a lower burn during the window, and a pull towards kin (heritable, not age-gated; adults follow kin too, a pack-like side effect that is watched) |
| The stalk, chase, pounce and bite sequence is innate and refined by practice; kittens raised without their mother pounce but kill less often (Caro 1980)               | The hunter reflex at birth (`applyInstinct`, and `applyHunterReflex` for a flip child); reward-gated plasticity of the hidden-to-output path (`hebbianStep`)           |
| Practice is reinforced by the meal, not by the attempt                                                                                                                 | A reward trace gates the Hebbian update; no fitness term pays for a kill                                                                                               |

### Selection

[principles.md](../principles.md) rule 7 credits "kin that were fed" to the feeder, and rule 4 forbids restocking a
role. Provisioning moves energy a hunter has already earned to its own young; nothing enters the world. "Measure
scenes, reward them almost never" refuses a fitness or merit term for kills: the doc adds detectors, never a reward.

## Config keys and options

Every switch defaults to false and every behaviour key to its no-op, so the golden hashes do not move until a phase
adopts a switch.

| Key                  | Type    | Default | Meaning                                                                                                                                         |
| -------------------- | ------- | ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `provisionEnabled`   | boolean | false   | Phase 1. A hunter that stores energy from a kill, a bite or a carcass hands `provisionShare` of it to its young in range                        |
| `provisionShare`     | number  | 0.3     | Share of the stored energy moved, in [0, 1]                                                                                                     |
| `provisionRange`     | number  | 120     | Largest centre distance in world units between giver and young                                                                                  |
| `juvenileTicks`      | integer | 3000    | A `canHunt` young is juvenile while `ageTicks` is below this; the age bands of the detectors also use it                                        |
| `juvenileDependency` | boolean | false   | Phase 1. A juvenile `canHunt` body pays `juvenileBurn` of its cost, and every `canHunt` child gets the kin pull floor in `childBrain`           |
| `juvenileBurn`       | number  | 0.6     | Multiplier on the standing, movement and turn cost of a juvenile hunter, in [0, 1]                                                              |
| `kinPullWeight`      | number  | 0.5     | The kin thrust and turn cells of a hunter are raised to at least `kinPullWeight` x `instinctWeight` while `juvenileDependency` is on, in [0, 4] |
| `flipReflex`         | boolean | false   | Phase 1. A child whose specialisation `canHunt` while its mother's does not gets the hunter prey reflex added to its row                        |
| `rewardedHebb`       | boolean | false   | Phase 2. A reward trace scales the Hebbian rate: `eta x (rewardBase + rewardGain x trace)`                                                      |
| `rewardGain`         | number  | 4       | Gain of the trace, in [0, 10]                                                                                                                   |
| `rewardBase`         | number  | 0.25    | Share of the plain rate that applies with no reward, in [0, 1]                                                                                  |
| `rewardDecay`        | number  | 0.02    | Per-tick decay of the trace, in [0, 1]                                                                                                          |

The four booleans are entered in `scripts/flag-ledger.json` with this doc and the task that adopts or deletes each
(T10, T11, T12 and T22 below).

## Phases

### Phase 1 - Provision, depend and flip

**Targets:** eval.hunter_zero_kill_share, eval.hunter_juvenile_death_share, eval.hunter_first_kill_age, eval.hunter_kill_rate_by_source.spec-flip, eval.predators_at_end, eval.prey_types_at_end, eval.population_mean

Better means, on 16 seeds with `floorTrigger` 0: `eval.hunter_zero_kill_share` down at least 0.10 from about 0.85;
`eval.hunter_juvenile_death_share` down at least 0.10; `eval.hunter_first_kill_age` lower;
`eval.hunter_kill_rate_by_source.spec-flip` at least 0.15 per 1,000 ticks (from 0.060); `eval.predators_at_end` at least 1
on 12 of 16 seeds; `eval.prey_types_at_end` and `eval.population_mean` not worse.

Mechanisms:

- **Provisioning** (`provisionEnabled`). `provisionYoung` in a new `js/sim/provision.js` runs once per kill or meal when a
  `canHunt` agent stores energy in `consumeFood` (meat) or `applyBite`. It calls `cellRange` on the spatial hash and copies
  `hash.range` into locals, as `eatAround` does, then scans for agents that are the giver's offspring (`parentA` or
  `parentB` equals the giver's `genomeId`), `canHunt`, below `juvenileTicks` and within `provisionRange`. It splits
  `provisionShare` of the stored energy equally among them, caps each part by the young's headroom, and calls
  `transferEnergy` with the capped amount; `transferEnergy` clamps to the giver's energy and throws only on a non-finite or
  negative amount. The giver pays only what the young store. Nothing is created. `provisionTotals` (a two-slot
  `Float64Array` on the world: energy handed away, energy received) counts both sides; the two are equal by construction
  and `assertInvariants` checks it. The hunter income kinds (`INCOME`) are unchanged: the parent's meal is already income
  once, and a fifth kind would count the same energy twice in `eval.hunter_income_share.*`.
- **Juvenile dependency** (`juvenileDependency`). `juvenileBurnOf` in a new `js/sim/juvenile.js` returns `juvenileBurn` for
  a `canHunt` body below `juvenileTicks` and 1 otherwise. `tickCost` in [energy.js](../../js/sim/energy.js) multiplies the
  base cost and the nominal standing, movement and sprint terms it stores for the ledger (`nominal[0..2]`) by it, so the
  ledger split stays consistent. The kin pull is `applyHunterReflex` raising the `kin` strength-to-thrust and
  sine-to-turn cells to at least `kinPullWeight` x `instinctWeight` (a floor, so repeated calls do not accumulate).
  `childBrain` calls it for every `canHunt` child, and `applyInstinct` calls it for a founder. The pull is not age-gated
  (the age input is `ageTicks / maxAgeTicks`, about 0.04 at 3000 ticks): adults follow kin too, a pack-like cohesion that
  `eval.hunter_kill_rate_by_age.adult` and the population guards watch. The juvenile window applies to the burn only.
- **Hunter reflex on a flip** (`flipReflex`). `applyInstinct` splits its `canHunt` branch into `applyHunterReflex`.
  `childBrain` in [natural-birth.js](../../js/evo/policies/natural-birth.js) calls it with the prey terms, at
  `instinctWeight`, on the child's row before mutation, when the child's specialisation `canHunt` and its mother's does
  not (the trigger is the specialisations, not `birthSource`, so mating and split children are covered alike). The terms
  are added on top of the copied or crossed row and clamp at `WEIGHT_CLAMP`; the flee terms the row carries from a
  non-hunter mother stay and evolution sorts them out. The call draws nothing.
- Brain layout, `inputCount` and `ARCHIVE_VERSION` do not change.

### Phase 2 - Practice

**Targets:** eval.hunter_kills_per_life, eval.hunter_zero_kill_share, eval.predators_at_end, eval.prey_types_at_end, eval.population_mean

Better means, on 16 seeds with `floorTrigger` 0 and the Phase 1 defaults as the reference: `eval.hunter_zero_kill_share`
down at least 0.10 from the Phase 1 result; median `eval.hunter_kills_per_life` not below the Phase 1 result;
`eval.predators_at_end` at least 1 on 12 of 16 seeds; `eval.prey_types_at_end` and `eval.population_mean` not worse.

Mechanism:

- **Reward-gated learning** (`rewardedHebb`). A new `js/sim/reward.js` keeps a per-slot `rewardTrace`: `noteReward` adds
  `min(1, stored / foodEnergy)` when a bite or a meat meal stores energy, and `hebbianStep` multiplies `eta` by
  `rewardBase + rewardGain x trace` and decays the trace by `rewardDecay`. `hebbianStep` changes only `fastOut`, the
  hidden-to-output block, so practice refines the hidden-to-output path and does not rewrite the skip-weight reflex of
  Phase 1. The genome row stays unchanged, so a child does not inherit the practice, as `hebbian.js` states.
- Brain layout does not change. A pooled "carcass" or "weakened prey" input would change the layout; it is outside this doc.
