# Continuous follow

**Status:** Implemented - follow frames a living subject and hands off after death.
**Depends on:** [worker-and-renderer.md](worker-and-renderer.md)

## Behaviour

Follow (`F` or the Follow button) toggles following. Entry chooses the selected living agent, otherwise the highest
interest living agent. Entry sets `targetZoom` to `max(targetZoom, min(canvas.width, canvas.height) / 900)`, clamped to
the existing camera zoom limits. A closer view stays close. Subsequent wheel, pinch and keyboard zoom changes persist.

The camera follows one genome id until death or explicit cancellation. Missing subjects leave the camera at its last
target for 650 ms of visible frame time, then hand off to the highest interest living agent. The delay freezes while
paused or hidden; frame gaps above 100 ms contribute no hold time. A stale selected id falls back immediately on entry. Empty populations end follow. Reset clears
pending handoffs. No packed frame yet is not an empty population and cannot end follow. A second Follow press, Escape with no drawer, Home, drag or keyboard pan cancels follow.

Interest uses existing renderer pursuit pairs and the director's event scorers, with a separate 32-slot follow-interest ring. Each candidate receives the largest
of its pursuit score, a recent event score decaying to zero over 5 seconds, pregnancy (35), or movement (5).
Both pursuit endpoints score 50 plus 30 times closeness. Movement means positive speed. Kill subjects are surviving biters; escape and smoke subjects are the victim
or emitter. Birth and mating subjects receive a 45-point event remembered in the follow-interest ring. Dead ids cannot win.
A proximity bonus of up to 8 points within 600 world units preserves local continuity between comparable subjects.
Ties use distance, then lower genome id. Selection and the inspector stay independent of automatic handoffs.

This serves **A real chase**, **Scavenge**, and **Condition changes the walk** in [principles.md](../principles.md).
It changes the camera only; it adds no fitness reward, sim rule or worker command that affects evolution.

## Verification

Select a living blob and press F: the camera eases into a close view. Adjust zoom and observe it staying fixed.
Wait for death: its last position remains briefly, then a living subject receives the follow ring and the camera eases
across. Repeat with no selection, a stale selection, an empty population, pause during the delay, reset during the delay,
and cancellation during the delay. Home and keyboard/drag pan release follow. Unit tests inject these transitions
without relying on random deaths. Browser smoke checks the real renderer and worker; CI runs the full regression suite.

Local verification: the quick gate passes all eight checks; affected unit suites pass, including the suspended-tab
regression. Chromium browser smoke passes all 21 specs, including F centring a subject for inspector selection.
Fresh conformance review reports no blocking findings. Death, pause, empty-population and cancellation transitions
are verified with injected unit scenarios; natural-death timing is not asserted by the browser suite.
