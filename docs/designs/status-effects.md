> Companion to [body-genes.md](body-genes.md), whose lanes are what a body is for its whole life. This doc owns what changes within a life: an effect multiplies a lane, or closes a gate, for as long as it lasts.

A lane is constant for a life. A body is also cold, tired, full, hurt, sick, in a pack that has just called, or pressed against its kin, and each of those changes what it can do for a while. Today each is a rule of its own in a different file. Here each is one entry of a registry: what puts it on a body, how many times it stacks, how it fades, and which lanes it scales. The readers of a lane read the lane with its effects applied, and nothing else in the sim multiplies a lane.

Scenes served: **Pack, or loner**, **Call, or don't**, **Condition changes the walk**, **Refuge** (the huddle) and the trait **Needs conflict in the same tick** ([principles.md](../principles.md)). Selection rules 7 and 8 bound every social effect: the payoff returns to the actor's offspring, and a cheater must not win.

## The registry (`js/sim/effects.js`)

`EFFECTS` is a frozen list, in `js/sim/effect-list.js`; `js/sim/effects.js` holds the engine that is built from any list (`createEffects`, so the contract test runs it on fixture effects) and the readers bound to `EFFECTS`. `EFFECT_INDEX` (by name), `EFFECT` (by name in `UPPER_SNAKE`) and the per-slot state are built from the list.

Only a `stacks` effect stores anything: `effectStacks[effect][slot]` and `effectTicks[effect][slot]`, both `Float64Array` rows of `world.agentRows`, so they are saved and zeroed by `freeAgent` with the rest of an agent's state. As built, `worldHash` leaves the stored rows (and `world.heardFrom`) out until a social number of `SOCIAL_KEYS` (`js/sim/effect-numbers.js`) is above 0: with every number at 0 the stacks cannot reach the rest of the world, so a neutral world hashes as it did before the effects, and Phase 3's adoption puts them in the hash with its re-pin.

### How a lane is read

`liveOf(world, slot, LANE.X) = world.lanes.X[slot] x the factor of every effect that scales X, in registry order`.

- An effect is evaluated where it is read, from the state as it stands at that point of the tick. A `share` or `step` level is computed then; a `stacks` level is the stored count. This is what keeps today's order: grazing reads this tick's `BITE` output, the passive cost reads the temperature after `stepTemperature`, the fat factor reads the energy after the call costs, and a meal closes the `eat` gate within the same `feed`.
- A gate is read with `gateOpen(world, slot, GATE.EAT)`, which is closed when any effect that names it has a level above 0, read the same way.
- `fadeEffects(world)` is the one pass over stored stacks: it runs as the last entry of `STEP_PHASES` in `js/sim/step.js`, counts each `ticks` timer down and drops a stack of each `every` effect on its tick. A stack gained in tick `t` with `ticks: n` is on through tick `t + n - 1`.
- A reader that takes `liveOf` reads its lane; a reader this doc touches is moved from the config key to its lane in the same commit and the lane is marked `moved` ([body-genes.md](body-genes.md), "The lane catalogue"), so Phase 5 there moves what is left.
- Nothing allocates and nothing draws from the RNG. Factors multiply in registry order, so a world replays bit for bit.

### Appliers (`js/sim/effect-appliers.js`)

A `stacks` effect is raised by `applyEffect(world, slot, EFFECT.X, source)`, called at the site where the cause happens, in the phase it happens in: where a call is heard (`hearOne` in `js/sim/calls.js`), where a bite lands (`js/sim/feeding.js`), where a defender shoves (`tryDefenderRepel`). It adds one stack up to `maxStacks`, restarts a `ticks` timer, and pushes `EffectApplied`. Two effects are raised on a clock instead: `chilled` (in `stepTemperature`) and `huddled` (a pass over the spatial hash every 8 ticks, in slot order, in the signal phase).

### What the body feels

A brain already reads its energy, its temperature bands, `ate`, `bitten`, `pregnant` and what it hears. One body input is added with the layout of [body-genes.md](body-genes.md) Phase 1: `reserve`, the sprint store's level, so a brain can pace a graded sprint. No effect adds another input.

## Every modifier of today, as an effect (Phase 1)

As built, the factors multiply on the lane in registry order, so thrust, turn and the standing cost round differently from the old sequence: the `busy` short golden and the busy and bouncy wasm physics digests moved (the default, disease and sprint-store short goldens did not) and were re-pinned with that commit. A call is two gates, not one, because an alarm and a hunt call each wait on their own timer.

## The social effects (Phase 2)

Each is built with its numbers at 0 (no effect) and switched on by Phase 3, which chooses the numbers with the calculator and reads them under the keep rule. Each row states where its payoff returns and what stops a cheater, as selection rules 7 and 8 require. Kin is `isKin` in `js/sim/kin.js` (a parent, a child or a sibling); T12 reads how often a hunter has kin in call range, and the pack is widened to the lineage only if that share is under 0.1.

Hearing gains an owner: `hearOne` in `js/sim/calls.js` keeps the loudest call's loudness and bearing, and calls `noteHeard` (`js/sim/effect-hear.js`) for every caller and hearer in range. As built, `world.heardFrom` (six numbers a slot: for the alarm and then the hunt half, the caller's slot, its genome id and the tick its call began) keeps the last call that gave the hearer a stack, not the loudest, since `answered` needs the caller that rallied a hunter; a hearer gains a stack once per call, on the tick the call starts (`timers[caller] === callTicks`), not on each tick the call lasts, however many live calls it is in range of; a hearer that comes into range later hears the call and gains no stack. A hunter rallied by two callers answers the later one only. `hoarse` is settled by `settleCalls` (`js/sim/effect-social.js`) after the hearing passes of the tick the call is made: `noteCall` marks `world.callHeard` when a hearer takes the call as a stack, and a call with no mark, or an alarm with no threat in view, raises a stack; a heard alarm that warned of a threat takes one off. The cost is read by `alarm` and `huntCall` as the `alarmCost` and `huntCallCost` lanes through `liveOf`, so it is the price before the call's own stack. A kill that qualifies takes a `hoarse` stack off the caller even when it holds `answered`'s 2 stacks already.

As built in T11: `alert`'s grazing cost is the computed effect `alertGrazing` (its level is the `alert` stacks of a body that grazes this tick; it scales `maxThrust` by `-alertGraze` a stack, times `nerve`), because the `grazing` table reads `grazeThrust` without effects. `staggered` closes `bite`, which a gate cannot make neutral, so `noteShoved` raises it only with `staggerThrust` above 0; the shove itself is logged as `DefenderShoved` (`slot`, `target`) either way, and `bites` in `js/sim/feeding.js` is the reader of the `bite` gate. `huddled` counts a kin inside `huddleRange` times the counting body's size, centre to centre, and a body counts once however many cells it covers; its fed test is the neighbour's energy above half its store. The pass is skipped while `huddleWarmth` is 0, so a neutral world pays no neighbour search and `eval.effect_share.huddled` reads 0 until the warmth is on. `alert` needs no kin: any non-hunter that hears an alarm with a threat in view gains a stack.

## Detectors

Computed by the eval observer in `js/evo/scene-effects.js`; none is a reward.

| Metric                       | Direction      | Reads                                                                                    |
| ---------------------------- | -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `eval.effect_share.<effect>` | watch          | the share of agent-ticks with the effect on, over all bodies (T12 reads it not by spec)  |
| `eval.rally_bite_share`      | watch (higher) | of rallied stacks, the share followed by a bite on prey within `rallyTicks`              |
| `eval.hoarse_call_share`     | watch (lower)  | of calls, the share made at 3 or more `hoarse` stacks                                    |

## The calculator and the levels

- [role-budget.md](role-budget.md) reads effects through `roleParams(config, specName, traits, {effects})`: the fourth argument is the options record that already held `age` and `storeShare`, and `effects` is a level per effect that stores stacks (`rallied: 1`), composed as `liveOf` composes it (`lanesWithEffects` in `js/evo/role-budget-effects.js`: each lane times the `effectFactor` of every effect above level 0, in registry order, so the budget reads the sim's own numbers). With every level 0 the record is today's, for every row. `alertGrazing` and the effects read from the body (the temperature bands, the cooldowns) take no level. The defender's row reads `staggered`, the scout's row `alert`, the sitter's row `chilled`, and a new row `pack:forager` reads two hunters, one rallied, against one alone and prints `packGain`, the catch margin a rally adds.

As built in T13: `budgetPack` in `js/evo/role-budget-pack.js` reads the caller as a lone hunter (every effect off; a caller's own call gives it nothing) and the hearer at `rallied: 1`, each against a forager through `budgetChase`. `packGain` is the better of their broken-bound catch margins minus the lone hunter's, in px (so never below 0), and `packGainTicks` is the same in ticks of the lone hunter's sprint, the unit of `tightTicks`; T15's "`packGain` above `tightTicks`" reads `packGainTicks`. The row is an amplifier, not a gate: `AMPLIFIER_ROWS` is apart from `BUILT_ROWS`, a default `role:budget` prints it after the matrix, `--rows pack:forager` reads it alone, and the gated rows are read with every effect off as before. At the neutral numbers (`rallyThrust` and `rallyBurn` 0) a rally changes no lane, so `packGain` is 0 by construction; on the default arena the lone hunter's forager row is `impossible` (margin -102 px), so any rally gain is read against a negative margin. With `--set rallyThrust=0.5 --set rallyBurn=0.2` the hearer closes (+1220 px) and `packGain` is +1322 px (92 ticks). The rally's `rallyTicks` window and the hunt call's own cost (`hoarse`) are not modelled in the row. T13 asks for no scripted two-hunter scene, so none was built.

As chosen in T15 (candidate arm `evals/role-budget/step5-d.json`, E271, working in `evals/decisions/step5-numbers/results.md`; no default changed, adoption is T18): `rallyThrust` 0.1 and `rallyBurn` 4. On the `step5-b` base the lone hunter's forager row is `ok` turn 73 (+607.8 px) with every effect off, so the rally is margin on a working job. `rallyThrust` 0.05, 0.1, 0.15, 0.2 and 0.3 give `packGain` +60.1 px (4.2 ticks, under `tightTicks` 5), +119.7 (8.3), +177.2 (12.3), +234.1 (16.3) and +349.6 (24.3): 0.1 is the smallest on that grid that clears `tightTicks`, with a 1.7 margin. `rallyBurn` leaves `packGain` unchanged from 0.05 to 8 and moves the hearer's chase cost against the lone hunter's from -11.2 to -1.9; 4 (-6.0) makes a rally cost 7.3 energy over its `rallyTicks` at rest and keeps the chase heat estimate at 4.7 of 15 headroom (8.4 at burn 8, over half). `staggerThrust` 0.5 and `staggerTicks` 30 are not read by the calculator (the defender row reads the shove, not the `staggered` stack, and that row is red with every effect off, so the stagger is margin and never what makes the defence possible); the screen's `eval.effect_share.staggered` is their first reading. The cold stack is `coldStackRate` 0.4 with `entrantEnergy` 700 (400 in `step5-b`): the sitters' idle ratios fall from 1.54 to 1.67 down to 0.96 to 1.08 (forager, scout and reproducer `ok`; predator and defender `tight`), and the four plant eaters' first-meal rows stay `ok`; a rate above 0.4 at 700, or any rate at 400, turns a first-meal row red on `find`. `--check` on the arm exits 1 on five `forager:idle` readings (the formula's cold stack lives 3.9% shorter than the scene's sitter), a drift of the calculator to correct before T18.

## Decisions

| Decision                                                       | Holds because                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| One registry for every within-life modifier                    | The rules of the Phase 1 table sit in `physics.js`, `temperature.js`, `feeding.js`, `toxin.js`, `disease.js`, `juvenile.js`, `sprint-store.js`, `mate-eligibility.js`, `reproduction.js`, `calls.js` and `smoke.js`, each applied its own way; one place makes them testable by contract, visible in the inspector, readable by the calculator, and cheap to add to        |
| An effect scales a lane; it never sets a behaviour             | A scripted mode that plays the life for the brain is refused (`docs/principles.md`); an effect changes what the body can do and the brain still chooses                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| No effect pays for an act                                      | A body paid for calling calls for ever. `rallied` goes to the hearer and costs it; `answered` pays the caller only for a pack-mate's bite on prey; `hoarse` prices the call                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Every effect has a cap                                         | Stacks without a cap make the largest pack unbeatable and the longest sitter immortal; the cap is in the registry and the contract tests assert it                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| A factor is never below 0                                      | The old `fatFactor` could go below 0 only for a `fatSlowShare` lane of 1 or more (the config key is under 1, but a trait or a body override can lift the lane), which would reverse a body's thrust; `1 + perStack x level` is clamped at 0 for every effect, and a test pins the case                                                                                     |
| An effect is read where its rule is read today                 | Today's rules read state made earlier in the same tick (this tick's bite output, the temperature after it steps, a meal that closes the gate at once); one pass at the start of the tick would move each by a tick                                                                                                                                                         |
| Three forms: a factor on a lane, a table of factors, a drain   | A ramp, a band table and a flat energy loss are what the sim has; one form cannot keep all three numbers the same                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Social numbers start at 0 and are chosen with the calculator   | An effect's strength is a balance question; the mechanism lands first so the calculator and the detectors can read it                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| The effects are built before any training                      | Each changes what a trained brain must learn; one retraining follows all of them (user, 2026-11-21)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| A wasted call is what costs (user, 2026-11-21)                 | an alarm nobody heard, or one with no threat in view, stacks `hoarse` and each stack doubles the next call's cost; an honest call that was heard costs nothing extra, so spam burns out while a rare false alarm stays cheap; this is a price on the call, not a reward for an act                                                                                         |
| Effects amplify, the defaults carry the job (user, 2026-11-21) | Every role's job works at the defaults with every effect off ([role-budget.md](role-budget.md) gates its rows that way). The social and adrenaline effects, for example a defender that answers an alarm moving faster, are margin for teamwork once it takes off. Phase 3 sizes each effect as a margin above a row that is already green, never to turn a red row green. |
