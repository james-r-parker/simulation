# Training

**Status:** Proposed - Phase 1 (drills: fixed scenarios that test a genome's skills) is built first; Phase 2 adds the local trainer, Phase 3 puts champions in the live world after the world gate.
**Validated:** 26bc8ed (2026-11-03)
**Priority:** 3 - the drills and the trainer are tools that do not depend on the balance numbers, and the balance round needs trained champions to test whether unskilled founders cause the floor restarts (world-balance.md H10); champions reach the live world only after the world gate (user, 2026-10-16)
**Depends on:** [evolution-rework.md](evolution-rework.md), [evolution-evals.md](evolution-evals.md)

> Companion to [evolution-rework.md](evolution-rework.md), whose Phases 1 and 2 built the genome, the archive and the live policy. This doc replaces its Phase 3.

Training runs locally, headless, on every core. A genome is trained by natural selection in whole worlds: the trainer runs many short worlds on fixed seeds, offers every agent that lived to the archive by the live policy's fitness, and starts the next worlds from the archive. Drills are the exam. Each drill stages one situation (food in view, prey in reach, a hunter closing) in a small arena and counts how often a genome does the thing a viewer would expect. A drill score is a detector: it never enters fitness, merit, mate rank or archive rank ([principles.md](../principles.md), Measure scenes, reward them almost never). Easier worlds come first in a curriculum, so a skill is reachable before the full world tests it; selection in every stage is still survival and offspring.

## Drills

`js/evo/drill-scenarios.js` (new) exports `DRILLS`, a frozen list in the shape of `SCENARIOS` in `js/evo/memory-probe-scenarios.js`: each drill has a `name`, the `scene` it detects, the subject `spec`, `needsHunter`, and `plan(rng, view)` returning a `Plan` that `playTrial` in `js/evo/memory-probe-run.js` runs unchanged with the `intact` control. Placement helpers `heading` and `around` and the arena constants (`CENTRE_X`, `CENTRE_Y`, the 3000 x 4000 arena) are reused from `memory-probe-scenarios.js`.

| Drill            | Scene                  | Subject  | Stage                                                                                                                                    | Won when                                                                         | Max ticks |
| ---------------- | ---------------------- | -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| `food-ahead`     | Leave a dying patch    | forager  | One food at `FOOD_VIEW_SHARE` (0.9) of the subject's view, within 45 degrees of its heading                                              | the subject eats it                                                              | 300       |
| `food-behind`    | Leave a dying patch    | forager  | One food at 0.6 of view, 120 to 180 degrees off the heading (out of the ray fan)                                                         | the subject eats it                                                              | 600       |
| `two-foods`      | Season changes the map | forager  | A toxic item (toxicity 1) at 0.4 of view and a safe item at 0.7 of view, 40 to 80 degrees apart; flora hue gives each its species colour | the first item eaten is the safe one                                             | 600       |
| `prey-ahead`     | A real chase           | predator | One forager driven by `COMBAT_CONTROLLERS.fleer` at `PREY_VIEW_SHARE` (0.6) of view, within 45 degrees of the heading                    | the subject kills it                                                             | 900       |
| `prey-too-big`   | Abort                  | predator | One forager of twice the subject's energy and size, driven by `fleer`, at 0.6 of view                                                    | the subject is alive at the end and did not bite (a bite here is a failed abort) | 600       |
| `hunter-closing` | A real chase           | forager  | One predator driven by `COMBAT_CONTROLLERS.hunter` at `HUNTER_ENERGY`, 500 px away and heading at the subject                            | the subject is alive at the end                                                  | 600       |
| `rock-refuge`    | Refuge                 | forager  | As `hunter-closing`, with a rock ring (the refuge layout) 250 px from the subject on the side away from the hunter                       | the subject is alive at the end and entered the ring at least once               | 900       |

- Each drill runs `--trials` (32) trials from the drill's seeded stream (`fork(createRng(seed), 'drill:<name>')`), so a run replays from seed and subject.
- A genome's score per drill is its win share. Its **edge** is the win share minus that of a random brain of the same specialisation run on the same trials (`randomWeights` from `js/evo/mutation.js` with the drill seed). The reference controller `referenceSubject('memoryless')` from `js/evo/memory-probe-references.js` is printed beside it as the skilled bar.
- A drill whose subject `spec` does not match a genome's specialisation is skipped for that genome and reported as `-`.

`npm run drill -- --champions <dir> | --archive <file> | --policy <name>` (`scripts/drill.mjs`, new; `--drills a,b` (default all), `--seed <n>` (1), `--trials <n>` (32), `--config <file.json>`, `--json`, `--out <path>`, `--workers <n>`). `--champions` loads files through `loadChampions` in `scripts/memory-probe.mjs`; `--archive` takes each specialisation's elite with the highest lower bound; `--policy` drills `--count` (default 8) genomes from a fresh 20000-tick world of that policy. It prints one row per genome: win share and edge per drill, and the mean edge. Trials run on `scripts/lib/worker-pool.mjs`, in index order, so `--workers 1` and `--workers 8` print the same table. Logs go to `.sim/logs/drill-<seed>x<trials>.jsonl` (`DrillTrial` debug per trial, `DrillResult` info per genome and drill).

## Trainer

`npm run train -- --generations <g> [--episodes <e>] [--workers <n>] [--curriculum <file>] [--resume <archive.json>] [--checkpoint-every <n>] --out <dir>` (`scripts/train.mjs`, new, with `scripts/train-worker.mjs`):

- **Policy and fitness.** The trainer runs the live policy (`LIVE_POLICY` in `js/evo/policies/index.js`, `map-elites-v6` today) and scores offers with the fitness that policy names. The archive is `createArchive` from `js/evo/archive.js` with `nCap` `TRAINER_N_CAP` (8).
- **Episodes.** Episode `k` of generation `g` runs on `evals/train-seeds.json` (new: 256 seeds, disjoint from `evals/seeds.json`) entry `(g x e + k) mod 256`, for `EPISODE_TICKS` (60000) ticks, with `initialAgents` 100. Its initial agents and floor entrants come from a frozen copy of the archive taken at the start of the generation. Generation 0 starts from random brains; no scripted controller seeds the archive (user, 2026-10-17).
- **Offers.** An agent is offered when it dies and, at the last tick, while alive, if it was born after `OFFER_AFTER_TICK` (10000) and reached `maturityTicks`. Living agents are offered after the last death, in slot order.
- **Merge.** `runEpisode` in `js/evo/trainer.js` (new) returns the offers in death order, each tagged with `archiveRef` and `isClone`; `mergeEpisode` applies them in episode-index order, so `--workers 1` and `--workers 32` write byte-identical archives. `--episodes` defaults to 32.
- **Curriculum.** `--curriculum <file>` names a JSON list of stages, each `{ "name", "config", "generations" }`, run in order on one archive. `evals/train-curriculum.json` (new) holds `nursery` (`evals/micro-world.json`, 20 generations), `hunt` (`evals/predator-world.json`, 20 generations) and `world` (the default config, the rest of `--generations`). Without `--curriculum` every generation runs the default config. The archive records each stage's `rulesHash` in `stages`; `--resume` checks the `layout` and the current stage's `rulesHash`.
- **Checkpoints.** Every `--checkpoint-every` (10) generations the trainer picks champions as `train:export` would, without writing them, runs every drill on them (seed 1, 32 trials) and a 4-seed eval (`evals/seeds.json` entries 33 to 36, 50000 ticks). `curvePoint` in `js/evo/learning-curve.js` (new) appends a row to `<dir>/curve.jsonl`: `generation`, `stage`, `trainTicks`, `wallSeconds`, `coverage`, `qdScore`, each specialisation's best lower bound, each drill's mean edge, `eval.food_energy_per_1k`, `eval.births_per_1k`, `eval.fed_median_lifespan`, `eval.catch_rate`, `eval.escape_rate`. Checkpoints only read the archive.
- **Plateau.** `npm run train:curve -- <dir>` prints the curve and the plateau generation from `findPlateau`: the first checkpoint after which `qdScore`, `eval.food_energy_per_1k` and the mean drill edge each rise by less than 5% over the next 3 checkpoints, or "no plateau yet".
- **Output.** After each generation `<dir>/archive-<g>.json`, and a `TrainGeneration` log line (generation, stage, coverage, qdScore, wall seconds, CPU share). A failed episode stops the run with exit 1 and `TrainEpisodeFailed`.
- **Export.** `npm run train:export -- --archive <file> --out <dir>` (`scripts/train-export.mjs`, new) writes one champion file per specialisation through `exportChampions` in `js/evo/champion-export.js` and an `index.json`. These files are what `npm run drill -- --champions`, `npm run league` and `npm run probe:memory` read.

## Champions in the live world

Phase 3 only, after the world gate below. `npm run train:export -- --out public/champions/v1/` publishes the champions. `createChampionsV1` in `js/evo/policies/champions-v1.js` (new) is the live policy with one change: when the seeder (`createSeeder` in `js/evo/policies/map-elites-seed.js`) would draw a random brain, it draws a mutated champion of the same specialisation instead. Founders and floor entrants are therefore skilled; births are unchanged.

**World gate** (from evolution-rework.md, kept): world-balance.md's post-feature round is built (T93), its scorecard passes on both seed halves, and the user has watched the default world in the renderer and signed it off.

## Phases

### Phase 1 - Drills

**Targets:** none - a detector tool; its numbers describe genomes and change no default

`drill-scenarios.js`, `scripts/drill.mjs` and `npm run drill`, with the edge against a random brain. The first table is recorded for the live policy and for `referenceSubject('memoryless')` in [sim-notes.md](sim-notes.md).

### Phase 2 - Local trainer

**Targets:** none - champions stay local in this phase; Phase 3 judges them in the live world

The trainer, the curriculum, checkpoints with drills, `train:curve` and `train:export` to a local directory, with the two archive fixes it needs: `seedArchive` in `js/evo/archive.js` stops adding a false 0 sample (it calls `welfordAdd(..., 0)` today), and `createArchive` takes `nCap`. Done when a run of `npm run train -- --generations 40 --curriculum evals/train-curriculum.json --out .sim/train/first` finishes, its curve shows the mean drill edge of the exported champions above 0 on `food-ahead` and `prey-ahead`, and its champions play `npm run league` without error. The curve and drill table go in [sim-notes.md](sim-notes.md).

### Phase 3 - Champions in the live world

**Targets:** eval.food_energy_per_1k, eval.food_energy_per_1k@20k, eval.floor_episodes

After the world gate. Train past the plateau on the gated world, publish `public/champions/v1/`, and compare `champions-v1` with the live policy on 16 seeds x 200000 ticks. Done when `champions-v1` is `better` on `eval.food_energy_per_1k` and `eval.food_energy_per_1k@20k` (new: `eval.food_energy_per_1k` over ticks 0 to 20000, from the observer's checkpoints) and not `worse` on `eval.floor_episodes`, `eval.basin_family_effective` or `eval.predators_at_end`; each champion is admitted with `npm run league -- <file> --admit` and its `eval.champion_elo` recorded. `LIVE_POLICY` moves to `champions-v1` only with the user's sign-off after watching it.

## Decisions

| Decision                                                                                           | Why                                                                                                                             | Who                         |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Drill scores are detectors only                                                                    | A drill rewarded in fitness would breed drill-passers, not animals (principles.md: measure scenes, reward them almost never)    | Planner, from principles.md |
| The curriculum changes the world, not the reward                                                   | Selection stays survival and offspring in every stage                                                                           | Planner, from principles.md |
| Drills, trainer and curve are built before the world gate; champions enter the live world after it | The tools change no rules; champions bake the rules in, so a later rules change means retraining                                | User, 2026-10-16; planner   |
| The trainer starts from random brains                                                              | Scripted behaviour in the archive would bake into every champion                                                                | User, 2026-10-17            |
| Mutation v2 (`map-elites-v1m`) from evolution-rework.md is not carried over                        | The live policy is now `map-elites-v6`; mutation changes belong to [brain-capacity-and-memory.md](brain-capacity-and-memory.md) | Planner                     |
