**Status:** Proposed - Phase 1 T1 and T2 built (#343) and the T5 baseline run (`E142`, 32 floor-on seeds, in Baseline). T3 (`scripts/season-map.mjs`) and T6 (the sim-notes diagnosis and conformance review) are still open; Phase 2 adds the snowline and the green wave behind default-off switches and is unbuilt.
**Validated:** f5c7880 (2026-11-07)
**Priority:** 20 - the Season changes the map scene reads flat (`eval.winter_basin_density` and `eval.winter_pop_ratio` about 1.0), but nothing in the keep rule waits on it; it queues behind the balance and refuge work
**Depends on:** [natural-selection.md](natural-selection.md)

Build the scene **Season changes the map** ([principles.md](../principles.md)): winter in a basin is slower, tighter and nearer shelter, herds follow season-weighted fruiting patches, and summer spreads. Phase 1 measures where agents, food and herds sit by season. Phase 2 gives the map a seasonal gradient so that moving pays. Phase 3 adds shelter and torpor.

## Principle - a season must differ in space before a blob can move for it

The seasons act today as scalars on the whole map. A blob that stays where it is pays the same winter everywhere, so no brain gains from walking, and the detectors read 1.0. Each mechanism below makes one seasonal quantity depend on position. Nothing is added to fitness; a blob that does not move still lives, and a blob that does move eats better or spends less (principles.md, selection rules: nothing rewards the scene itself).

### Diagnosis - why the map stays flat

1. **Uniform scalars.** Every seasonal quantity is global except species windows; a blob gains nothing by changing place (table above).
2. **The cold margin is not a winter feature.** It doubles loss at the same ratio in summer (heat relief) and winter (extra cold), at a fixed width; `eval.winter_shelter_distance` counts the margin edge as winter shelter, which the physics does not make it.
3. **The temperature response is weak.** Winter is 36% hot against 58% in summer ([sim-notes.md](sim-notes.md), the heat-stress entry): eating warms a blob more than winter cools it, so cold rarely slows anyone.
4. **The detectors are coarse.** `eval.winter_basin_density` is a basin count ratio (`wallGaps` 3 of `wallGapWidth` 200), so it moves only when blobs cross the wall. `eval.winter_pop_ratio` mixes births, deaths and the population floor.
5. **The plant pool is capped.** The plant food count sits at `maxFood` in spring to autumn, so the winter `foodScarcity` shows only in winter, and the 20% background food (`foodBackgroundShare`) ignores the season and the map. Floor births are 0 in the precursor runs, so they do not flatten it, but they place uniformly if they occur.
6. **Winter patches are spread thin.** In winter (year phase 0.75 to 1) the weight rests on grass and fungus, but the species of a patch is independent of its place, so the winter food is a scatter of single patches, not a region.

The Baseline section holds the precursor numbers; Phase 1 re-measures them on 32 seeds.

### Nature model

| Observation in nature                                                                                                             | What the world needs                                                         | Mechanism here                               |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Ungulates track the wave of spring green-up and surf it uphill or poleward (green wave; Drent and Prins 1987; Merkle et al. 2016) | Fruiting that starts at different times in different places                  | `fruitWaveShift`                             |
| Wildebeest follow rain and grass quality gradients (Holdo, Holt and Fryxell 2009)                                                 | A food gradient the herd can climb                                           | `fruitWaveShift`, herds follow `patchWeight` |
| Deer and elk leave deep snow for low, sheltered winter range (Parker and Robbins 1984; Hobbs 1989)                                | Winter cost and winter food that depend on place, and a place that is better | `snowlineGrow`, `snowRegrow`                 |
| Thermal cover cuts winter heat loss under conifers and in lee ground (Parker and Robbins 1984)                                    | Cheaper standing cost inside shelter                                         | `winterRefugeDrain` (Phase 3)                |
| Torpor and hibernation cut winter energy use in small animals (Geiser 2004)                                                       | A cheaper resting state in winter                                            | `winterTorpor` (Phase 3)                     |
| Migration also escapes predators and density limits (Fryxell and Sinclair 1988; Fryxell, Greever and Sinclair 1988)               | Nothing new: hunters already follow prey                                     | Detector only (`eval.herd_season_shift`)     |
| Births are timed to the season of plenty (birth pulses)                                                                           | Built: `reproductionBonus` in the season table                               | None                                         |

## Config keys and options

All default to the off value (`torporSpeed` and `torporFactor` are inert while `winterTorpor` is false); the golden hashes stay unchanged until a verdict task sets a default.

| Key                 | Type    | Default | Meaning                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------- | ------- | ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `snowlineGrow`      | number  | 0       | Share by which the cold margin widens in winter, `coldMarginWidth` x (1 + `snowlineGrow`); 0 is off; range 0 to 1 and the margins may not meet (`terrainProblem`) |
| `snowRegrow`        | number  | 1       | Patch regrowth multiplier for a patch inside the winter margin, on top of `winterRegrow`; 1 is off; range 0 to 1                                                  |
| `fruitWaveShift`    | number  | 0       | Year-phase delay of a patch's fruiting window from the north edge to the south edge, as a share of a year; 0 is off; range 0 to 0.5                               |
| `winterRefugeDrain` | number  | 1       | Phase 3: multiplier on the season's `energyDrain` for an agent inside a refuge ring in winter; 1 is off; range (0, 1]                                             |
| `winterTorpor`      | boolean | false   | Phase 3: a winter agent below `torporSpeed` of `maxVelocity` pays its standing cost times `torporFactor`                                                          |
| `torporSpeed`       | number  | 0.1     | Phase 3: the speed fraction below which torpor applies; range (0, 1)                                                                                              |
| `torporFactor`      | number  | 0.6     | Phase 3: the standing-cost multiplier in torpor; range (0, 1]                                                                                                     |

Each key is a rule in `js/sim/config-rules.js` and a default in `js/sim/config-defaults.js`; `winterTorpor` is a boolean, so T20 adds its `scripts/flag-ledger.json` entry (plan this doc, task T23) in the commit that adds it.

## Detectors

All are `watch` metrics read at the 1000-tick checkpoints from `BURN_IN_TICKS` on, in the same code path as `scene-season.js`.

| Metric                        | Reads                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Scene direction                                       |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| `eval.season_map_distance`    | Total variation distance between the agent occupancy grids (16 columns by 9 rows) of winter and summer checkpoints, minus the same distance between two halves of the summer checkpoints (the noise floor); `null` without both | Higher: the map moves with the season                 |
| `eval.winter_band_shift`      | Share of alive agents inside the cold margin (at the winter width) at winter checkpoints minus the same share at the others                                                                                                     | Negative: agents leave the snowline in winter         |
| `eval.winter_patch_proximity` | Share of alive agents within `foodPatchRadius` of a patch with positive weight, winter minus the others                                                                                                                         | Higher: winter concentrates on the patches that fruit |
| `eval.herd_season_shift`      | Total variation distance between the herd centroid grids of winter and summer checkpoints, minus the summer half-against-half floor                                                                                             | Higher: herds follow the seasonal food                |

`eval.winter_basin_density`, `eval.winter_pop_ratio` and `eval.winter_shelter_distance` stay as guards that must not turn `worse`; T2 makes `shelterDistance` in `scene-season.js` read the winter margin width so the guard follows the snowline.

## Decisions

| Decision                                                                                                                                      | Why                                                                                                                 | Who       |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| Detectors and a baseline come before any mechanism                                                                                            | The current two metrics cannot tell "no movement" from "no gradient"; the noise floor decides what an arm must beat | Delegated |
| The cause is the missing spatial gradient, so the first mechanisms are gradients (snowline, green wave)                                       | Nature: animals move where the season differs by place; the code has no such difference                             | Delegated |
| The snowline reuses the cold margin and `coldFactor`, widened in winter, not a second terrain                                                 | One existing structure, already drawn and already a detector input; fewer new keys                                  | Delegated |
| The green wave shifts only the patch fruiting window by `patchY`; herds and blobs follow through `patchWeight`                                | Herds are environment, not brain-equipped agents; no herd code changes; the existing `patchScore` is the follower   | Delegated |
| No calendar input comes back                                                                                                                  | The sense stays local: temperature and food carry the season                                                        | Delegated |
| No fitness reward for moving or for winter presence                                                                                           | principles.md: no reward per scene; a gradient pays through energy                                                  | Delegated |
| The `maxFood` cap and the background-food share are tested as arms (`foodBackgroundShare` 0.1, a larger `maxFood`) and not changed by default | The pool sits at the cap in three seasons (diagnosis 5); the Phase 1 numbers decide whether an arm is worth a key   | Delegated |
| Phase 3 is conditional on the refuge sense                                                                                                    | Winter shelter is only used by a blob that can find a ring; today prey are 1,400 to 2,400 px from one               | Delegated |
| Every switch key is off at its default and a loser's key and code are deleted at its verdict                                                  | AGENTS.md "Delete what a switch replaces"                                                                           | Delegated |
| Priority 15                                                                                                                                   | Slot after the queued balance, refuge and training work; the user has not asked for this scene ahead of them        | Delegated |
