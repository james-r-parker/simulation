# Megafauna

**Status:** Proposed - wandering megafauna herds (large, slow, scripted bodies that graze, dung, carry seeds, trample by speed and leave huge carcasses), built in four phases on their own branches after flora; Phase 1 (herds that walk, nudge, stampede and die) first.
**Validated:** 9d63367 (2026-10-30)
**Priority:** 3 - built after flora (Priority 2), which it extends: it reads flora's patch weights, per-item lifetimes, fertile-zone fungus and seeding path (user, 2026-10-30)
**Depends on:** [flora.md](flora.md), [natural-selection.md](natural-selection.md)

The wandering obstacles were deleted in the big bang (`4260127`): they moved in straight lines, ignored the world and killed by bumping. This doc brings back large moving bodies as herds of megafauna: slow animals that move in loose groups between food patches, flee hunters, graze, leave dung, carry seeds, age and die. A blob cannot bite them and they have no brains. They are the climate of the map: a blob that does not track where a stampede is heading is trampled, a blob that follows a herd finds the food its dung grows, and a dead animal is a feast.

The movement skill is the point. A pooled ray gives position and not velocity, so a blob has to predict where a body will be from two looks, which is what the memory cells are for. Dodging a stampede is a few ticks, following a herd is seasons ([principles.md](../principles.md), "Time has more than one speed").

The scenes it serves ([principles.md](../principles.md)): **Scavenge** (a herd carcass is the largest meal in the world), **Season changes the map** (herds follow the fruiting weights, so their routes turn with the year), **Cross or stay** (herds file through the wall gaps and carry seeds across), **Refuge** (the rock rings are too tight for a herd animal), **A real chase** (a hunter near a herd starts a stampede that cuts across both of them), **Boom and bust** (a herd strips a patch, moves on, and the patch regrows), **Other blobs are the climate**, and **Something unnamed**. It serves the selection rule "Luck is not skill" by making contact avoidable: a trample is a dodge that was not made, and `eval.dodge_skill` reads it. It adds no fitness term: a trampled blob loses energy and may die, a blob that eats dung-grown food or a herd carcass is fed, and nothing else reaches selection ([principles.md](../principles.md), "Measure scenes, reward them almost never"). The herd animals are not agents: they are fixtures with scripted motion, like the seasons, so the refusal of "a scripted mode that bypasses the brain" does not apply.

Scope: the herd model in `js/sim/`, contact damage and its death cause, the ninth pooled ray channel and its instinct, grazing, dung and seed carrying, the detectors, and the look in `js/app/renderer/`. Out of scope: a herd animal with a brain, a genome or any evolution; a blob bite on a herd animal; trampling of food; predators that hunt herds as prey; a second herd species.

## The herds

A herd is a group of `herdSize` animals. The world holds `herdCount` herds (0 turns the feature off) split over the two basins in alternation, so a basin holds `ceil` or `floor` of half of them. An animal is a circle of radius `herdRadius` with a velocity, an age, a life span, an energy store and a group.

| Part      | Behaviour                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Placement | `createHerds(world)` in `js/sim/herds.js`, called by `createWorld` after `createPatches`: a centre in the herd's basin at least 3 `herdRadius` from every rock (a `placeClear` rule over the basin's x range, at most `placementTries` draws), the animals in a ring round it, each with an age drawn in [0, `herdLifeTicks`) so the herd is not one generation. | Route     | The group's target is the alive patch with the highest `patchWeight / (1 + d / (4 foodPatchRadius))` for its centroid distance `d`, other than the current one, lowest index on a tie. The group stays `herdStayTicks` after its centroid is within `foodPatchRadius` of the target, then chooses again. Fruiting weights move with the year, so the route turns with it. ### Stampede

Every 8th tick, staggered by group index, `senseHunters` loops the alive agents whose specialisation `canHunt` (`specParams` in `../../js/sim/spec-params.js`) and starts a stampede on any group that has an animal within `herdStartleRadius` of one: `groupAlarm` becomes `herdStampedeTicks` and the group's flee vector is the unit vector from the nearest hunter to its centroid. While `groupAlarm` is above 0 the desired speed is `herdRunSpeed`, the heading is the flee vector bent by the repulsion terms, and the group does not graze. The alarm ticks down, and a hunter still in range starts it again. `HerdStampede` logs each start.

A walking herd moves at `herdWalkSpeed` 1.5 and a stampede at `herdRunSpeed` 6.5, a little below a blob's `maxVelocity` 8. An animal at 6.5 closes a 300 px sight in under 50 ticks, and a blob that sees it late and in a straight line cannot clear a lane 160 px wide. A blob that moves across the heading, or into a refuge, can. Herds that startle at a hunter drive a stampede over the prey the hunter is after, and over the hunter.

### Contact

`trampleHerds(world, slot)` in `js/sim/trample.js` runs in `integrate` after the rock bounce. For every alive animal whose circle overlaps the blob's: the blob is pushed out along the centre normal, `vn = (v_blob - v_animal) . n` is the relative normal speed, and when `vn < 0` the blob's velocity is reflected by `v -= 2 vn n` and it loses

`trampleDamage x (closing / maxVelocity)^2 x (size / baseSize)`, where `closing = -vn`,

as `LOSS_CAUSE.TRAMPLE`. A walking herd (closing 1.5) costs 14 energy at the default 400, a stampede head on (closing 6.5 at size `baseSize`) 264, and the same speed against a fat blob of size 40 costs 420. A blob running away at the herd's speed has a closing speed near 0 and takes nothing. A blob pinned against a rock by a herd is pushed every tick and takes the damage every tick. `deathCause` returns the new `DEATH_CAUSE.TRAMPLED` when the last loss cause of an energy death is `TRAMPLE`; `AgentDied` carries the animal's stable `herdId` in the field that holds the killer's genome id for `EATEN`.

## The ninth sense

The herd body is cast against like a rock. Phase 1 writes it into the obstacle channel (`CH_OBSTACLE` in `../../js/sim/ray-march.js`, via a new `HERD` hit kind ranked after `SMOKE` in `../../js/sim/ray-gather.js`), so a blob sees a large moving rock and can already dodge it, with no layout change and no archive break. Phase 2 gives it its own channel.

- **Channel.** `RAY_CHANNELS` goes from 9 to 10 with `CH_MEGAFAUNA` 9 (the new flag appended, so no earlier offset moves). `POOLED_CHANNELS` gets a ninth name, `megafauna`, after `smoke`: the ray part of the pooled row is nine channels of two hits of strength, cosine and sine, 54 values. A herd hit writes the flag 1 and the proximity as every non-agent hit does; `poolRays` pools it with the unchanged code. It is not a threat, prey, kin or other, so no existing channel changes meaning, and the animal blocks rays behind it as a rock does.
- **Layout.** `POOLED_VALUES` is `POOLED_CHANNELS.length x POOLED_STRIDE + FOOD_HUE_VALUES` = 56 (the two hue values move to indices 54 and 55) and `inputCount` 89 (56 pooled plus 33 body). `rawInputCount` is `rayCount x 10 + 33`. `inputOffsets.bodyStart` and every `wSkip` row follow the constants.
- **Genome.** `weightCount` grows by `6 x hidden + 6 x geneOutputs` floats (the inputs into `wIn` and the skip rows). The Genome sizes table of [brain-capacity-and-memory.md](brain-capacity-and-memory.md) gets the row below, and `ARCHIVE_VERSION` in `../../js/evo/archive-codec.js` goes from 5 to 6 (an archive or champion of another length or version is refused, naming the field).
- **Velocity is not sensed.** A blob learns where a body is, not where it goes. The two memory cells and the leak genes carry the last positions; `eval.dodge_skill` is the measure of whether they do.
- **Input activity.** A channel that is silent for most blobs would be read as inactive by the `weightDecay` mask (active share under 0.01 over 10,000 samples) and its weights would decay at every birth, removing the instinct. Phase 2 requires the `eval.input_active_share` of the six megafauna values to be at least 0.02 at the defaults; `herdCount` and `herdSize` are screened with it.

### Instinct

`applyInstinct` adds a wary reflex to every body, hunters included: thrust on the strength of the strongest megafauna hit and a turn away on its sine, each at `herdWary` x `weight` (`herdWary` 0.5, so half the strength of the threat reflex). No reflex approaches a herd: following one, which pays only through dung food and carcasses, has to evolve against the wary reflex. The terms are added after the random fill with no draws, so `instinctWeight` 0 and `herdWary` 0 leave a genome bit-identical.

## Grazing, dung and seeds

Phase 3. The herd eats, fertilises and plants through flora's mechanisms by name.

| Rule     | Behaviour                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Grazing  | `grazeHerds(world)` in `js/sim/herd-graze.js`: a calm animal whose `herdGraze` timer is 0 eats the nearest alive plant item (`FOOD_KIND.PLANT`, of any species) overlapping its circle: the item is removed, `depletePatchAt` takes `foodPatchDepletion` from the item's patch as when a blob eats, `herdStore` gains `herdAssimilation` x the item value up to `herdBodyEnergy`, and the animal's `herdGraze` timer is set to `herdGrazeTicks`. Nothing is booked in the energy ledger: the item's energy never entered it. A stampeding animal does not graze |
| Leaving  | A group leaves its patch before `herdStayTicks` when the patch's `patchWeight` has fallen below `herdLeaveShare` of its value on arrival: it moves on from a patch it has eaten down, as a blob should                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Dung     | Every `herdDungTicks` an animal with a store above half of `herdBodyEnergy` adds a fertile zone at its place through `addFertileZone` with `size` `herdDungSize`. Zone items then spawn by the unchanged rule (`fertileZoneSpawnChance`), grass all year and fungus in its window, so a trail of food follows the herd. The zones count against `fertileZoneMax` and are the first to be replaced (least fertility)                                                                                                                                             |
| Seeds    | An animal that grazes an item of a pollinated species (flora's `pollenChance` above 0) loads a seed with probability `herdSeedChance`: `herdSeedPatch` (patch + 1), `herdSeedSerial` and `herdSeedTicks` (`herdSeedTicks`, counted down). At its next dung drop, with a valid seed, at least `herdSeedMinDistance` from the source patch and no alive patch within `foodPatchRadius`, `plantSeedling` plants a patch there and clears the seed                                                                                                                  |
| Seedling | `plantSeedling(world, {x, y, parent})` in `js/sim/pollination.js` is the part of flora's `seedPatch` that takes a free `patchAlive` slot, copies the parent's traits through `mutateTraits` (one parent, so no blend) and sets stock `seedStock` x `foodPatchCapacity`; `seedPatch` calls it too. It draws from flora's `flora` stream. `SeedingRefused` carries a `source` field (`pollen` or `herd`) and the reason `pool` or `near`; `HerdSeeded` logs a planted seed                                                                                        |

Herds carry seeds across the wall: a herd that crosses a gap plants the species of one basin in the other. A seed is not a free patch: it needs a pollinated species, a distance and a free slot, and the seedling is as exposed to `patchDieTicks` as any other.

## Phases

### Delivery

Megafauna is built after flora is `Implemented`, in four phases, each on its own branch from the latest `origin/main`. Phase 1 has two chunks (AGENTS.md, "Build in chunks"), each its own branch and PR; the other phases have one. Each chunk pins the short goldens in each behaviour commit and re-pins the long goldens once at the end of its code (Node, then `npm run browser:hash` in Chromium), and each phase re-runs the survival gate on its final world before its last PR. A numeric value is a config key; a boolean with one chosen value is not a key. Runs and timing stay out of code chunks. The conformance review closes each phase.

## Decisions

| Decision                                                                                                                                                                   | Why                                                                                                                                                                      | By      | Date       |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------- | ---------- |
| Wandering megafauna herds: large, slow bodies in loose herds on seasonal routes, as a movement-skill device                                                                | Dodging needs prediction, and a pooled sense gives position without velocity, so memory pays; the old wandering obstacles were the unnatural form of the same idea       | User    | 2026-10-30 |
| Contact is a trample whose damage scales with the animal's speed relative to the blob and the blob's size: a walking herd nudges, a stampede kills; death cause `trampled` | The harm has to be avoidable and graded, or a contact is luck and not skill                                                                                              | User    | 2026-10-30 |
| Herds stampede when a hunter comes within `herdStartleRadius`, the way grazing herds flee predators                                                                        | Prey animals bolt together when a predator comes near; the trigger is a fact of the world, not a timer, so a hunter can drive a herd and a stampede can catch the hunter | Manager | 2026-10-30 |
| Herds graze plants (competing with blobs), fertilise behind them (dung zones where food and fungi sprout) and carry seeds (planted at dung drops through flora)            | Megaherbivores shape the vegetation they cross: they eat it, fertilise it and disperse its seeds; blobs can learn to follow them                                         | User    | 2026-10-30 |
| Herds do not trample food                                                                                                                                                  | Not chosen: food stays a blob's concern                                                                                                                                  | User    | 2026-10-30 |
| A ninth pooled ray channel, `megafauna` (2 hits x strength, cosine, sine): +6 pooled values and inputs (83 to 89), a Genome sizes row, `ARCHIVE_VERSION` 6, a re-pin       | A herd is a class of its own: not a threat, prey or rock. | Herd animals are born without a brain, a genome or evolution; they age and occasionally die, leave a huge carcass, and the herd recovers slowly by births                  | The herd is climate, not a lineage; its carcass is a feast for hunters and scavengers and later for fungi (flora)                                                        | User    | 2026-10-30 |
| `bench.step_ms@500` within 15% of the baseline at the time and the browser frame rate held at 4K; herd count, size and speed are config knobs                              | The sim and the draw run near their budgets, and the knobs are the screen's levers                                                                                       | User    | 2026-10-30 |
| Built after flora, in phases on their own branches; Priority 3                                                                                                             | Herds read flora's patch weights, item lifetimes, fertile-zone fungus and seeding path                                                                                   | User    | 2026-10-30 |
| Phase 1 shows the animal on the obstacle channel, and Phase 2 splits it into its own                                                                                       | A blob that cannot see a stampede cannot dodge it, and a trample it cannot sense is luck; the split waits until the dodge has a baseline                                 | Manager | 2026-10-30 |
| An innate wary reflex (away from the strongest megafauna hit, at `herdWary` 0.5 of the instinct weight), and no innate approach                                            | Prey animals are born wary of large moving bodies; following a herd for its dung has to evolve against the reflex, and the cost of a wrong first guess is a trample      | Manager | 2026-10-30 |

| Blobs cannot bite or kill a herd animal                                                                                                                                    | The herd is climate; a bite would make it prey and a hunting scene of its own                                                                                            | Manager | 2026-10-30 |
