> Companion to [senses-and-motor.md](senses-and-motor.md), whose `npm run audit:chase` oracles are the first cross-check, and to [body-genes.md](body-genes.md), which lets the budget read a spec's gene ranges.

The sim has five specialisations (`SPECIALISATION_KEYS` in `js/sim/config-spec-rules.js`). Each has a job: the predator catches, the defender protects the herd, the scout warns, the forager and the reproducer eat and breed. Whether a job is possible with the numbers in the config is arithmetic first and evolution second. A hunter whose sprint empties before it reaches the prey cannot be trained to catch it; a defender that cannot reach a caller before the hunter's last bite cannot protect it.

`npm run role:budget` computes, for each role, a closed-form budget from the config alone (no world, no RNG, no brain). The budget names every step of the job, says whether a perfect performer completes it, and gives each step a margin: how imperfect the performer may be at that step. The result is a matrix. A red cell names the number that makes a role impossible. The calculator is a detector and a tuning tool, never fitness, merit or rank.

Scenes served: **A real chase** (the hunter's budget), and every scene where a role is judged possible before it is trained for ([principles.md](../principles.md)). Selection rule 6 (hunting wins by the chain) is served by Phase 1.

## The matrix

Rows are a performer against the pressure on it; columns are the steps. A cell holds the margin of that step, or `-` when the step is not part of the job.

| Row                   | Steps used                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Phase |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----- |
| `predator:forager`    | see, turn, run, bite                                                                                                                                                                                                 | 1     |
| `predator:reproducer` | see, turn, run, bite                                                                                                                                                                                                 | 1     |
| `predator:scout`      | see, turn, run, bite                                                                                                                                                                                                 | 1     |
| `predator:defender`   | see, turn, run, bite                                                                                                                                                                                                 | 1     |
| `predator:average`    | the weighted mean of the four rows above (a hunter that cannot tell its targets apart)                                                                                                                               | 1     |
| `predator:best`       | the row with the largest slack (a hunter that picks its target)                                                                                                                                                      | 1     |
| `defender:predator`   | hear, turn, run, intercept, smoke (the defender's and the caller's)                                                                                                                                                  | 2     |
| `scout:predator`      | see, hear, warn, turn, run                                                                                                                                                                                           | 3     |
| `forager:predator`    | eat, breed, run                                                                                                                                                                                                      | 4     |
| `reproducer:predator` | eat, breed, run                                                                                                                                                                                                      | 4     |
| `predator:living`     | eat, breed (a hunter's life: do its kills pay for its chases and reach the split gate)                                                                                                                               | 4     |
| `<spec>:first-meal`   | reach, find, eat (each of the five specs, from where and with what it is born, to its first good meal)                                                                                                               | 4     |
| `<spec>:idle`         | sit (a body that never moves, against one that searches: the sitter dies first, or the row is red)                                                                                                                   | 4     |
| `<spec>:herd`         | see, turn, run, survive (each of the five specs against a stampeding herd)                                                                                                                                           | 5     |
| `pack:forager`        | two hunters, one rallied by the other's call, against one alone: `packGain` ([status-effects.md](status-effects.md) T13); an amplifier read beside the gated rows with the effect on, never part of the gate (built) | 1     |

A cell is `{margin, unit, distance, status, binding}`. `margin` is the slack in ticks. `status` is `ok` (margin at least `tightTicks`), `tight` (0 to under `tightTicks`), `impossible` (a perfect performer fails; margin `null`) or `n/a`. `binding` names the step whose slack is smallest in the row. A row is `impossible` when any of its cells is.

A step's slack is found by bisection on the row's timeline: the timeline takes a `delays` option (ticks added at one step, the others perfect) and the slack is the largest delay for which the job still completes, capped at `SLACK_CAP` (200, printed `>200`).

`--mix forager=0.6,reproducer=0.2,scout=0.1,defender=0.1` sets the weights of the average (default: equal, since a seeded population draws its specialisation uniformly). `predator:average` counts a prey as zero slack when it is `impossible` (every step of the row is `impossible` when no prey in the mix can be caught) and reports `catchableShare`, the mix share of prey the hunter can catch at all; `predator:best` reports which prey it picks. `choiceGain` is the best row's slack minus the average's, in ticks: the margin a hunter gains by telling its targets apart, which [body-genes.md](body-genes.md) "The body sense" can buy it.

## Kinematics (`js/evo/role-budget-kinematics.js`)

Exact for a body moving on a line or holding a steady turn; each is tested against `bodyTable` in `js/evo/chase-body.js`. With the sprint store on the sprint is graded ([fair-chase.md](fair-chase.md), "Graded sprint"): an effort `e` from 0 to 1 scales the sprint's thrust factor, its rise of the speed cap, its drain and its energy cost, so every function below takes `e` (1 for a perfect performer) and `burstTicks` is `(1 - sprintFloor) / (drain x e)`.

| Function                     | Closed form                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `speedStep(v, a, d, cap)`    | `min(cap, (v + a) d)`: the speed law of `actAll` then `moveAgentsKernel` for a straight run, where `a` is the thrust of the gait (the sprint factor included while sprinting, so the cap and the thrust both rise); uncapped steady speed `a d / (1 - d)`                           |
| `ticksToSpeed(a, d, cap)`    | the first `n` with `a d (1 - d^n) / (1 - d)` at the cap, from rest                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `turnCircle(a, d, w)`        | steady speed on a held turn `a d / sqrt(1 - 2 d cos(w) + d^2)`, radius that over `w`: 21.54 and 41.72 (cruise) and 32.31 and 75.09 (sprint) for the forager and the predator at the defaults. `bodyTable` settles for 80 ticks and reads 21.51, 41.81, 32.26 and 75.26, inside 0.5% |
| `swingTicks(phi, w)`         | `phi / w`: ticks to turn the velocity through `phi` once the circle is steady                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `burstTicks(store)`          | `(1 - sprintFloor) / drain` ticks at full factor (140 for the `courser` predator), `1 / drain` ticks to empty (200); infinite with the store off                                                                                                                                    |
| `refillTicks(store)`         | `(1 - level) / sprintRefill` ticks, counted only while `SPRINT` is at or under 0 and `THRUST` is under `sprintRestThrust` (0.2): a cruising body does not refill                                                                                                                    |
| `sprintFactor(level, floor)` | 1 at or above the floor, `level / floor` under it (`storeFactor`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |

## Phase 1 steps - the hunter against a prey (`js/evo/role-budget-chase.js`)

A perfect hunter creeps at cruise until the prey sees it (`seeLead`), then turns to the prey's bearing (`swingTicks`), sprints while the store lasts (a burst spent after the prey is aware, never before), closes the gap, and bites once on each contact tick until the prey is under its kill line. The prey flees straight at its sprint cap while its store lasts, then at its cruise cap.

`chaseTimeline(hunter, prey, options)` is a scalar recurrence, not a closed form (at most `MAX_TICKS` of 250 iterations, no world). Each tick, for each body: read `factor = storeFactor(level)` before the drain; `k = 1 + sprintMultiplier x sprintBoost x factor x e`; `v = min(cap x k, (v + a x k) x d)`; advance the store; subtract `tickCost` from the energy and recompute the size and the fat factor from it. Contact is the centre gap under `size(h) + size(p)`. As built it is a 2D loop of both bodies (position, velocity and heading, the `pursuerBrain` and `evaderBrain` rules of `js/evo/chase-oracles.js` read from the same snapshot, `js/evo/role-budget-play.js`), so the hunter's speed falls to the held-turn speed in a turn (4.38 for the predator at cruise) and recovers over about 30 ticks. The order of a tick is the sim's: the store, `actAll`, `moveAgentsKernel`, the contact test (`resolveContacts`: the impulse with `restitution` and the separation), a bite when the prey is in the jaws, then `tickCost`. It returns `{contactTick, killTick, bites, gate, hunterEnergy, preyEnergy, hunterSpent, income, kick, hunterLevel, speed, ticks, heat}`; the kill is the tick of the bite that leaves the prey at or under its kill line, which is `contact + bitesToKill - 1` on a straight chase and later, or never, when the prey breaks away from the bites. Each term:

| Step | Perfect performance                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Cost of a delay `d`                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| see  | the hunter acts the tick the prey is inside `viewReach` (or `reliableView` with `--reliable`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | the prey, once aware, runs `d` ticks at its flee speed before the hunter moves                                                                                                                                                    |
| turn | the hunter's heading is on the prey after `swingTicks(angle, w)`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | `d` extra ticks of the hunter at the turn circle's speed (`turnCircle`), below its straight speed                                                                                                                                 |
| run  | the sprint starts when the prey is aware and ends when the store hits the floor                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | the sprint starts `d` ticks late, so the burst ends later in the chase                                                                                                                                                            |
| bite | the size gate holds at contact (`size(h) > huntSizeRatio x size(p)`, both sizes from the energies the timeline carries; else the pair is `impossible` at this step), the hunter faces the prey inside `biteArc` (`inJaws`), it may eat (`canEatNow`: under `satiationShare` and past `digestTicks`), and it lands one bite on each contact tick; the bodies collide and the hunter closes again, so the kill lands at `contact + bitesToKill - 1`, with `bitesToKill` from the prey's energy at contact, not at the start | each tick of delay is a tick of `strikeMissCost` with `BITE` held and nothing in the jaws, and a tick the prey's flight burns nothing extra; the slack is the ticks until the hunter's energy or the chase's `MAX_TICKS` runs out |

Two bounds are reported for every pair, because the escape depends on the prey's play:

| Bound      | Prey play                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Reads                                                                                           |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `straight` | flees straight; exact                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | An upper bound on any hunter's catches                                                          |
| `broken`   | the oracle evader's play (`evaderBrain` in `js/evo/chase-oracles.js`), by start bearing: the 2D loop of the hunter against a prey that breaks through `breakAngle` each time the gap falls under `JINK_RANGE`, run for each of the 8 bearings of `audit:chase`; the rear bearings are the ones a hunter loses | A bound checked by sign and rank against the audit per bearing, never a prediction of its share |

`maxCatchableGap(hunter, prey, frame)` is how far a kill reaches around the awareness gap. A kill is not monotone in the gap (a prey that sprints only when the hunter is within 360 px, or has spent its burst, is caught from further), so a bisection on the start gap is not enough: it walks outward from `gapAtAwareness` in steps of 15 px while a kill lands and bisects the first gap that fails, and when no kill lands there it walks inward to the nearest gap that kills (0 when none does down to 40 px). `catchMargin = maxCatchableGap - gapAtAwareness`, where `gapAtAwareness = viewReach(prey, PI) + size(hunter)` is how close the hunter can creep unseen to a prey that faces away (the audit's prey always does): positive when the perfect hunter kills at the awareness gap, negative by how much closer it would have to be. The headline `catchable` is `catchMargin >= 0` on `broken` with the hunter facing the prey; the margin at each of the eight bearings of `audit:chase` is read beside it (`bearings`: the margins, the share that close and the worst), and the `straight` margin is printed beside both.

Also reported per pair (not slack cells): `chaseEnergy` (the cost of the timeline from `tickCost`'s terms against the energy the hunter holds above its `birthReserve` and the bite energy a kill returns, plus the kick `biteCost x sizeRatio x fullness x kickMultiplier` of each of the `bitesToKill` bites, `kickOf`), `chaseHeat` (temperature gained over the timeline against the headroom to `temperatureOptimalMax`), and `refillTicks` after a spent burst.

### Performer tiers: possible is not learnable

The `courser` arm is possible for a scripted hunter (E253: a catch at every start distance) and not learnable from random brains (E255: 1 of 8 populations). Every predator row is computed for three performers, and the gate reads all three.

| Tier       | Play                                                                                                                                      | Reads                                    |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| `perfect`  | creeps unseen, turns at once, sprints only once the prey is aware at the best effort of a two-pass sweep (0.05, then 0.01 near the best)  | whether the job is possible at all       |
| `reactive` | no creeping: heads for the prey and sprints at effort 1 from the tick it sees it, `reactionTicks` late at every step                      | what a simple reflex reaches             |
| `naive`    | heads for the prey and holds a constant effort `naiveEffort` from tick 0, prey in view or not (the brain a random population starts near) | whether a beginner ever touches the prey |

`tierMargins(hunter, prey)` returns the catch margin of each tier on both bounds, in px. A frame starts a tier at an awareness gap: the `perfect` hunter was creeping at its cruise speed and acts at tick 0; the `reactive` and `naive` hunters start at rest where they see the prey (or at the awareness gap when that is further), the prey standing still until the gap falls to it, the reactive one acting `reactionTicks` late. `learnSlope` is `ok` when the margins rise from `naive` to `reactive` to `perfect` and the `naive` tier reaches contact on the `straight` bound at the start gap of the `prey-ahead` drill (`preyDrill` in `js/evo/drill-staging.js`); else it names the tier that fails (`naive` first, then `reactive`, then `perfect`). `perfect` is an upper bound to a stated resolution: the best over a sweep of the sprint effort, in two passes. The first is 0 to 1 in steps of `EFFORT_STEP` (0.05; `EFFORTS` in `js/evo/role-budget-frame.js`); the second is steps of `FINE_EFFORT` (0.01) within 0.04 of the best of the first. A tie goes to the higher effort, so a row the oracle's effort 1 already wins reads as before. Effort 1 is not the best on `broken`: sprinting flat out overshoots the oracle evader's break, and the `naive` tier at effort 0.5 closed rows of the courser arm that effort 1 did not (E257, the finding that made the sweep). The effort is the one choice the performer has: its heading is the oracle's lead aim, its sprint starts when the prey is aware, and it bites every tick. The step is chosen from the model's resolution, which is the break: the reach of a kill is not smooth in the effort, because the break and the burst running out flip on a tick, so it steps by up to 60 px between neighbouring efforts (E-RB-1: `long` forager 231 px at 0.70, 289 at 0.74) and a pass of 0.05 alone under-reads it. Against a pass of 0.0025 over the `long` and `even` broken rows the two passes read within 7 px, which is the resolution the bound is claimed to; the grid of the first pass holds `naiveEffort` (0.5) and the oracle's 1. The cost is bounded by dropping an effort that cannot beat the best so far after two chases (it must kill at the awareness gap and at the best's reach), which changes the cost and never the answer. `maxCatchableGap` of a `perfect` frame reads the best, and `catchable` and the step cells read the same effort (`bestEffort`); a frame with an `effort` plays that one. The eight audit bearings read the first pass only (straight ahead reads both, and a bearing reads as its mirror), so the share of bearings that close is a little conservative. The cross-check (`--check`) plays the oracle at effort 1, so it validates the formula there and not at the efforts under 1. An arm is fit for the rung screen only when its `predator:forager` row is `catchable` for `reactive` on `broken` and `learnSlope` is `ok`. `--search` looks for the smallest one-setting change that gives both.

### The search

`searchSettings(config, pair, options)` in `js/evo/role-budget-search.js` tries each candidate setting alone at the factors 0.5, 0.75, 1.25, 1.5 and 2 and reports, for a pair that is not `catchable`, the changes that make it so (and for a pair that is catchable but not fit for the rung screen, the changes that make it fit), smallest relative change first, with the resulting margin. The default candidates are the hunter's `sprintBoost`, `sprintDrainMultiplier`, `visionRange`, `rearVisionShare`, `turnMultiplier`, `maxEnergyMultiplier`, the prey's `sprintBoost`, `visionRange`, `turnMultiplier`, and the global `maxVelocity`, `maxThrust`, `maxTurn`, `damping`, `sprintDrain`, `sprintRefill`, `sprintFloor`, `killShare`. Each result is written as an overrides file in the format `audit:chase --config` reads (`evals/role-budget/<arm>-<hunter>-<prey>-<k>.json`, the five smallest of a pair; a value the config refuses is skipped), so a finding feeds the scripted chase and the ladder screen directly.

As built, where the steps above left a choice (the numbers in Anchors were computed by the same loops):

- A step's delay is applied to the perfect hunter at the awareness gap: `see` is `d` ticks of cruising straight and blind while the prey flees, `turn` is `d` ticks with its speed held at the held-turn speed (heading still on the prey), `run` is a sprint that starts `d` ticks late. The slack is the largest `d` up to `SLACK_CAP` that still ends in a kill inside `MAX_TICKS`. The `bite` slack is `min(SLACK_CAP, MAX_TICKS - killTick, floor(hunterEnergy / strikeMissCost))`.
- A chase that does not end in a kill reads no step: `run` is `impossible` (and `bite` too when the bodies touched but the prey was not held), `see` and `turn` are `n/a`; a refused size gate makes `bite` the one `impossible` cell. `binding` is a boolean on the cell with the least slack; the row's `binding` names it.
- Play names: `always` holds `SPRINT` from tick 0 to an empty store (the play of the straight-chase anchors); `view` is the oracle's, sprinting only while the store is above `sprintFloor` and, with the store on, the prey within the hunter's `visionRange` (and the prey's rationed play, within 360 px of the hunter). With the store off both always sprint.
- `--check` reads the kinematics against `bodyTable`, and plays the scripted chase of `audit:chase` without start jitter beside the formula, at five start distances and the eight audit bearings on both bounds; a start at exactly the hunter's view is flagged as the edge, since the sim's sprint gate is decided by float32 rounding there.

## Phase 2 steps - the defender (`js/evo/role-budget-defend.js`)

The job: when a hunter closes on a caller (a forager, reproducer or scout), the defender hears the shout, reaches the hunter before the kill completes, and can buy time with smoke.

| Step      | Formula                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| hear      | the defender is inside `hearWindow` of the caller: distance under `callRadius` (the call spreads over that radius around the caller) and loudness above `hearThreshold`; the alarm fires only when the caller's alarm output is above `alarmThreshold` and the caller is not a hunter; delay `reactionTicks` for the caller, 1 tick for `heard` to be rebuilt, `reactionTicks` for the defender |
| turn      | `swingTicks(angle to the hunter, w)` of the defender                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| run       | the defender's `speedStep` profile over the distance to the hunter, sprinting while its store lasts                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| intercept | the defender is within `interceptRange` (`size(d) + size(h)`) of the hunter before the kill completes (`bitesToKill` scaled by `alarmBiteShare` when the caller's alarm is seen) at tick `contactTick + bitesToKill - 1`, where `contactTick` is `chaseTimeline` of the hunter on the caller; the slack is the ticks to spare                                                                   |
| smoke     | see below                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

A defender that arrives shoves the hunter off, if the shove is on. `defenderRepelEnergy` and `defenderRepelImpulse` are both 0 today (`js/sim/defender-repel.js`, built and switched off), so at the defaults the defender has no power on arrival, the intercept cell is `impossible` and the row's binding is `repel: off`. The budget reads whatever the config says: `--set defenderRepelEnergy=40 --set defenderRepelImpulse=4` is the first arm of [prey-guilds.md](prey-guilds.md) Phase 2, with `defenderRepelCost` read at 0, 2 and 4 by T12 (`evals/decisions/E-RB-2/arms/`). With `defenderRepelEnergy` or `defenderRepelImpulse` above 0 the defender repels a hunter in its jaws: `tryDefenderRepel` in `js/sim/defender-repel.js` fires when the defender holds `BITE` above 0 with the hunter in contact and inside `biteArc` (`inJaws`), drains `defenderRepelEnergy` from the hunter, adds `defenderRepelImpulse` along the defender-to-hunter vector and costs the defender `defenderRepelCost`. The intercept cell then needs a shove to land (the hunter in the defender's jaws at some touch tick) before the kill, and the report prints whether it delays the kill (`breaksBite`) and the bite energy the delay prevents. A hunter biting the defender pays `kickMultiplier` (2) of a bite's kick and takes `biteTakenMultiplier` (0.5) of a bite, printed beside the cell.

As built, where the steps above left a choice (the numbers of `evals/decisions/E-RB-2/` come from these loops):

- The scene is `defenceTimeline` (`js/evo/role-budget-defence.js`): a perfect hunter creeps at cruise speed from the awareness gap (345 px for the forager) at a standing forager facing away, the caller of the matrix row (a reproducer or scout is the same call with its numbers). The caller sees the hunter inside that gap and its alarm fires `reactionTicks` later; the defender stands `escortGap` (the model, derived: 87 px at the defaults) beside the caller facing it, listens from the tick after the alarm, acts `reactionTicks` after it hears, and runs at the hunter by the `view` play of `hunterOut`. A defender that holds `BITE` is a non-hunter at the grazing gait (`grazeThrust`, 0.6 of thrust), and the oracle holds it (the shove needs it), so the run is at that gait; a defender and a caller that overlap push each other apart. `bite` takes whether the victim's alarm is running, so `alarmBiteShare` cuts only the bites inside the call's `callTicks`.
- A step's delay: `hear` shifts the tick the defender tests the call (the call must still be above `hearThreshold` at its distance then, so the slack stops at the audible ticks less one and a defender beyond `callRadius` reads `hear` impossible and no step after it), `turn` holds its speed at the turn circle's for that many ticks from its start, `run` starts its sprint late. The slack is the largest delay for which the defender's power (the first touch with the shove off, the first shove with it on) still lands before the kill of the same scene with no defender. A defender that cannot arrive has `run` impossible and `turn` not read; `turn` caps at 200 when the defender starts under the turn circle's speed.
- `intercept` is the kill tick less the tick of the first shove, in ticks to spare, impossible with the shove off or when no shove lands before the kill.
- The prey's smoke, `smokeCell(hunter, prey, model)`, takes the model with an optional `sinceRelease` (the ticks since the prey last released: `n/a` inside `smokeCooldown`, impossible when its energy is not above `smokeCost`). The prey releases at `smokeGap` (240 px) from a hunter at its sprint cap, facing away, then runs sideways at full sprint on the body law of the sim (it turns and gathers speed). `hiddenTicks` counts the ticks from the release for which the segment from the hunter to the body meets the disc of the cloud (`segmentHitsCloud`), at most `smokeTicks - 1`, the ticks the cloud is present when a brain senses; a hunter inside the cloud is blind, so the plan's far-side exit is the segment test's own result and the defender's `hiddenTicks` is not given the caller's lateral run as a further term. `escapeNeed` is one more than the last tick the body is inside the hunter's view (`viewReachCos` at the bearing from its heading, plus the body's size), `Infinity` when it never leaves; a hunter keeps its heading and speed along `hunterXs`. A negative smoke slack reads impossible.
- The defender's smoke, `defenderSmokeGeometry([hunter, caller, defender], model)`, aims the defender at the spot one body width short of the caller and models the stop (`walkAndStop` in `js/evo/role-budget-walk.js`): it turns to the spot, thrusts once aimed, cuts its thrust when one more tick would carry it past the spot, coasts down on the sim's damping (a body at its sprint cap coasts about 228 px, `coast`) while it turns to face the caller, and releases once it faces the caller and is below `STILL` (1 px a tick). It stops short of or beside the spot (at the defaults 47 px from the caller and 28 px off the line, the release at tick 53 against the 41 that the walk and turn alone took), so the cloud lands behind where it stopped (`releasePoint`, `offLine`), not behind the spot. `releaseTick` is `2 x reactionTicks + 1 + runTicks` (walk, turn and stop), and the caller starts to run a tick after the release (it senses the cloud then). `facing: 'hunter'` is refused. `standing` plays a defender already on the spot at rest, for the older cross-check scene.
- `--check` (`js/evo/role-budget-defence-check.js`) plays the scenes beside the formulas with the model's `reactionTicks` as the brains' reaction, and reads the smoke slack without it (the oracle prey reacts at once): the call, the hearing and the first move exactly, the kill with and without a defender within 2 ticks, the touch within 10% or 2 ticks, the cloud within 1 px, the cloud life, the release tick, and the signs. With the shove on it also reads the first shove tick (2 ticks), whether the shove breaks the bite (`breaksBite`, a sign) and the bite energy it prevents (`prevented`, exact, from the scene's bite count). The defender's cloud, blind ticks and the caller's escape are read only for a release before the hunter reaches the cloud (`slackDefender` 0 or more): after that the hunter bumps the defender, which the formula does not model. The walk is its own scene (`playDefenderSmoke` with `walk`, `walkerBrain` in `js/evo/role-scenes-walk.js`, the hunter standing still): the defender starts `escortGap` to the side facing along it, and the release tick (2 ticks) and the cloud's x and y (3 px, a stop is a hundred ticks of damped motion) are read; it is not read for a defender never heard or whose path runs through the caller (the formula has no bumps). **Known gap:** with the shove on and the defender arriving (the slow world of the slow test), the kill tick reads 116 in the formula and 201 in the sim, because the sim's hunter bites the defender dead and rests after that kill, and the formula plays neither. The first shove tick, `breaksBite` and `prevented` agree there.
- `--search` on the defender row (`js/evo/role-budget-search-defend.js`) has two goals, the defence (`hear`, `turn`, `run` and `intercept` all possible) and the smoke, and writes the single settings that reach them as override files under `--out-dir` (`<arm>-defender-predator-<goal>-<k>.json`); a setting that is 0 in the config is skipped, so the shove is turned on with `--set` first.

**Smoke.** `smokeCell(hunter, prey, model)` reads how smoke works in the sim: a releaser needs its `SMOKE` output above `smokeThreshold` (0.8), energy above `smokeCost`, a cooldown of 0 and a free cloud (`maxClouds`; a full pool pushes `SmokeRefused` and pays nothing), then pays `smokeCost` and and drops one cloud of radius `smokeRadius` centred `size + smokeRadius` behind it along its heading (`releaseSmoke`), for `smokeTicks` ticks, with `smokeCooldown` ticks before the same body releases again (`smoke` in `js/sim/calls.js`). A cloud is a ray fixture (`SMOKE` in `js/sim/ray-gather.js`): a ray that meets it returns a smoke hit, so a body behind it on that ray is not seen; the hunter's brain loses the prey for as long as the cloud lies on the rays towards it. Its recurrent state may keep its heading; the budget assumes the worst case for the prey, a hunter that keeps its speed and heading while blind.

| Quantity           | Formula                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `shadow`           | the prey is hidden while its bearing from the hunter lies within `asin(smokeRadius / distanceToCloud)` of the cloud's, and the cloud has ticks left                                                   |
| `hiddenTicks`      | the ticks the segment from the hunter to the body meets the cloud, at most `smokeTicks - 1` (the body runs sideways, so it leaves the shadow after about the sideways run needed / its lateral speed) |
| `escapeNeed`       | the ticks the prey needs to open the gap past the hunter's `viewReach` while the hunter keeps its speed (`chaseTimeline` with the hunter blind)                                                       |
| smoke slack (prey) | `hiddenTicks - escapeNeed - reactionTicks`; `n/a` when `smokeCooldown` has not elapsed at the scene's start; the energy cost (`smokeCost`) is printed against the energy the prey holds               |

**Defender smoke.** `defenderSmokeGeometry(hunter, caller, defender)` answers where and when the defender must release so that the cloud lies on the hunter-to-caller line. `releaseSmoke` drops the cloud `size(d) + smokeRadius` behind the releaser along its heading, so a defender that stands on the hunter-to-caller segment must face the caller (its back to the hunter) for the cloud to land between the two; facing the hunter drops the cloud behind it, on the caller's side, and the hunter's rays meet the defender first.

| Quantity             | Formula                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `releasePoint`       | the defender's position `x` along the segment (hunter at 0, caller at `L`) with `x - (size(d) + smokeRadius) - smokeRadius` at least the hunter's travel in `reactionTicks`, so the cloud's near edge is still ahead of the hunter at the release, and the cloud's centre within `smokeRadius` of the line |
| `facingTicks`        | `swingTicks(angle to face the caller, w)` of the defender; the release waits for it                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `latestRelease`      | the last tick at which the cloud's near edge is still ahead of the hunter: `(x_cloud - smokeRadius - x_hunter) / hunter speed - reactionTicks`                                                                                                                                                             |
| `hiddenTicks`        | the ticks the hunter's rays to the caller meet the cloud: from the release until the hunter exits the cloud's far side, `(x_cloud + smokeRadius - x_hunter) / hunter speed`, or the caller leaves the shadow sideways, at most `smokeTicks - 1`                                                            |
| defender smoke slack | `latestRelease - (hear + facingTicks + run to the releasePoint)`, and `hiddenTicks - escapeNeed - reactionTicks` for the caller; the smaller is the cell's margin; `n/a` with `smokeCooldown` not elapsed                                                                                                  |

The job: see a hunter before the hunter is within reach of the herd, and warn in time for a forager to turn and flee.

### The first meal (`<spec>:first-meal`)

A body is born somewhere with some energy. The row asks whether that energy carries it to a food source that suits it, by the distance food really is from where bodies are born. A good source for a spec is one it can live on: a food patch for a spec with `plantFoodValue` above 0, prey or a carcass for a hunter.

The row is computed for each way a body starts, since each has its own place and energy (`spawnAgent` in `js/sim/spawn.js`): a founder (`initialEnergy`, a random clear point), a floor entrant (`entrantEnergy`, a random clear point), and a child (its mother's gift, beside its mother).

| Step    | Formula                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `reach` | the range a body covers before its energy is gone, `energy / cost per tick x speed` at the gait that goes furthest per unit of energy (`bestRangeSpeed`, found on `tickCost`, since movement costs `speed^2`), against the distance to the nearest good source: for a random point and `n` sources in a world of area `A`, the nearest centre is beyond `r` with probability `exp(-n pi r^2 / A)`, and the source's own radius is subtracted. The cell reports the mean distance, the 90th percentile, and `reachShare`, the share of birth points from which the source is inside the range; the slack is the range less the mean distance, in ticks at that speed. A child's distance is its mother's distance to food, taken as inside a patch |
| `find`  | the same body with no knowledge of where the source is: walking straight, it sees a strip `2 x viewReach` wide (the sideways reach, `viewReach` at PI / 2), so the expected path before a source of radius `rho` enters view is `A / (n x 2 (viewReach + rho))`. The cell compares that path with the range; `findShare` is the share of straight walks that meet a source inside the range, `1 - exp(-range x n x 2 (viewReach + rho) / A)`. Food scent, where the body senses it, widens the strip to the scent's reach and is printed as a second line                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| `eat`   | on arrival, the meals needed to climb back to the energy it was born with, at the net rate of the `eat` step of this phase, against what the patch holds                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |

A hunter's source is prey: `n` is `maxAgents x (1 - hunterShare)` (the density of the prey the hunter's living reads), `rho` the mean size of the prey at the mix of `--mix`, the `eat` step reads the net of a chase from `predator:living`, and the row uses the hunter's juvenile burn. Every first-meal row is read at the juvenile burn. The row's cells are the worst of the three birth rules (an impossible one first, else the least slack); the report prints each rule's line. The carcass line is not built: it needs the kills and herd deaths per tick of a shared baseline, which no config gives.

At the defaults the world is 14400 by 8100 with 16 patches of radius 400. By the formulas, a random point is about 1350 px from the nearest patch centre and 959 px from its edge (90th percentile 1912), an entrant with 200 energy walks 3172 px at the gait that goes furthest per energy (2.10 px a tick; a founder with 800 walks 11527), and a straight walk with a 300 px view meets a patch after about 5200 px on average, so the share of entrants that find one is 0.46 (a founder's 0.89). `reach` is green and `find` is red for the four that eat plants; the hunter finds prey after 419 px and its first meal is red on `eat` (its chase nets a loss at the catch share of 0.3: the living row, which a kill pays for only above that share). These are the numbers `T17b` computed from the code; the scene reads the shares ("Cross-check against the sim").

### The sitter (`<spec>:idle`)

A body that stands still and does nothing is common in the first generations. Selection removes it only if sitting kills sooner than searching does; a change that gives a newborn more energy lengthens the sitter's life by the same amount, so the two are read together.

| Step  | Formula                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ----- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `sit` | `idleLife`: the ticks a body with `THRUST`, `TURN` and `SPRINT` at 0 survives from its birth energy. A scalar recurrence, since the cost moves with the body's temperature: each tick the temperature falls by `temperatureLossPassive` (and by the season's slope), the passive loss is multiplied by `passiveMultiplier(temperature)` (at most `temperaturePassiveLossFactor`, 2, at temperature 0), and the cold stack below is applied when it is on. `activeLife`: the ticks the same body survives searching at `bestRangeSpeed` (the `find` step of the first meal). `idleRatio = idleLife / activeLife`. The cell is `ok` at or under 1, `tight` up to 2, and `impossible` above 2: a sitter that outlives a searcher twice over is what the world selects for |

At the defaults the first season cools a still body by 0.0075 x 1.15 = 0.0086 a tick (the season's `temperatureModifier` x `temperatureSeasonSlope`), so it leaves the optimal band (40) after about 1160 ticks, and the cold at most doubles its passive loss; a body walking at the gait that goes furthest per energy spends about 0.13 a tick, more than twice what it spends sitting. The sit is `tight` for every spec: a sitter lives 1.4 to 1.9 times as long as a searcher, the entrant (the poorest) the longest, and the sim reproduces both lives to 0.2% (the cross-check). The cold stack of `chilled` is read from `coldStackRate`, `coldStackMax`, `coldStackTicks` and `coldRecoverTicks` where the config has them (status-effects.md T9), as `1 + coldStackRate x stacks` on the standing terms, and is off otherwise.

The row gates any rise in birth energy (`entrantEnergy`, `initialEnergy`, `childEnergyMin`): `--search` on a first-meal row that is red tries the birth energy together with the cold settings, and accepts a change only when the first-meal row turns `ok` and `idleRatio` does not rise. The cold settings it may move are `temperatureStart`, `temperatureLossPassive`, `temperaturePassiveLossFactor` and the `chilled` effect of [status-effects.md](status-effects.md) (`coldStackRate`, `coldStackMax`).

### A hunter's living (`predator:living`)

The row asks whether a hunter's kills pay for its chases and reach the split gate inside its life (selection rule 6: a kill that costs more than it returns is a failed gene).

| Step    | Formula                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `eat`   | net energy per chase: `catchShare x killIncome - (catchShare x killCost + (1 - catchShare) x failCost)`, with `killIncome` what a kill returns, the bites to the kill line and then the gorge `min(killGorgeShare x the victim's energy above its kill line, killGorgeCap, the killer's store room)` (`js/sim/kill-body.js`; 700 on a forager at 1000 energy, the anchors), and the two costs the spent energy and the kicks of the `reactive` tier's chase of Phase 1, averaged over the prey at the `--mix`: a chase that kills (`killCost`) is never charged the ticks of one that does not (`failCost`). The cost model is the mix of the two at the catch share, so a kill's income is paired with the cost of the chase that made it. `killCost` is the broken bound's chase when it kills, else the straight bound's (the prey that does not break), else no chase kills and it is `failCost`; `failCost` is the broken bound's chase cut where the sim gives up (the give-up rule below). The carcass eaten later at `meatFoodValue` is not read, and neither is `temperatureGainEat` (the heat a meal adds, which the formulas leave out; set to 0 the sim's settled net rate of a forager moves from 5.67 to 5.78, 2%, and no row or verdict moves); `impossible` when the net is 0 or less at `catchShare` 1, which is `killIncome - killCost` |
| `breed` | chases to `splitGate(params)` of the `breed` step from the energy at birth, at the net of `eat`, against the chases a life holds: `encountersPerLife` of this section with the prey's density; the slack is the spare chases, printed in ticks at the mean ticks per chase                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |

The give-up rule is the sim's, read from the code and not a chosen number (`js/evo/role-budget-lapse.js`, constants of `js/sim/merit-state.js`, the lapse of `scene-approach.js` that the observer counts as a give-up, split into lost, outrun and quit by `scene-chase-lapse.js`). The brain decides when to quit, and the `reactive` tier has no quit: it sprints at effort 1 and steers at the prey until the kill or the end of the run, as the scripted pursuer of `audit:chase` does. So the end of a failed chase is the observer's lapse: a chase is open on a tick when the prey was on the rays within `INTENT_WINDOW` (30) ticks and the hunter closed on it (a speed towards it above `CLOSING_SPEED` 0.25 of `maxVelocity`) on `CLOSING_MIN` (6) of its last `CLOSING_BITS` (10) ticks, and it lapses on the first tick more than `INTENT_WINDOW` after the last open one (the prey left the rays, or the hunter stopped closing). The chase is cut there, or at `MAX_TICKS` (250) when it does not lapse. At the defaults and on every arm of `evals/fair-chase/` the broken prey does not leave a sprinting hunter's rays, which keeps closing, so a failed chase of the reactive tier runs the 250 ticks: the median 30-tick chase of `docs/notes/2026-10.md` is an evolved hunter's, which gives up by its own brain, not a scripted one's. A slow cross-check (`test/evo/role-budget-lapse-check.slow.test.js`) plays the scripted chase in the sim, applies the same rule to its bodies each tick, and holds the formula's give-up tick within `TOLERANCE.contactTicks` (2) of the sim's and the energy spent by then within `TOLERANCE.killNet` (5%).

The row also prints the resting cost between chases (`passiveLoss x passiveLossMultiplier`, the gorge-and-fast body) and the ticks a full store lasts with no kill.

As built, where the steps above left a choice (the numbers of `evals/decisions/E-RB-4/` come from these loops):

- The rows read the default world, not the empty arena of Phases 1 to 3 (an arena has 8 agents and no food): `matrix` takes a `worldConfig` (the script builds it with `createConfig(overrides)`) and the Phase 4 rows read the world's numbers (`contextOf`: area, patches, `maxFood`, `maxAgents`) and their own body from it, the hunter's budgets of Phase 1 from the arena config. The modules are `role-budget-forage.js` (the world, `gaitCost`, `bestRangeSpeed`, `lifeOf`, `forageCycle`, `splitGate`), `role-budget-thrive.js`, `role-budget-living.js`, `role-budget-first-meal.js`, `role-budget-idle.js`, `role-budget-search-birth.js` and `role-budget-matrix-thrive.js` (the rows for the matrix).
- `lifeOf` is the one scalar recurrence under `idleLife`, `activeLife` and the first-meal range: each tick the temperature gains `min(speed / maxVelocity, heatSpeedCap) x temperatureGainMove`, loses `temperatureLossPassive` x the first season's `1 - temperatureModifier x temperatureSeasonSlope` (1.15 at the defaults), is held to 0 to `temperatureMax`, and the tick's cost is taken at the new temperature's `passiveMultiplier` (the sim's own function, through the one-slot lane view of `startWorld`), as `applyCosts` orders it. A body at the cold margin of the world (a `coldMarginWidth` of 600 px at each edge doubles the cooling) is not modelled. `bestRangeSpeed` is `sqrt(A / B)` of the cost `A + B speed^2` at the heat factor 1.
- `breed` takes the energy at birth as the poorer of a founder's and an entrant's (`poorestBirth`), so the slack is capped at 200 ticks in the matrix and the true slack is in the detail (71256 at the defaults). The `eat` cell's margin is the net energy per 100 ticks (unit `energy per 100 ticks`), capped at 200 for printing as `>487`.
- `predator:first-meal` reads a hunter against `maxAgents x (1 - hunterShare)` prey at the `--mix`, and its `eat` step is the net of a chase from `predator:living`: a hunter with a living of 0 or less never climbs back, so the row is red on `eat` wherever the net of `predator:living` at the catch share is. The `eat` colour is inherited from the living row, not a reading of its own: the first-meal row adds no number of its own to it, so a change to the living row's cost model moves it.
- The first-meal and idle searches (`searchBirth`) try `entrantEnergy`, `initialEnergy` and `childEnergyMin` at the factors 0.5 to 4, alone and with each of `temperatureStart`, `temperatureLossPassive`, `temperaturePassiveLossFactor`, `coldStackRate` and `coldStackMax` at the same factors (a key the config lacks or holds at 0 is skipped), and accept a change when the first-meal row turns `ok` and the sitter's largest ratio is not above the baseline's; `--search` writes the three smallest per spec as `<arm>-<spec>-birth-<k>.json`.
- `--check` (`js/evo/role-budget-thrive-check.js`) plays the scenes of "Cross-check against the sim" beside the formulas: the sitter's and the walker's lives from each birth energy (0.5% and 2%; the sit matches to the tick), the settled net rate of a forager in a grid of food (30%), the Phase 1 chase read as a living (kill tick within 2 ticks, net within 5%), and the reach and find shares of the first meal (reach within 0.2, find as an upper bound within 0.15). The oracle brains are in `js/evo/forage-oracles.js` (not `chase-oracles.js`, which is at its line limit), the scenes in `role-scenes-thrive.js` and `role-scenes-first-meal.js`. The first-meal scene makes every plant the same grass (energy 1, radius 1, always in fruit): the sim's flora is a mixture (items of 60 to 180 energy and radii of 14 to 26 px, and a patch bears fruit only in its species' season, so 9 of 16 patches carry food at tick 0), which the formulas do not model; the plan's meal of `foodEnergy x plantFoodValue` and its patch density are the grass-only world, an upper bound on the real one.
- Every changed file of Phase 4 is held at 100% by the fast tests: the scenes run on a small world and a short life (`test/evo/role-budget-thrive-check.test.js`), and the slow test (`role-budget-thrive.slow.test.js`, about 2.5 minutes) reads the default world.
- A straight walk's find share is an upper bound: the formula is a walk on a free plane and the world's rocks and walls only lower it (a founder's 11527 px read 0.52 in the scene against 0.89 by the formula; an entrant's 3172 px read 0.43 against 0.46). This gap is why the find check is one-sided: the sim is allowed to read below the formula by any amount (a founder reads 0.37 under it) and is drift only when it reads more than 0.15 above it.

## Phase 5 steps - the herd (`js/evo/role-budget-herd.js`)

A herd animal that closes on a body tramples it, walking or stampeding (`resolveContact` in `js/sim/trample.js`): the body loses `trampleDamage x (closing speed / maxVelocity)^2 x size / baseSize` and its velocity is reflected. The job, for each of the five specs: see the herd coming, and get out of its path in time if it chooses to.

How a herd moves is a recurrence, not a constant speed (`js/sim/herd-startle.js`, `js/sim/herd-motion.js`, `js/sim/herds.js`):

- Only an agent that can hunt startles a herd, within `herdStartleRadius` (200) of any animal, polled every 8 ticks; the alarm lasts `herdStampedeTicks` (360).
- An animal's velocity relaxes toward its desired velocity by 0.05 a tick, so it takes about 65 ticks to reach `herdRunSpeed` (6.5).
- The herd spawns on a ring of radius `herdSize x 2.2 x herdRadius / (2 pi)` (224 at the defaults); its half-width is that plus `herdRadius` and the body's size (333 for a forager).
- While the alarm is up `summarizeGroup` held the herd's centroid where it was, and an animal more than `4 x herdRadius` from it was pulled back at full speed. Measured before the T21a fix: the mean speed peaks at 5.1, the centroid travels 329 px by tick 80 and 516 px by tick 360 (a free run at 6.5 would be 2340), the half-width shrinks from 224 to 66, and a standing forager in the path loses 43 energy, not the 306 of one trample at run speed. T21a found the hold was a side effect of holding the flee vector (`2c09ec7d`, which says nothing of the centroid) and fixed it: the centroid follows the herd and only the flee vector is held (a 6-animal herd in an open world: 2227 px by tick 360); see the note of 2026-11-21 in `docs/notes/2026-11.md`. T22 re-measured the herd alone (`measureStampede`): the centroid travels 407 px by tick 80 and 2227 px by tick 360, the mean speed peaks at 6.50 and the half-width goes from 224 to 234; the pre-fix numbers above are gone from the rows, which read the recurrence below.

`herdTimeline(config, options)` steps one animal's 1D position with that recurrence from its ring offset and returns its position and speed per tick. The lateral pull is not modelled: the half-width over time is read from the scene.

| Step      | Formula                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `see`     | the herd is on a ray (`CH_MEGAFAUNA`) inside `viewReach(spec, bearing)` plus `herdRadius`; the warning is the ticks `herdTimeline` takes to cover that distance (41.7 for a forager facing the herd at a constant 6.5, the upper bound printed beside it)                                                                                |
| `turn`    | `swingTicks(angle to the nearer flank, w)` of the body                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| `run`     | ticks to clear the half-width sideways with the chase recurrence (53 at cruise and 39 sprinting for a forager from the centre line at the defaults), against the ticks until the front arrives; the slack is the difference. A second line reads the straight run ahead of the herd: the body's cruise cap against the herd's peak speed |
| `survive` | the loss of one trample at the herd's speed on arrival and at its walk speed (306 and 16 for a forager-sized body at 6.5 and 1.5), against the body's energy at `storeShare`, and the tramples it survives                                                                                                                               |

Rows: `forager:herd`, `predator:herd`, `reproducer:herd`, `scout:herd`, `defender:herd`. Each prints the typical case (the body beside the path) and the worst (in the middle of the path, facing away, on the rear view of `rearVisionShare`).

As built, where the steps above left a choice (the numbers of `evals/decisions/E-RB-5/` come from these loops):

- `herdTimeline(herd, {ticks, start})` takes `herdNumbers(config)` (the ring, `herdRadius`, the two speeds, `trampleDamage`, `maxVelocity`, `herdStampedeTicks`) and one animal's run from a standing start: each tick its velocity relaxes toward the desired velocity, `herdRunSpeed` plus the flee bias of 0.25 (6.75), by 0.05, is capped at `herdRunSpeed`, and it moves by it (65 ticks to the run speed). `ticksToCover` is the first tick it has gone far enough, extended at its last speed past the timeline.
- `see`: the front animal is first on a ray when its gap is `viewReach + herdRadius`, and arrives when the gap is `size + herdRadius`, so it covers `viewReach - size` (271 px for a forager): the `see` cell is the ticks `herdTimeline` takes (59, from a standing start; 41.7 at a constant 6.5 is printed beside it as the herd's worst case). `turn` is the ticks left once the model's `reactionTicks` and `swingTicks(PI / 2)` are paid, `run` the ticks left once the better of the two gaits (`clearTicks`: the speed law from rest, 53 at cruise and 39 sprinting, 55 and 36 for a predator) has cleared the half-width `ring + herdRadius + size`, and `survive` the tramples `floor(energy / loss)` at the herd's speed on arrival (the speed of the front at the arrival tick, 6.42 px a tick for a forager, 299 energy, not the constant 6.5's 306); 0 is `impossible`. The cell is the worst case, a body on the centre line facing away on its rear view; the typical case (facing the herd, on its forward view) is printed beside it. The plan's "body beside the path" is not read: a body beside the path has nothing to clear.
- A body that can hunt within `herdStartleRadius` of an animal starts its own stampede away from it (`senseHunters`), so the herd turns before it arrives: the `predator:herd` row reads `n/a` ("the herd turns from a hunter"; the typical and worst cases and the trample count are printed beside it), and the scene reads only the four that do not hunt.
- A ring wider than `4 x herdRadius` (a herd of 12 at the defaults) pulls the front animal back toward the centroid at full speed, so the formula's front is too fast; `HerdNumbers.pulled` flags it and the report prints a warning. The lateral pull, the separation between animals, rocks, the wall and the route to a patch are not modelled.
- `--check` (`js/evo/role-budget-herd-check.js`) plays `playStampede` (`role-scenes-herd.js`, the oracle in `herd-oracles.js`): a herd startled from a standstill toward a body `viewReach + herdRadius` ahead of its front animal. A body that stands reads the front's arrival (exact: 59, 51, 74 and 49 ticks) and the first trample (within 2%); a body that runs for the nearer flank reads the ticks to clear (the formula is an upper bound on it, 51.5 against 45, since the sim's body runs while it turns) and that a body the formula calls clear is not trampled. The tolerance is the 10% of "Tolerances".

## Tolerances

`--check` and the slow cross-check tests fail a cell outside these, with `RoleBudgetDrift`. The scene of the cross-check stages a newborn hunter, which pays the juvenile burn, so the formula plays a juvenile hunter there (`formulaChase`); the budget's own rows play an adult. Both charge `strikeMissCost` on every tick the hunter holds BITE with nothing in its jaws, as the feed phase does.

| Reading                                                                                          | Tolerance                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| speeds, caps, ticks to the cap, store ticks, size, bites at a fixed energy, kill income, hearing | exact (1e-9, or 0 ticks)                                                                                                                                                        |
| turn radius against `bodyTable`                                                                  | 0.5%                                                                                                                                                                            |
| energy per 100 ticks against `bodyTable`                                                         | 0.1%                                                                                                                                                                            |
| a straight chase's contact tick against the sim                                                  | 2 ticks                                                                                                                                                                         |
| the largest gap closed in 250 ticks against the anchor (a size sum held at 74)                   | 7 px, the timeline under the anchor: the bodies shrink as they burn and pay the strike misses                                                                                   |
| the kill tick                                                                                    | the tick the last bite lands (no sooner than `contact + bites - 1`), within 2 ticks of the sim                                                                                  |
| the hunter's net energy per kill                                                                 | 5% of the larger of the net and the kill's income (a break-even chase has a net near 0)                                                                                         |
| a catch share against the audit                                                                  | sign and rank per bearing only, read against the scripted scene of the sim (the audit's own shares are 2 trials a cell and noisy: the slow test reads them against the margins) |
| the herd rows                                                                                    | the scene's reading until T21a settles the stampede; then 10% on the warning and the clear ticks                                                                                |
