# Role budget

**Status:** Proposed - a closed-form budget of each specialisation's job (hunt, defend, warn, eat and breed, clear a herd) with a margin per step, three performer tiers for the hunter and a cross-check of every cell against a scripted scene; Phase 1 is first, and all five phases are read before any training starts (fair-chase.md, Before any training).
**Validated:** f77acde1 (2026-11-21)
**Scheduled by:** [fair-chase.md](fair-chase.md) step 1 - gate 1 of fair-chase.md "Before any training": Phases 1 to 5 are built and read before T7b and T8 there
**Depends on:** none

> Companion to [senses-and-motor.md](senses-and-motor.md), whose `npm run audit:chase` oracles are the first cross-check, and to [body-genes.md](body-genes.md), which lets the budget read a spec's gene ranges.

The sim has five specialisations (`SPECIALISATION_KEYS` in `js/sim/config-spec-rules.js`). Each has a job: the predator catches, the defender protects the herd, the scout warns, the forager and the reproducer eat and breed. Whether a job is possible with the numbers in the config is arithmetic first and evolution second. A hunter whose sprint empties before it reaches the prey cannot be trained to catch it; a defender that cannot reach a caller before the hunter's last bite cannot protect it.

`npm run role:budget` computes, for each role, a closed-form budget from the config alone (no world, no RNG, no brain). The budget names every step of the job, says whether a perfect performer completes it, and gives each step a margin: how imperfect the performer may be at that step. The result is a matrix. A red cell names the number that makes a role impossible. The calculator is a detector and a tuning tool, never fitness, merit or rank.

Scenes served: **A real chase** (the hunter's budget), and every scene where a role is judged possible before it is trained for ([principles.md](../principles.md)). Selection rule 6 (hunting wins by the chain) is served by Phase 1.

## The matrix

Rows are a performer against the pressure on it; columns are the steps. A cell holds the margin of that step, or `-` when the step is not part of the job.

| Row                   | Steps used                                                                             | Phase |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----- |
| `predator:forager`    | see, turn, run, bite                                                                   | 1     |
| `predator:reproducer` | see, turn, run, bite                                                                   | 1     |
| `predator:scout`      | see, turn, run, bite                                                                   | 1     |
| `predator:defender`   | see, turn, run, bite                                                                   | 1     |
| `predator:average`    | the weighted mean of the four rows above (a hunter that cannot tell its targets apart) | 1     |
| `predator:best`       | the row with the largest slack (a hunter that picks its target)                        | 1     |
| `defender:predator`   | hear, turn, run, intercept, smoke (the defender's and the caller's)                    | 2     |
| `scout:predator`      | see, hear, warn, turn, run                                                             | 3     |
| `forager:predator`    | eat, breed, run                                                                        | 4     |
| `reproducer:predator` | eat, breed, run                                                                        | 4     |
| `predator:living`     | eat, breed (a hunter's life: do its kills pay for its chases and reach the split gate) | 4     |
| `<spec>:herd`         | see, turn, run, survive (each of the five specs against a stampeding herd)             | 5     |

A cell is `{margin, unit, distance, status, binding}`. `margin` is the slack in ticks. `status` is `ok` (margin at least `tightTicks`), `tight` (0 to under `tightTicks`), `impossible` (a perfect performer fails; margin `null`) or `n/a`. `binding` names the step whose slack is smallest in the row. A row is `impossible` when any of its cells is.

A step's slack is found by bisection on the row's timeline: the timeline takes a `delays` option (ticks added at one step, the others perfect) and the slack is the largest delay for which the job still completes, capped at `SLACK_CAP` (200, printed `>200`).

`--mix forager=0.6,reproducer=0.2,scout=0.1,defender=0.1` sets the weights of the average (default: the spec shares of a seeded population). `predator:average` counts a prey as zero slack when it is `impossible` and reports `catchableShare`, the mix share of prey the hunter can catch at all; `predator:best` reports which prey it picks. `choiceGain` is the best row's slack minus the average's, in ticks: the margin a hunter gains by telling its targets apart, which [body-genes.md](body-genes.md) Phase 4 (who is that) can buy it.

## Body numbers

`roleParams(config, specName)` in `js/evo/role-budget-params.js` resolves one specialisation's numbers from the config: the global body keys times the specialisation's fields, so it follows whatever the config says (and, after [body-genes.md](body-genes.md) Phase 1, the spec table). Every formula below reads only this record.

| Number        | Read from                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| thrust `a`    | `maxThrust` x `thrustEfficiency` (1 in the optimal band) x fat factor `1 - fatSlowShare x storeShare` (`fatFactor` in `js/sim/physics.js`) x, while sprinting, the sprint factor `1 + sprintMultiplier x sprintBoost x factor` (`thrustOf`)                                   |
| damping `d`   | `damping`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| speed caps    | `maxVelocity`; sprint cap `maxVelocity x (1 + sprintMultiplier x sprintBoost x factor)` (`move_agent` in `wasm/src/physics.rs`)                                                                                                                                               |
| turn rate `w` | `maxTurn x turnMultiplier x fat factor`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| size          | `baseSize + storeShare x maxEnergy x maxEnergyMultiplier / energyToSize` (`updateSize` in `js/sim/energy.js`)                                                                                                                                                                 |
| view          | `visionRange`; rear reach by `reachFactor(rearVisionShare, theta)` (`js/sim/ray-reach.js`); `rayCount`                                                                                                                                                                        |
| store         | `sprintDrain x sprintDrainMultiplier`, `sprintRefill`, `sprintRestThrust`, `sprintFloor` (`js/sim/sprint-store.js`); off when `sprintStoreEnabled` is false                                                                                                                   |
| bite          | `biteEnergy x biteMultiplier x biteTakenMultiplier of the victim`, `killShare x maxEnergy` of the victim (`killLine`), `biteArc`, `huntSizeRatio` (`couldBite` in `js/sim/bite-rule.js`), `biteCost` and `kickMultiplier` (`kickOf` in `js/sim/feeding.js`), `alarmBiteShare` |
| costs         | `passiveLoss`, `sizeEnergyLoss`, `movementCost`, `turnCost`, `sprintCost`, spec multipliers (`tickCost` in `js/sim/energy.js`)                                                                                                                                                |
| heat          | `temperatureStart`, `temperatureGainMove`, `temperatureLossPassive`, `temperatureOptimalMax`, `thrustEfficiency` bands                                                                                                                                                        |
| call          | `callRadius`, `callTicks`, `scoutCallRadiusMultiplier`, `alarmThreshold` (`js/sim/calls.js`)                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| smoke         | `smokeRadius`, `smokeTicks`, `smokeCooldown`, `smokeCost`, `smokeThreshold`, `maxClouds` (`releaseSmoke` in `js/sim/smoke.js`, `smoke` in `js/sim/calls.js`)                                                                                                                  |

`storeShare` is the share of its store a body holds at the start of a scene (0.5, the arena's `STORE_SHARE` in `js/evo/chase-body.js`).

The model options a budget needs beyond the config live in `js/evo/role-budget-model.js` (`MODEL_DEFAULTS`, validated, written into every report and overridable by `--model <file.json>`):

| Option          | Default  | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| --------------- | -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `reactionTicks` | 2        | Ticks from a stimulus on a ray to the brain's output changing the body                                                                                                                                                |
| `hearThreshold` | 0.1      | The `heard` loudness a brain acts on                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `breakAngle`    | PI / 2   | The turn through which a breaking prey forces the hunter                                                                                                                                                              |
| `tightTicks`    | 5        | The slack under which a cell is `tight`                                                                                                                                                                               |
| `preySprint`    | `always` | `always`: the prey sprints while its store lasts (a bound against the hunter); `oracle`: the evader of `js/evo/chase-oracles.js`                                                                                      |
| `hunterShare`   | 0.1      | The share of the population that hunts, for the pressure on a forager                                                                                                                                                 |
| `foodDensity`   | `null`   | Food items per px2 inside a patch (Phase 4); `null` derives it from the config as `patchFoodDensity(config) = maxFood x (1 - foodBackgroundShare) / (foodPatchCount x pi x foodPatchRadius^2)`, a number overrides it |
| `naiveEffort`   | 0.5      | The constant sprint effort of the `naive` performer tier                                                                                                                                                              |
| `catchShare`    | 0.3      | The share of chases a hunter wins, for `predator:living`; the middle of the nature band of 0.1 to 0.4, overridden by a measured share                                                                                 |

## Kinematics (`js/evo/role-budget-kinematics.js`)

Exact for a body moving on a line or holding a steady turn; each is tested against `bodyTable` in `js/evo/chase-body.js`. With the sprint store on the sprint is graded ([fair-chase.md](fair-chase.md), "Graded sprint"): an effort `e` from 0 to 1 scales the sprint's thrust factor, its rise of the speed cap, its drain and its energy cost, so every function below takes `e` (1 for a perfect performer) and `burstTicks` is `(1 - sprintFloor) / (drain x e)`.

| Function                     | Closed form                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `speedStep(v, a, d, cap)`    | `min(cap, (v + a) d)`: the speed law of `actAll` then `moveAgentsKernel` for a straight run, where `a` is the thrust of the gait (the sprint factor included while sprinting, so the cap and the thrust both rise); uncapped steady speed `a d / (1 - d)` |
| `ticksToSpeed(a, d, cap)`    | the first `n` with `a d (1 - d^n) / (1 - d)` at the cap, from rest                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `turnCircle(a, d, w)`        | steady speed on a held turn `a d / sqrt(1 - 2 d cos(w) + d^2)`, radius that over `w`; reproduces `bodyTable` 21.5 and 41.8 (cruise) and 32.3 and 75.3 (sprint) at the defaults                                                                            |
| `swingTicks(phi, w)`         | `phi / w`: ticks to turn the velocity through `phi` once the circle is steady                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `burstTicks(store)`          | `(1 - sprintFloor) / drain` ticks at full factor (140 for the `courser` predator), `1 / drain` ticks to empty (200); infinite with the store off                                                                                                          |
| `refillTicks(store)`         | `(1 - level) / sprintRefill` at rest                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `sprintFactor(level, floor)` | 1 at or above the floor, `level / floor` under it (`storeFactor`)                                                                                                                                                                                         |

## Senses (`js/evo/role-budget-senses.js`)

| Function                           | Closed form                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `viewReach(params, bearing)`       | `visionRange x reachFactor(rearVisionShare, bearing)`; a ray hits the body edge, so add the target's size                                                                                    |
| `reliableView(params, targetSize)` | the range at which two neighbouring rays (`2 pi / rayCount` apart) cannot both miss a body: `targetSize / sin(pi / rayCount)`                                                                |
| `seeLead(hunter, prey, bearing)`   | `viewReach(hunter, 0) - viewReach(prey, bearing of the hunter from the prey)`: the distance the hunter closes unseen, negative when the prey sees first                                      |
| `hearLoudness(d, t, caller)`       | `(1 - d / R) x (1 - t / callTicks)` with `R` the caller's radius (`hearOne` and `hearPass` in `js/sim/calls.js`); `hearWindow` is the ticks and distance with loudness above `hearThreshold` |

## Phase 1 steps - the hunter against a prey (`js/evo/role-budget-chase.js`)

A perfect hunter creeps at cruise until the prey sees it (`seeLead`), then turns to the prey's bearing (`swingTicks`), sprints while the store lasts (a burst spent after the prey is aware, never before), closes the gap, and bites for `bitesToKill = ceil((preyEnergy - killLine) / bite)` ticks while in contact. The prey flees straight at its sprint cap while its store lasts, then at its cruise cap.

`chaseTimeline(hunter, prey, options)` steps the two bodies' scalar speeds with `speedStep` (at most `MAX_TICKS` of 250 iterations, no world), advances both stores, and returns the tick of contact or `null`. Each term:

| Step | Perfect performance                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Cost of a delay `d`                                                                                       |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| see  | the hunter acts the tick the prey is inside `viewReach` (or `reliableView` with `--reliable`)                                                                                                                                                                | the prey, once aware, runs `d` ticks at its flee speed before the hunter moves                            |
| turn | the hunter's heading is on the prey after `swingTicks(angle, w)`                                                                                                                                                                                             | `d` extra ticks of the hunter at the turn circle's speed (`turnCircle`), below its straight speed         |
| run  | the sprint starts when the prey is aware and ends when the store hits the floor                                                                                                                                                                              | the sprint starts `d` ticks late, so the burst ends later in the chase                                    |
| bite | the size gate holds (`size(h) > huntSizeRatio x size(p)`, else the pair is `impossible` at this step), the hunter faces the prey inside `biteArc` at contact (`inJaws`), and contact lasts `contactTicks = reach / closing speed`, reach `size(h) + size(p)` | the hunter needs `d` more contact ticks than the closing speed allows (`bitesToKill + d <= contactTicks`) |

Two bounds are reported for every pair, because the escape depends on the prey's play:

| Bound      | Prey play                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Reads                                                                     |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `straight` | flees straight; exact                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | An upper bound on any hunter's catches                                    |
| `broken`   | at `JINK_RANGE` (`js/evo/chase-oracles.js`) breaks through `breakAngle`: the hunter's gap becomes `sqrt((g - advance)^2 + lateral^2)` with `advance = turnCircle speed x swingTicks x 2 / pi` and `lateral` the prey's sideways run over those ticks; repeated each time the gap falls under `JINK_RANGE` | A first-order bound, tested against the oracle evader; never a prediction |

`maxCatchableGap(hunter, prey)` is the largest awareness gap a perfect hunter still closes (the bisection of `chaseTimeline` on the start gap). `catchMargin = maxCatchableGap - gapAtAwareness`, where `gapAtAwareness = viewReach(prey, bearing) + size(hunter)` is how close the hunter can creep unseen. `catchable` is `catchMargin >= 0` on `broken`; the `straight` margin is printed beside it.

Also reported per pair (not slack cells): `chaseEnergy` (the cost of the timeline from `tickCost`'s terms against the energy the hunter holds above its `birthReserve` and the bite energy a kill returns, plus the kick `biteCost x sizeRatio x fullness x kickMultiplier` of each of the `bitesToKill` bites, `kickOf`), `chaseHeat` (temperature gained over the timeline against the headroom to `temperatureOptimalMax`), and `refillTicks` after a spent burst.

### Performer tiers: possible is not learnable

The `courser` arm is possible for a scripted hunter (E253: a catch at every start distance) and not learnable from random brains (E255: 1 of 8 populations). Every predator row is computed for three performers, and the gate reads all three.

| Tier       | Play                                                                                                                                      | Reads                                    |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| `perfect`  | creeps unseen, turns at once, sprints at effort 1 only once the prey is aware                                                             | whether the job is possible at all       |
| `reactive` | no creeping: heads for the prey and sprints at effort 1 from the tick it sees it, `reactionTicks` late at every step                      | what a simple reflex reaches             |
| `naive`    | heads for the prey and holds a constant effort `naiveEffort` from tick 0, prey in view or not (the brain a random population starts near) | whether a beginner ever touches the prey |

`tierMargins(hunter, prey)` returns the catch margin of each tier on both bounds. `learnSlope` is `ok` when the margins rise from `naive` to `reactive` to `perfect` and the `naive` tier reaches contact on the `straight` bound at the start gap of the `prey-ahead` drill (`preyDrill` in `js/evo/drill-staging.js`); else it names the tier that fails. An arm is fit for the rung screen only when its `predator:forager` row is `catchable` for `reactive` on `broken` and `learnSlope` is `ok`. `--search` looks for the smallest one-setting change that gives both.

### The search

`searchSettings(config, pair, options)` in `js/evo/role-budget-search.js` tries each candidate setting alone at the factors 0.5, 0.75, 1.25, 1.5 and 2 and reports, for a pair that is not `catchable`, the changes that make it so, smallest relative change first, with the resulting margin. The default candidates are the hunter's `sprintBoost`, `sprintDrainMultiplier`, `visionRange`, `rearVisionShare`, `turnMultiplier`, `maxEnergyMultiplier`, the prey's `sprintBoost`, `visionRange`, `turnMultiplier`, and the global `maxVelocity`, `maxThrust`, `maxTurn`, `damping`, `sprintDrain`, `sprintRefill`, `sprintFloor`, `killShare`. Each result is written as an overrides file in the format `audit:chase --config` reads (`evals/role-budget/<name>.json`), so a finding feeds the scripted chase and the ladder screen directly.

## Phase 2 steps - the defender (`js/evo/role-budget-defend.js`)

The job: when a hunter closes on a caller (a forager, reproducer or scout), the defender hears the shout, reaches the hunter before the kill completes, and can buy time with smoke.

| Step      | Formula                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| hear      | the defender is inside `hearWindow` of the caller: distance under `callRadius` (the call spreads over that radius around the caller) and loudness above `hearThreshold`; the alarm fires only when the caller's alarm output is above `alarmThreshold` and the caller is not a hunter; delay `reactionTicks` for the caller, 1 tick for `heard` to be rebuilt, `reactionTicks` for the defender |
| turn      | `swingTicks(angle to the hunter, w)` of the defender                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| run       | the defender's `speedStep` profile over the distance to the hunter, sprinting while its store lasts                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| intercept | the defender is within `interceptRange` (`size(d) + size(h)`) of the hunter before the kill completes (`bitesToKill` scaled by `alarmBiteShare` when the caller's alarm is seen) at tick `contactTick + bitesToKill - 1`, where `contactTick` is `chaseTimeline` of the hunter on the caller; the slack is the ticks to spare                                                                   |
| smoke     | see below                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

A defender that arrives shoves the hunter off: the budget's defender rows are computed with the repel on, at the first arm of [prey-guilds.md](prey-guilds.md) Phase 2 (`defenderRepelEnergy` 40, `defenderRepelImpulse` 4) with `defenderRepelCost` read at 0, 2 and 4 by T12. With `defenderRepelEnergy` and `defenderRepelImpulse` both 0 (the default today, `js/sim/defender-repel.js`) the defender has no power on arrival and the intercept cell is `impossible` with the binding `repel: off`; with `defenderRepelEnergy` or `defenderRepelImpulse` above 0 the defender repels a hunter in its jaws: `tryDefenderRepel` in `js/sim/defender-repel.js` fires when the defender holds `BITE` above 0 with the hunter in contact and inside `biteArc` (`inJaws`), drains `defenderRepelEnergy` from the hunter, adds `defenderRepelImpulse` along the defender-to-hunter vector and costs the defender `defenderRepelCost`; the cell then also needs the defender facing the hunter at contact, and prints the energy a repel takes against the bite energy it prevents. A hunter biting the defender pays `kickMultiplier` (2) of a bite's kick and takes `biteTakenMultiplier` (0.5) of a bite, printed beside the cell.

**Smoke.** `smokeCell(hunter, prey, geometry)` reads how smoke works in the sim: a releaser needs its `SMOKE` output above `smokeThreshold` (0.8), energy above `smokeCost`, a cooldown of 0 and a free cloud (`maxClouds`; a full pool pushes `SmokeRefused` and pays nothing), then pays `smokeCost` and and drops one cloud of radius `smokeRadius` centred `size + smokeRadius` behind it along its heading (`releaseSmoke`), for `smokeTicks` ticks, with `smokeCooldown` ticks before the same body releases again (`smoke` in `js/sim/calls.js`). A cloud is a ray fixture (`SMOKE` in `js/sim/ray-gather.js`): a ray that meets it returns a smoke hit, so a body behind it on that ray is not seen; the hunter's brain loses the prey for as long as the cloud lies on the rays towards it. Its recurrent state may keep its heading; the budget assumes the worst case for the prey, a hunter that keeps its speed and heading while blind.

| Quantity           | Formula                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `shadow`           | the prey is hidden while its bearing from the hunter lies within `asin(smokeRadius / distanceToCloud)` of the cloud's, and the cloud has ticks left                                     |
| `hiddenTicks`      | the ticks until `min(smokeTicks, sideways run needed to leave the shadow / the prey's lateral speed)`                                                                                   |
| `escapeNeed`       | the ticks the prey needs to open the gap past the hunter's `viewReach` while the hunter keeps its speed (`chaseTimeline` with the hunter blind)                                         |
| smoke slack (prey) | `hiddenTicks - escapeNeed - reactionTicks`; `n/a` when `smokeCooldown` has not elapsed at the scene's start; the energy cost (`smokeCost`) is printed against the energy the prey holds |

**Defender smoke.** `defenderSmokeGeometry(hunter, caller, defender)` answers where and when the defender must release so that the cloud lies on the hunter-to-caller line. `releaseSmoke` drops the cloud `size(d) + smokeRadius` behind the releaser along its heading, so a defender that stands on the hunter-to-caller segment must face the caller (its back to the hunter) for the cloud to land between the two; facing the hunter drops the cloud behind it, on the caller's side, and the hunter's rays meet the defender first.

| Quantity             | Formula                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `releasePoint`       | the defender's position `x` along the segment (hunter at 0, caller at `L`) with `x - (size(d) + smokeRadius) - smokeRadius` at least the hunter's travel in `reactionTicks`, so the cloud's near edge is still ahead of the hunter at the release, and the cloud's centre within `smokeRadius` of the line |
| `facingTicks`        | `swingTicks(angle to face the caller, w)` of the defender; the release waits for it                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `latestRelease`      | the last tick at which the cloud's near edge is still ahead of the hunter: `(x_cloud - smokeRadius - x_hunter) / hunter speed - reactionTicks`                                                                                                                                                             |
| `hiddenTicks`        | the ticks the hunter's rays to the caller meet the cloud: from the release until the hunter exits the cloud's far side, `(x_cloud + smokeRadius - x_hunter) / hunter speed`, at most `smokeTicks`, plus the caller's lateral run out of the shadow                                                         |
| defender smoke slack | `latestRelease - (hear + facingTicks + run to the releasePoint)`, and `hiddenTicks - escapeNeed - reactionTicks` for the caller; the smaller is the cell's margin; `n/a` with `smokeCooldown` not elapsed                                                                                                  |

## Phase 3 steps - the scout (`js/evo/role-budget-warn.js`)

The job: see a hunter before the hunter is within reach of the herd, and warn in time for a forager to turn and flee.

| Step | Formula                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| see  | `seeLead(scout, hunter)` against the hunter's approach: the ticks of lead `(viewReach(scout) - viewReach(forager)) / hunter cruise speed`                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| hear | the forager is inside `callRadius x scoutCallRadiusMultiplier` of the scout, loudness above `hearThreshold` (`hearWindow`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| warn | the delay chain: `reactionTicks` (scout) + 1 (`heard`) + `reactionTicks` (forager); the hunter's bite is cut by `alarmBiteShare` for a caller whose alarm saw a threat (`alarmSeen`), which lengthens `bitesToKill`; the forager's gap to the hunter when it starts to flee is `viewReach(scout) - hunter travel in the delay` along the hunter's approach |
| turn | `swingTicks(angle away from the hunter, w)` of the forager                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| run  | the warned forager is safe when its gap at the start of the flight is at least `maxCatchableGap(hunter, forager)` of Phase 1; the slack is `(gap - maxCatchableGap) / hunter closing speed` ticks                                                                                                                                                          |

## Phase 4 steps - the forager and the reproducer (`js/evo/role-budget-thrive.js`)

The job: eat, reach the energy to split or mate, and survive the hunters met on the way.

| Step    | Formula                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `eat`   | net energy per tick: `min(find, 1 / (digestTicks + handlingTicks)) x foodEnergy x plantFoodValue - tickCost at the foraging speed`, `find` in items per tick: `foodDensity x sweepWidth x speed` with `sweepWidth = 2 (size + foodRadius)` (eating is by contact) and `foodDensity` from `MODEL_DEFAULTS` (`patchFoodDensity(config)` unless overridden); zero above `satiationShare` of the store. The margin is the net energy per 100 ticks, in ticks of standing loss it covers |
| `breed` | ticks to the split gate `splitMinEnergy x splitMinEnergyMultiplier` (or the mate gate `mateMinEnergy`, cost `mateCost`, gift `childEnergyMin`) from the energy at birth (`entrantEnergy` for an entrant, the gift for a child), at the net rate of `eat`; slack is the life (`maxAgeTicks`, less `maturityTicks`) minus that, in ticks, and `birthReserve` and `maxBirthsPerWindow` are checked                                                                                     |
| `run`   | Phase 1's slack of the nearest hunter against this body, scaled by `encountersPerLife = hunterDensity x 2 viewReach(hunter) x hunter cruise speed x life`, with `hunterDensity = maxAgents x hunterShare / (worldWidth x worldHeight)`; printed, and the cell is `impossible` only when `encountersPerLife` is at least 1 and the pair is `catchable` with `catchMargin` above the forager's own flight slack                                                                       |

### A hunter's living (`predator:living`)

The row asks whether a hunter's kills pay for its chases and reach the split gate inside its life (selection rule 6: a kill that costs more than it returns is a failed gene).

| Step    | Formula                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `eat`   | net energy per chase: `catchShare x killIncome - chaseEnergy`, with `killIncome` what the killer takes at the kill, `min(killGorgeShare x the victim's energy above its kill line, killGorgeCap, the killer's store room)` (`js/sim/kill-body.js`); the carcass eaten later at `meatFoodValue` is printed beside it and `chaseEnergy` of Phase 1 for the `reactive` tier; `impossible` when it is 0 or less at `catchShare` 1 |
| `breed` | chases to the split gate `splitMinEnergy x splitMinEnergyMultiplier` from the energy at birth, at the net of `eat`, against the chases a life holds: `encountersPerLife` of this section with the prey's density; the slack is the spare chases, printed in ticks at the mean ticks per chase                                                                                                                                 |

The row also prints the resting cost between chases (`passiveLoss x passiveLossMultiplier`, the gorge-and-fast body) and the ticks a full store lasts with no kill.

## Phase 5 steps - the herd (`js/evo/role-budget-herd.js`)

A herd animal that closes on a body tramples it, walking or stampeding (`resolveContact` in `js/sim/trample.js`): the body loses `trampleDamage x (closing speed / maxVelocity)^2 x size / baseSize` and its velocity is reflected. The job, for each of the five specs: see the herd coming, and get out of its path in time if it chooses to.

| Step      | Formula                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `see`     | the herd is on a ray (`CH_MEGAFAUNA`) inside `viewReach(spec, bearing)` plus `herdRadius` (one animal's radius); the warning is `(viewReach - herdFront) / herdRunSpeed` ticks                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| `turn`    | `swingTicks(angle to the nearer flank, w)` of the body                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| `run`     | ticks to clear the herd's half-width sideways, `herdSize x 2.2 x herdRadius / (2 pi) + herdRadius + size(spec)` (the ring `js/sim/herds.js` spawns a herd on, plus one animal and the body) with `speedStep`, sprinting while the store lasts, against the ticks until the front arrives; the slack is the difference. A second line reads the straight run ahead of the herd: whether the body's cruise cap exceeds `herdRunSpeed` for the `herdStampedeTicks` a stampede lasts |
| `survive` | the tramples a body survives at the herd's run speed and at its walk speed, from the loss formula above against its energy at `storeShare`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

Rows: `forager:herd`, `predator:herd`, `reproducer:herd`, `scout:herd`, `defender:herd`. The worst case is printed beside the typical one: the herd startled at `herdStartleRadius` with the body in the middle of its path, and a body facing away (the rear view, `rearVisionShare`).

## Decisions

| Decision                                                              | Holds because                                                                                                                                                                               |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Per-term closed forms, a scalar tick recurrence only for the timeline | The speed law, turn circle, store, view and hearing are exact formulas; the timeline composes them without a world, so a run takes microseconds and can be searched                         |
| Two prey bounds, `straight` and `broken`                              | The straight run is exact and bounds every hunter from above; the break is a first-order bound whose gap to the oracle evader is measured, not claimed                                      |
| A margin is a delay found by bisection, not a second formula          | One timeline function answers every step, so a margin cannot disagree with the verdict                                                                                                      |
| The budget reads `roleParams`, not the config keys directly           | One resolver is where [body-genes.md](body-genes.md) adds spec fields and gene values; a cell for a best or worst body is the same call with other numbers                                  |
| The scene checks sit in the tool, behind `--check`                    | A formula that was never played against the sim drifts; the scene and the formula print side by side                                                                                        |
| The calculator changes no sim rule and adds no config key             | Its inputs are the config and `MODEL_DEFAULTS`; no `rulesHash` change                                                                                                                       |
| Three performer tiers, not one                                        | A perfect performer showed the `courser` arm possible (E253) and a learning one could not use it (E255); the beginner's and the reflex's margins say whether selection has a slope to climb |
| The predator has a living row                                         | A catch that costs more than it returns, or too few chases in a life to reach the split gate, fails the hunter whatever its catch margin (E190)                                             |
| The herd is a row for every spec                                      | A stampede is the one pressure every body meets; whether a body can clear it in time is arithmetic on the herd's speed and width                                                            |
| The defender's rows are computed with the shove on                    | A defender with no power on arrival has no job to budget (user, 2026-11-21); the shove is the repel of `js/sim/defender-repel.js`, built and switched off                                   |
