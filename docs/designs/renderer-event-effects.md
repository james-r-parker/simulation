Every sim feature gets a drawn signature a viewer learns once and then reads without a legend. Each signature has three parts:

- **A shape burst.** Instanced, GPU-animated and pooled.
- **A body tween.** A keyframed squash, pulse, wobble or shiver on the agent itself.
- **A colour role.** Never the only cue.

Ambient layers carry the world's slow state: the season's weather, cold, drought and the pheromone field. The renderer reads what the snapshot and the effect ring already carry. Phase 2 adds one per-agent state word and Phase 3 adds five effect kinds and one header word, all read-only. `worldHash` and `test/sim/golden.json` do not change. The scenes served are the ones in [principles.md](../principles.md): A real chase, Call or don't, Refuge, Scavenge, Condition changes the walk, Season changes the map, Cross or stay, Boom and bust, Other blobs are the climate. Each is listed in the inventory below.

## Inventory

Each row is one sim feature or event. It gives the visual signature, its data source and the phase that draws it. Some sources are new; they are listed under Data plumbing.

- **Duration** is in sim ticks, with a wall-time floor (see Effect system).

- **Sound** is a `cueFor` cue in [`cues.js`](../../js/app/audio/cues.js), all behind the existing limiter.
- **"Built"** marks a signature that exists today and stays.

- **Contrast.** Event colours sit above the bloom threshold (0.85) for their first 30% of life, then fall below it, so a burst flares and settles. The ambient weather layer keeps to `WEATHER_MAX_LUMINANCE` (new, in `colors.js`) 0.02: under the dimmest agent body (about 0.04) together with the aurora (0.010).
- **Separation.** The five new roles join the Interface colour test in `test/app/renderer/colors.test.js`. They keep its CIEDE2000 distances from every family slot and every semantic colour, under each colour-vision simulation and in each of the three `FAMILY_PALETTES`.
- **Lit rooms.** The `high-contrast` palette multiplies event luminance by 1.5 and doubles the ring strokes (`RING`, `CHEVRON`, `ARC`): `HIGH_CONTRAST_GAIN` and `uContrast` in the fx pool.
- **Burn-in.** No effect is static for more than 3 s at full brightness on the OLED. Dev overlay labels fade after 2 s.

### Effect system

- **One pool.** `createFxPool` in `renderer/fx-pool.js` owns one `InstancedMesh` of fixed capacity: 2048 at level 0, 1024 at level 1, 256 at level 2. `spawnFx(pool, rec)` copies a scratch record into the next ring slot and widens the attribute update range. The record holds x, y, angle, size, rgb, kind, start tick, life ticks and seed. It allocates nothing; a full pool overwrites its oldest slot.
- **GPU animation.** `renderer/fx-shader.js` computes age01 from its own `uFxTick` uniform, the fractional playhead tick plus `pool.clockOffset` (the demo's own clock, else 0), so pause freezes every effect, and evaluates the curve's keyframes in the vertex shader. `renderer/fx-curves.js` holds the tables (`FX_CURVES`, `FX_SHAPES`, `FX_MOTIONS`, `FX_EASE`, `CURVE`), their JS reference (`curveAt`) and the uniform packing (`curveUniforms`). Each `FX_CURVES` row has 4 scale keys, 4 alpha keys, a motion type (`out`, `in`, `rise`, `fall`, `orbit`, `fixed`) and an `FX_EASE` id (`linear`, `quadIn`, `quadOut`, `sineInOut`, `expoIn`, `backOut`, `elasticOut`). The fragment shader draws the `FX_SHAPES` SDF: `RING`, `STREAK`, `CRESCENT`, `SHARD`, `MOTE`, `DROP`, `PUFF`, `CHEVRON`, `ARC`, `ORBIT`, `SPORE`, `SPARKLE`, `CRUMB`, `SPROUT`, `FISSION`, `GHOST`. The CPU never touches a live instance.
- **Wall-time floor.** Life in ticks is raised at spawn to at least `FX_MIN_MS` (250 ms) times the actual tick rate.
- **Budget.** `renderer/fx-budget.js` (`createFxBudget`, `admitFx`) applies four limits. Each rejection is counted by family in `__perf().fx`:
    - a per-frame spawn cap of 96, 48 or 12 instances at levels 0, 1 and 2;
    - a token bucket per family per second (combat 80, feeding 60, courtship 30, calls 30, sickness 30, weather 20, herds 20, flora 20);
    - a priority order when the frame cap binds: kill, birth, first kill, bite, call, sickness, mate, eat, weather;
    - off-view culling with a 200-unit margin. Audio still gets the off-view cue at half gain.
- **Adaptive scale.** `adaptFxBudget` multiplies the frame cap and every bucket by `budget.scale`, which follows `TARGET_LOAD` (0.75) over the smoothed load (the larger of the last frame's draw CPU time and GPU time, `latest` in `gpu-timer.js`, over its interval) at 1x per second, between `MIN_SCALE` 0.5 and `MAX_SCALE` 4. `beginEffects` feeds it from the frame loop. A frame with no usable interval (none, over 250 ms, or a hidden tab) is no data and keeps the scale.
- **Zoom bands.** `zoomBand(pxPerUnit, typicalRadius)` gives `far` when an agent draws under 6 device px, `mid` under 20 and `close` above. At `far` each part of a recipe spawns only its lead instance, at the part's least size (3 device px unless the part sets more). At `mid` recipes run at half count. At `close` they run in full.
- **Recipes.** `renderer/fx-recipes.js` (`FX_RECIPES`, `DEATH_RECIPES`, `createFxPlayer`, `playRecipe`) maps each inventory row to its parts. It is a table, so a row is added without touching the dispatcher. A part whose `reach` or `life` is -1 takes the effect's own (a call's radius and length, a meal's distance).
- **Dispatch.** `effects-player.js` plays the worker's records (`playEffects`) and the flag edges (`playEdges`), and `beginEffects` feeds the budget each frame. The recipes are grouped by module: `effects-combat.js` (bites, deaths), `effects-life.js` (mating, pregnancy, births), `effects-signals.js` (calls, smoke, stampede, pollination, strains), `effects-edges.js` (meals, sickness) and `effects-weather.js` (cold breath). Edge events reach the sink (audio, overlay) as `CLIENT_KIND` records above 1000 (`renderer/client-kinds.js`).

### Body tweens

- **One-shot tweens.** `renderer/body-tweens.js` keeps per-slot arrays sized to `maxAgents`: start tick (`Uint32Array`) and kind (`Uint8Array`). `startTween(tweens, slot, kind, tick)` overwrites a running tween only when the new one ranks higher. `packAgents` writes `iTween` (kind, age01) per instance, and `agents-shader.js` applies the curve: `LUNGE`, `FLINCH`, `GULP`, `CALL_THROB`, `HOWL`, `COURT`, `BIRTH_POP`, `SHAKE_OFF`.
- **Continuous states.** Computed in the shader from attributes the agent already has, with no CPU state: `SICK_SWAY`, `SHIVER` and `PANT` in Phase 1 (`body-tweens-glsl.js`); in Phase 2 (`body-states-glsl.js`) the state word's `iBody` attribute drives `stateP` (resting breath at 0.4 Hz, sated 8% rounder), `eyeCover` (shut to a line at rest, 40% larger when juvenile, 30% larger when fleeing), `concealment` (an ordered dither of the fill in cover, rim kept), `gestationArc`, `strainTint` and the dose-scaled toxin tint; `iTween.w` marks a fleeing body for the 15% streamline; `gaitP` and `gaitRim` give each silhouette its `GAITS` signature.
- **Slot reuse.** A slot whose genome id changes clears its tween.

### Flag edges

`renderer/flag-edges.js` (`createFlagEdges`, `diffFlags`) keeps the previous flags and genome per slot. It reports rising and falling edges of `SICK`, `INFECTED`, `PREGNANT`, `ALARMING` and `HUNT_CALLING`, the ate timer reaching 1, and (Phase 2) the state bits. A fresh body (new genome in the slot) reports no edges. Edges feed `playRecipe` like effect records.

### Ambient layers

- **Weather.** `renderer/weather-layer.js` (`createWeatherLayer`) is one instanced quad mesh: 2400 motes at level 0, 1200 at level 1, none at level 2. Positions come from a hash of the instance id, wrapped into the view and drifted by the season's mode in the shader. No CPU update runs per frame. Uniforms carry the two blended modes and their mix, the cold margin width and the world height.
- **Pheromone flow.** Built into the haze shader at tier 0 (Phase 3).
- **Drought haze.** Part of the ground layer (Phase 3).

### Focus

The director scores new moment kinds and each caption names the event (Phase 4):

| Moment        | Trigger                                             |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| outbreak      | 3 or more infection onsets within 1000 units in 5 s |
| stampede      | a `STAMPEDE` effect                                 |

`director/moments-world.js` (`findWorldMoments`, called by `findMoments`) scores them; `director/cluster.js` holds the shared `largestCluster`. ### Developer overlay

`?fxlog=1` turns on `renderer/fx-log-overlay.js`:

- 48 pooled DOM labels, each naming an effect or edge at its world position as it fires (`BITE`, `EATEN`, `SPLIT`, `EAT`, `SICK+`, `INF-`, ...; `renderer/fx-names.js`), fading over 2 s;
- a corner table of counts per name over the last 60 s, with the budget's drops by family.

`?fxdemo=<family|all>` (`renderer/fx-demo.js`) pauses the world, zooms so the grid fits the canvas (at most zoom 2) and plays every recipe of the family in its own cell, again every 90 ticks of the pool's own clock; `&fxhold=<n>` freezes that clock n ticks after each replay, and `&fxhold=peak` spawns each cell so it sits at its recipe's brightest keyframe (`peakTicks`) when the clock freezes. The shader runs on the demo's own clock (the playhead tick at the start plus the demo's ticks), whatever the paused playhead does; `__fxdemo.frozen` says when the clock is held. `?fxstress=1` plays 60 recipes a second at random spots in view through the budget. `js/app/fx-dev.js` reads these parameters (`readFxParams`) and wires the tools into the sink and the after-draw hook; `window.__fxdemo` lists the cells in CSS px. `__perf()` reports `fx` (spawned, dropped and culled by family, and the pool's `occupancy`), `drawCalls`, and `ignoredFrames` (frames not counted: a hidden tab, no interval, or an interval over 250 ms).
