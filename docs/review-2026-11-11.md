**Where the project stands, in five lines.**

1. The measurement system is now honest, and that is the real result of the last two weeks. What it honestly says is
   that the default world is a boom to the cap followed by a crash, in most seeds.
2. Three recorded open problems are one problem. Hunters dying out, the mating Allee edge and flat skill are all the
   crash (H1, H2).
3. About half the scenes happen in some form. The other half are absent, and four of them have no detector that can
   see them (H4).
4. The part of the app built to make the world watchable with no charts open, the director, is written and tested and
   never runs. A visitor also always starts in an unevolved world (H3).
5. About 10 to 15 of the last 100 merged PRs changed what a viewer sees. The rest built instruments, plans and process
   (H7).

**The biggest thing between this project and a world worth watching** is that nothing but the hard cap and an
absorbing edge regulates the population. Food is never short, so no world settles. Every scene that needs a standing
population of prey with hunters living off it (chase, abort, pack, refuge, scavenge, alarm) is being tuned on top of a
world that falls under 20 agents once a run.

## High

- [ ] **H1. The default world booms to the cap and crashes; food never brakes it**
      Doc: world-balance.md Phase 6; natural-selection.md T64 to T66; principles.md K16 and "Smoothing away crashes"
      Evidence: on 128 floor-on seeds, 89 reach 450 agents (`maxAgents` 500 less `splitCapMargin` 50) and spend 14% of
      checkpoints there; 71 fall under 40 agents; only 30 stay at 100 or more after tick 20000; the floor guard fires on 61
      seeds. Floor off, 56 of 128 end at 0. The sim-notes T66 entry gives the cause: starvation per prey is flat at 0.19 to
      0.21 per 2000 ticks from 50 to 500 prey, edible slots sit at about 3,590 of `maxFood` 3,600, and 32% of food rots. `spawnFood` in `js/sim/food.js` is asked for about 10 times what the world eats and is clipped at `maxFood`.
      Births run just above deaths at every density until the cap, and nothing pulls them back until mating fails under about
      100 prey. `mate` in `js/sim/reproduction.js` also multiplies the mating chance by the season's `reproductionBonus`
      (1.5, 1.2, 0.7, 0.3), so the calendar starts the boom and the fall in every seed at the same ticks.
      Why it matters: a viewer sees one swell and one die-off per hour, map-wide, not patches stripped and recolonised.
      Every screen since E127 (mate range, cap margin, spring bonus, roaming) moved the edge or the boom size. None gave the
      world a brake below the cap.
      Change: make food the carrying capacity, take the calendar out of the mating dice, and let the clonal backstop work
      when mates are truly absent. That is [designs/carrying-capacity.md](designs/carrying-capacity.md).

- [ ] **H2. Hunters die out because the world crashes, not because they cannot hunt**
      Doc: training.md, hunter-learning.md, hunter-practice.md, brain-reflex-tests.md (ranks 2, 4, 5, 19)
      Evidence: all 33 seeds with no hunter at the last tick are among the 71 whose population fell under 40. No seed of the
      57 that stayed at 40 or more lost its hunters. Skill is the same in both groups: `eval.chase_catch_share` 0.056
      against 0.053, `eval.hunter_kill_conversion_by_age.1k-2k` 0.068 against 0.068. Live hunters already bite prey
      (`eval.bite_on_prey_view` 0.96). The one trained-champion edge did not replicate (training T22), and the trained
      archives lived in `/tmp` and are gone.
      Why it matters: ranks 2, 4 and 5 of the queue teach a skill that is not the binding constraint. This is a correlation
      on one report, so the big bang tests it directly: if the world holds and hunters still vanish, the skill plans come back.
      Change: freeze training.md Phases 4 to 6, hunter-practice.md Phases 2 and 3, hunter-learning.md Phase 3 and
      brain-reflex-tests.md Phases 3 and 4 until the held world is measured.

- [ ] **H3. The director is built and never runs, and every visit starts unevolved**
      Doc: worker-and-renderer.md Phase 4 (T30, T47 to T56) and Phase 5 (T36); rank 10
      Evidence: nothing outside `js/app/director/` imports `director.js`, `shots.js`, `scoring.js`, `captions.js`,
      `moments.js` or `cluster.js`; only `moment-log.js` is used. `prefs.director` defaults to false in `js/app/prefs.js`
      and has no consumer. There is no saved world, no `public/champions`, and no warm start, so a visitor to the live site
      watches generation 0 on a random seed. The evals judge tick 200000, which is 55 minutes at x1.
      Why it matters: this is the only work in the queue whose whole purpose is the first sentence of the principles, and it
      sits behind a brain plan whose own evidence says not to run it.
      Change: a new small plan, `director-live.md`: wire the director and captions, pace and era cards, then a warm start
      from a saved evolved world. Rank it directly behind the big bang, because a saved world is void each time `rulesHash`
      changes. The wiring itself does not wait.

- [ ] **H4. Six scenes have no detector that can see them**
      Doc: principles.md "How each scene is detected"
      Evidence: the table above. Call, Cross or stay, Parent spends, Same spec and Boom and bust rest only on unverified
      metrics. `eval.boom_bust_count` (`js/evo/scene-guards.js`) counts a hunter boom from a trough and reads 0 on 101
      seeds, while the population traces of the same report show a fall from 300 or more to under 60 on 50 seeds. Abort
      counts every dropped chase.
      Why it matters: a plan that claims one of these scenes cannot be judged, and alarm-calls.md, seasonal-movement.md and
      habitat-cover.md each list unverified Targets.
      Change: the big bang adds the crash detectors it needs. Each scene plan verifies its headline detector before its
      first behaviour task; a scene with no verified detector is judged by the clip and says so.

- [ ] **H5. The keep rule cannot say no, and the adoption bar is a coin**
      Doc: principles.md last bullet; AGENTS.md "Keep what does no harm"; realistic-keepers.md
      Evidence: `keepRule` has two seed guards and one metric guard. `GUARD_METRICS` is only `eval.hunter_catches_per_1k`,
      which needs 64 seeds, so at the rule's 32 seeds it is always `unchecked`. On random halves of the 128 defaults the rule
      reads `worse` 2% of the time and needs 10 to 17 more seeds harmed than recovered. The adoption bar (alive on 0.75 of
      seeds, no more seeds under 80 than the reference) is a raw count: an inert change passes it 42% of the time at 16
      seeds and 37% at 32, and the defaults themselves sit at 0.742 alive. E138, the whole kept bundle, read
      `eval.hunter_kill_conversion_by_age.1k-2k` worse by 25% with the interval excluding 0 and was adopted.
      Why it matters: "kept" then means "on by default, switch deleted", so a kept mechanism can never be tested again. Six
      mechanisms that each cost 3 seeds in 32 all pass alone. The rule also has no cost side: three brain inputs
      (`REFUGE_BEARING_SIN`, `REFUGE_BEARING_COS`, `REFUGE_NEAR`), six config keys and about 360 lines were kept with no
      measured effect, against the refusal "More sensors as a substitute for a scene".
      Change: principle edits P6 and P7 below. No new keeper is adopted until the held world is measured.

- [ ] **H6. Decisions rest on reports that were never committed**
      Doc: evals/experiments.json; metric-trust.md T35
      Evidence: `evals/results/` is ignored. Of 31 records from E120 to E150, 11 cannot be re-read and 3 are at risk. The
      flip reflex and hunter territory adoption (E122, E123) is among the 11.
      Why it matters: the registry can now void an old verdict, and without the report nothing can replace it.
      Change: every report a default, a deletion or a keep rests on is committed under `evals/decisions/<id>/`, and
      `scripts/check-experiments.mjs` fails a record whose verdict names a report that is not there. Screens already do this.

- [ ] **H7. Most of the effort does not reach the screen**
      Doc: the Build queue
      Evidence: of the last 100 merged PRs, 29 touch only docs or evals, 20 only detectors in `js/evo`, 21 touch `js/sim`
      and 9 touch `js/app`. Test code is 89,000 lines against 67,000 lines of source. `docs/designs` is 4.7 MB.
      Why it matters: the instruments were needed, and metric trust was the right call. It is now done. The next ten PRs
      should each change what the default world looks like or what the app shows.
      Change: the proposed queue below, and the process cuts in M5 to M8.
