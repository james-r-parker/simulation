### 2026-11-21 - The herd's front is the sim's to the tick, and only a body that sees 270 px of it can clear it (role-budget Phase 5, E262)

`npm run role:budget` reads each spec against a stampeding herd from the config alone and agrees with the scripted stampede of the real sim: the front animal's run (its velocity relaxing to `herdRunSpeed` plus the 0.25 flee bias by 0.05 a tick, capped at `herdRunSpeed`, so 65 ticks to the run speed) reaches a body that stands `viewReach - size` px ahead at the tick the sim says (59, 51, 74 and 49 for a forager, a reproducer, a scout and a defender facing away), and the first trample is within 2% (299 against 304: the front arrives at 6.42 px a tick, not 6.5). After the fix of the held centroid (T21a) a startled herd alone travels 407 px by tick 80 and 2227 px by tick 360 with a peak mean speed of 6.50 and a half-width of 224 to 234 (`measureStampede`). A body needs 52 ticks (2 to react, 10.5 to turn, 39 to clear the half-width of 333 px at a sprint) and the front covers 271 px in 59, so the forager and the scout can clear it and the reproducer, the defender and the predator cannot; every plant eater survives about 3 tramples at the herd's speed. Two limits of the formula: a body that can hunt within 200 px of an animal starts its own stampede away from it (`senseHunters`), so the herd turns before it arrives and the predator row is the arithmetic of a body the herd does not read as a hunter; and a ring wider than `4 x herdRadius` (a herd of 11 or more) pulls the front animal back toward the centroid at full speed, so the formula's front is too fast (flagged as `pulled`). The first trample is read at the herd's speed on arrival, not the constant run speed; a herd carries a body along after the first hit and the damage of the later contacts is small (`resolveContact` reflects the velocity).
