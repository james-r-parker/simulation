### 2026-11-21 - The perfect hunter is not the best hunter against a breaking prey (role-budget Phase 1, E257)

`npm run role:budget` reads the hunter against each prey from the config alone and agrees with the scripted chase of `audit:chase` cell by cell (kills within 2 ticks on the straight bound, the same fair band on the broken one, no drift on the defaults or the five `evals/fair-chase/` arms). What it showed: the `perfect` tier of the plan, effort 1 from the tick the prey is aware, is the oracle's own play and overshoots the oracle evader's break (the hunter arrives at 14 px a tick and turns on a 75 px circle), so on the courser and even arms a `naive` hunter at effort 0.5 closes the broken bound where `perfect` does not, and `learnSlope` names the `reactive` tier for the forager on both. Whether a kill is possible is also not monotone in the start gap, since the prey sprints only inside 360 px or after the hunter has spent its burst; `maxCatchableGap` therefore walks outward from the awareness gap instead of bisecting. A start exactly at the hunter's view disagrees between formula and sim: the oracle sprints only while the gap is at most `visionRange` and float32 positions put it a hair over.
