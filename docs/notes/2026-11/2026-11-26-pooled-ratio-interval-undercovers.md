### 2026-11-26 - The BCa interval under-covers pooled ratios and skewed per-seed metrics at 16 to 64 seeds

`pooledInterval` was BCa at every size, missing the studentised rule `bootstrapInterval` got in metric-noise D4. On the 128-seed pooled-metric power report, `eval:power --include-watch --sizes 16,32,64` read the A/A false-positive rate of `eval.bite_idle_pooled` at 12.7%, 8.7% and 7.9% (nominal 5%), against 4.6%, 7.2% and 4.3% for the per-seed `eval.bite_idle`. A studentised pooled interval (delta-method standard error per resample, `js/evo/pooled-t.js`) reads 6.5%, 5.4% and 6.4% at 16, 32 and 64 seeds once it is used at every size (BCa left 8.7% and 7.9% at 32 and 64). The price is power: the detectable change moves from none, 15% and 10% to none, 20% and 15% at 16, 32 and 64 seeds, since an interval that covers is wider than one that does not. The per-seed `eval.bite_idle` moves from 7.2% and 4.3% to 5.9% and 3.7% at 32 and 64 with its detectable change unchanged (15%, 10%). Across E276, the per-seed BCa interval at 32 and 64 seeds was also off (95 of 326 metrics over 7.5% at 32): seeded nulls of a lognormal (sigma 1) and a zero-inflated count read 10.3% and 13.5% at 32 seeds under BCa. The studentised interval is no worse in any shape and size tried, so it replaced both (D7, D8) and `STUDENTISED_BELOW` is gone. A heavy right skew still reads 7 to 9% at 16 to 32 seeds under it, because no mean-based interval fixes that, so the calibration holds per-shape ceilings (`test/evo/bootstrap-t.slow.test.js`). A symmetric (absolute t) studentised interval was tried and was worse on every skewed shape.
