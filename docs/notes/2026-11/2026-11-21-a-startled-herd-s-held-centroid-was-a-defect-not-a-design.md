Intent: `2c09ec7d` (`feat(mega): stampede from nearby hunters`) added the guard so that the flee vector set by `setFleeDirection` is not overwritten by the herd's mean velocity; its message and `docs/designs/megafauna.md` (Stampede) say the flee vector and alarm timer are held and the heading is the flee vector, and nothing says the centroid is held.
