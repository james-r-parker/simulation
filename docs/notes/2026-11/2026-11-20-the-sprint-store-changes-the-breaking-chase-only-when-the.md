### 2026-11-20 - The sprint store changes the breaking chase only when the hunter outlasts its prey (E253, E-FC-1)

On the chase envelope (`npm run audit:chase`, scripted oracles, 8 trials per cell) a store that empties as fast for the predator as for its prey (`even`, drain 0.01; `long`, drain 0.004) leaves the breaking chase identical to the store-off control trial for trial: 0.17 caught at 0.2 of view and none beyond, because the catches at 0.2 come in the first burst and the jink decides the rest. Halving the predator's drain (`courser`) lets it sprint on after the prey's store is under `sprintFloor`, and the predator-on-forager band covers all five start distances (0.50, 0.42, 0.67, 0.63, 0.25). With the store on the pursuer sprints only with the prey in view, so at 1.0 of view (start distance jittered by 5%) about half the trials start just outside it and the straight-runner share falls from 0.69 to 0.25.
