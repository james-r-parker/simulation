### 2026-11-21 - A defender that holds BITE walks at the grazing gait, and nothing that sprints can stop where it walks to (role-budget Phase 2, E258)

Playing the defender's formula beside the real sim found two things the plan did not say. `tryDefenderRepel` needs the defender to hold `BITE` above 0, and a non-hunter holding `BITE` moves at `grazeThrust` (0.6) of its thrust (`grazeFactor` in `js/sim/physics.js`), so a defender that is meant to shove arrives slower than one that only runs: with the gait in the formula the first touch agrees with the sim to the tick, without it the touch came 7 ticks early. And a body at its sprint cap coasts about 228 px when it stops pushing (`cap x d / (1 - d)` at the defaults), so the smoke geometry's defender that "stands on the line" at the release point cannot be reached by walking there; the cell flatters the defender and the cross-check plays the release from a defender already standing. At the defaults the defender row is red either way: a hunter seen at 345 px kills a standing forager 27 ticks later, 5 ticks after the defender first moves, so the shove never fires at any cost (E258).
