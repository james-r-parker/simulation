### 2026-11-21 - A meal is not search plus handle plus digest, the sim's plants are not one item, and a sitter is tight not red (role-budget Phase 4, E261)

Playing the forager's formula beside the real sim found three things the plan did not say. One `ateTimer` holds the handling window (thrust x `handlingThrust`, on top of the grazing 0.6 when `BITE` is held: 1.2 px a tick for a forager) and the digest window (no eating), so they overlap and a meal is `digestTicks` apart at the soonest; a grid scene reads meals every 10 ticks while items are under the body and every 18 to 20 once it has walked out of them, so the formula is the body that lives on the items its path newly covers (4.9 a tick against the scene's 5.7). The sim's plants are a mixture (`config.flora`: grass 60 energy and 16 px, berry 150 and 22, nut 180 and 26, in season: 9 of 16 patches carry fruit at tick 0), not one item of `foodEnergy x plantFoodValue`, so the plan's patch density is the grass-only world and the first-meal scene runs in it (`uniformFlora` in `role-scenes-first-meal.js`); a real newborn's find and reach shares are lower. And the sitter, which the plan predicted red, is `tight`: a still body cools 0.0075 x 1.15 a tick in the first season (not 0.0075), leaves the optimal band after about 1160 ticks and lives 1.4 to 1.9 times as long as a searcher at the gait that goes furthest per energy; the formula reproduces the sim's sit to the tick (2744 and 7741 ticks from 200 and 800 energy). A straight walk's find share is an upper bound (rocks and walls only lower it: a founder's 0.89 reads 0.52). `createConfig` accepts a full config as its base, so a scene can derive an emptied world from it with `{...config, key: value}`. See `evals/decisions/E-RB-4/results.md`.
