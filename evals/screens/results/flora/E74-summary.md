# E74 Flora 600k paired robustness report

E74 ran 16 matched seeds for 600,000 ticks under the live policy against the pre-promotion reference (`toxinDose: 0.45`) and C1 (`toxinDose: 1.2`). The screen command completed successfully. Reports, the standard screen verdict, and the paired comparison are in this directory. The paired comparison uses the default 5% minimum effect.

## Decision

Adopt `toxinDose: 1.2` as the measured Flora default in this PR and retain `floraPatchMax: 32`. The toxin signal persists: toxic-death share is 0.01583 (within the registered 0.002–0.10 band on 16/16 seeds), and toxic avoidance is 0.9712 versus 0.9150 on defaults. Paired toxic-avoidance gain is +0.0562, 95% CI +0.0350 to +0.0863. There is no clear local-economy regression of at least 5% under the registered paired minimum-effect rule: food energy is −3.20% (no-difference), fed median lifespan is −4.54% (no-difference at the 5% minimum effect), deliberate food share is +1.63%, patch-leave-depleted share +0.26 percentage points, and carcass-meal share +0.56%. Winter basin density is +5.46% but its paired interval is inconclusive. E72/E74 pollination and hue thresholds remain unmet and are carried to balancing; do not claim global survival or family criteria passed.

The standard screen verdict is FAIL because the shared population/species criteria do not meet their preregistered thresholds. This is reported honestly as a balancing diagnostic and does not block the Flora candidate decision, per user direction.

## Balancing diagnostics

Predators remained at end on 2/16 defaults and 3/16 dose-1.2 seeds; prey types remained present in 16/16 for both. `eval.basin_family_effective >= 3` occurred in 1/16 default seeds and 0/16 dose-1.2 seeds. These outcomes are not passed global gates.

For context only, versus E69’s pre-Flora 200k reference, the E74 default at 600k has food energy 377.07 vs 420.56/1k (−10.3%), fed median lifespan 4443.6 vs 7007 (−36.6%), deliberate food share 0.5130 vs 0.5282 (−2.9%), patch-leave-depleted share 0.3939 vs 0.3242 (+21.5%), carcass-meal share 0.9230 vs 0.8990 (+2.7%), and winter density 0.4914 vs 0.9458 (−48.0%). E69 predates Flora and used 200k ticks; these differences conflate the baseline world and horizon, are balancing context only, and are not causal Flora comparisons or gates.
