# E73 Flora integration comparison

E73 ran the live policy for 16 matched seeds at 200,000 ticks per arm. Its `defaults` reference pins the pre-promotion `toxinDose: 0.45`; the registered screen outputs and paired `eval:compare` reports are in `flora-e73/`, and both candidate comparisons use the 5% default minimum effect. `E69` is a pre-Flora historical baseline, so its differences are balancing diagnostics rather than causal Flora comparisons or pass/fail gates.

All three reports have 16 seeds, 200,000 ticks and 177 metrics; the table, verdicts and paired comparisons validate. The screen command stayed silent with a live session after all artifacts were written; it was interrupted gracefully with exit 130. The artifacts are complete, but the screen command did not return cleanly and is not recorded as a command pass.

## Balancing diagnostics

At the final tick, predators remained in 0/16 default seeds, 2/16 C1 seeds, and 3/16 C2 seeds. `eval.basin_family_effective >= 3` occurred in 0/16 seeds in every arm. Prey types remained present in all seeds, but mean end-of-run prey types were 2.50, 2.31, and 1.94 for defaults, C1, and C2 respectively. These measures are reported for the balancing plan and do not select a Flora lever.

Compared with E69’s pre-Flora reference, current defaults have lower food energy (375.52 vs 420.56/1k), fed median lifespan (4510.5 vs 7007), deliberate food share (0.4959 vs 0.5282), and winter basin density (0.4446 vs 0.9458), and a higher patch-leave-depleted share (0.3865 vs 0.3242). Carcass meal share is similar (0.9027 vs 0.8990). E69 has no Flora-specific detectors and predates the Flora world, so these are carried to balancing without treating them as Flora regressions or gates.
