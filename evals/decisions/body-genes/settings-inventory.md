## 3. Dead or inert keys

- Inert at the default (read, but switched off by their own value; not dead): `sprintDrain`, `sprintRefill`, `sprintRestThrust`, `sprintFloor`, `sprintDrainMultiplier` (behind `sprintStoreEnabled: false`); `impactDamage` 0 and `impactDeadband`; `defenderRepelEnergy`, `defenderRepelCost`, `defenderRepelImpulse` (0); `scoutCallRadiusMultiplier`, `scoutAlarmCostMultiplier` (1); `juvenilePlantShare` (0); `splitMaxPerLife` (0); `unitKnockoutRate` (0); `meritBirthBoost` (0, so `meritHalf` is unread in effect); `alarmBiteShare` (1); `specFlipNoHunt` (false).

## Borderline calls

- `impactDamage`, `impactDeadband`, `obstacleDamage`, `wallDamage`: classed C (hazards of the medium); a "fragility" lane would move them to B.
- `initialEnergy`, `entrantEnergy`: classed C (how the world seeds bodies, not a body property).
- `toxinDose`, `toxinCost`: classed C (flora); the body side (`sickDose`, `sickDrain`, `doseDecay`, `lethalDose`, `sickThrust`) is B.
- `diseaseDrain`, `diseaseTicks`: classed B (host tolerance); the other five disease keys are C.
- merit keys and `mateRank`: classed C (selection rules); the split proof thresholds are B because each is a per-agent gate.
- `herdWary`, `instinctWeight`, `kinPullWeight`, `specMutationRate`, `specFlipNoHunt`: classed D (genome init and mutation).
- `sprintStoreEnabled`, `capBirthsEnabled`, `checkInvariants`: switches, classed C.
